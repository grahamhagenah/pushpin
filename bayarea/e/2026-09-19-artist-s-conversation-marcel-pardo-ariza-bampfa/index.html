Artist’s Conversation: Marcel Pardo Ariza

BAMPFA · Sat, Sep 19 · 2pm

Tickets required. Gallery admission included with ticket purchase.

Marcel Pardo Ariza joins BAMPFA Chief Curator Margot Norton for a conversation about their new Art Wall project and Art Handlxrs, the award-winning organization Ariza cofounded to support marginalized communities within the art handling profession. ASL interpretation will be provided for this event.
