Curators’ Conversation: Natalia Brizuela and Julia Bryan-Wilson on Lotty Rosenfield: Disobedient Spaces

BAMPFA · Sat, Sep 19 · 1pm

Tickets required. Gallery admission included with ticket purchase.

Cocurators Natalia Brizuela and Julia Bryan-Wilson discuss Lotty Rosenfeld: Disobedient Spaces, examining Rosenfeld’s approach to art under authoritarianism, feminist strategies for disobedience, materiality and ephemerality, and performance and video. ASL interpretation will be provided for this event.
