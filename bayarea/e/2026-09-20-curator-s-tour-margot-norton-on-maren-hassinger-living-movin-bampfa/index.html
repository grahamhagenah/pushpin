Curator’s Tour: Margot Norton on Maren Hassinger: Living Moving Growing

Join Chief Curator Margot Norton, co-curator with Senior Curator Anthony Graham, for a walkthrough of the exhibition, featuring Hassinger's material experiments in sculpture, her long-standing engagement with movement and performance, and her work with the moving image.
