Pink Trash at Crescent Lawn, UC Berkeley

BAMPFA · Sun, Sep 20 · 2pm

Co-presented by the UC Berkeley Environmental Arts and Humanities Initiative

Maren Hassinger restages her iconic 1982 performance Pink Trash on UC Berkeley’s Crescent Lawn, joined for the first time by six UC Berkeley students in a work exploring the encounters between humans and nature in public space.
