Georgian Cinema Now Symposium—Activism in Authoritarian Times: The Republic of Georgia as a Case Study

BAMPFA · Sat, Sep 26 · 11:30am

Free Admission Copresented by the Berkeley Art Museum and Pacific Film Archive and the Institute of Slavic, East European, and Eurasian Studies at UC Berkeley Promotional partner: Center for Russian, East European, and Eurasian Studies at Stanford University

This symposium is timed with the launch of BAMPFA’s series Georgian Cinema Now. Presentations by visiting Georgian artists and experts will offer greater context and insights into the current situation for independent filmmakers and journalists working in Georgia today.
