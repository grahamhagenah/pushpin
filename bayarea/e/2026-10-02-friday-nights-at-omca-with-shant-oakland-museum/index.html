Friday Nights at OMCA with Shanté

Oakland Museum · Fri, Oct 2 · 5pm · Free

Live Music by Shanté, Music Set by DJ Shell Heart, Drag Storytime with Per Sia, Mini House Making, Nexus Black Art Week Panel Discussion, and Gallery Chats This Friday Night at OMCA, Oakland singer-songwriter Shanté brings her soulful sound to the Garden Stage with a powerful live performance. Blending smooth R&B, heartfelt storytelling, and uplifting messages of resilience, faith, and self-discovery, the East Oakland artist delivers an intimate yet energetic set that invites audiences to sing along, reflect, and leave inspired.

Keeping the music going, DJ Shell Heart takes over the Amphitheater with a high-energy mix of Hip-Hop, R&B, Afrobeats, and classic Bay Area rap and party anthems. With more than a decade behind the turntables, DJ Shell Heart brings a vibrant sound rooted in culture, connection, and community.

Families can also gather in the Garden for Drag Storytime with Per Sia, San Francisco’s Drag Laureate, featuring a reading of Hazel’s Best Day: A Story of Community, Accessibility, and Pride in Being Yourself. Guests of all ages are also invited to make their own mini home inspired by Mildred Howard’s house sculptures, using colorful materials to create a tiny home that tells their story.

Guests can also join artists Mildred Howard and Adrian Burrell for a special conversation presented in partnership with MoAD Nexus Black Art Week, moderated by OMCA’s Senior Director of Marketing, Communications, and Visitor Experience, Autumn King.

Alongside an exceptional event experience, Friday Nights at OMCA also features Gallery Chats from 5:30–7:30 pm. Putting a twist on the traditional museum tour, Gallery Chats invite visitors to engage in meaningful conversations, share personal reflections, and explore new perspectives on the artwork, artifacts, and stories throughout OMCA’s galleries. Enthusiastic volunteer facilitators can be found floating between all of OMCA’s galleries, including its Great Hall Special Exhibitions, throughout the evening. Look for the blue “Let’s Chat” shirts to join the conversation. Gallery Chats are included with Museum admission.
