Films by the COUSIN Collective and Christophe Katrib

BAMPFA · Wed, Oct 7 · 7pm

A selection of works made by COUSIN collective artists, including Sky Hopinka’s 2017 consideration of the Dakota Access Pipeline protest and Lindsay McIntyre’s film about caribou and lichen—made using those materials in the photographic process—screen with Lebanese artist Christophe Katrib’s This Dissonance.
