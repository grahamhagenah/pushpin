Friday Nights at OMCA with CHIKA DI

Oakland Museum · Fri, Oct 9 · 5pm · Free

Live Music by CHIKA DI, Dance Lesson and Showcase with Hustle By The Lake, DJ set by BLOOM, and Gallery Chats This Friday Night at OMCA, Colombian-American singer-songwriter CHIKA DI brings her vibrant sound to the Garden Stage with a high-energy bilingual performance. Blending Afro-Latin rhythms, Cumbia pop, electronic influences, and heartfelt storytelling, her joyful set celebrates culture and connection while inviting audiences to dance, sing, and celebrate together.

Over in the Amphitheater, Hustle By The Lake brings Oakland’s vibrant social dance community to OMCA for an evening of movement, music, and connection. Start with a beginner-friendly Hustle lesson led by Marquee and Amanda, followed by a showcase and open dance set to disco, funk, and feel-good grooves. No partner or experience required—just come ready to move.

Keeping the energy going, Bay Area DJ and dancer BLOOM takes over the Amphitheater with a soulful music set designed to keep the dance floor moving. Rooted in music, movement, and community, BLOOM creates welcoming spaces where rhythm and shared energy bring people together.

Alongside an exceptional event experience, Friday Nights at OMCA also features Gallery Chats from 5:30–7:30 pm. Putting a twist on the traditional museum tour, Gallery Chats invite visitors to engage in meaningful conversations, share personal reflections, and explore new perspectives on the artwork, artifacts, and stories throughout OMCA’s galleries. Enthusiastic volunteer facilitators can be found floating between all of OMCA’s galleries, including its Great Hall Special Exhibitions, throughout the evening. Look for the blue “Let’s Chat” shirts to join the conversation. Gallery Chats are included with Museum admission.
