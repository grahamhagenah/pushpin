The Dreamed Adventure

BAMPFA · Sun, Oct 11 · 6:30pm

A Bulgarian archaeologist investigates the disappearance of a former lover in this absorbing Cannes Film Festival prizewinner. German director Valeska Grisebach worked with a nonprofessional cast to create a slow-burn thriller with the grit of a Western and the scope of Chinatown.
