Remembering Harun Farocki

BAMPFA · Wed, Oct 14 · 7pm

This is a new restoration of Claudia von Alemann and Reinold E. Thiel’s documentary on the legendary experimental film festival of Knokke, Belgium, featuring political filmmaker Harun Farocki in action. Shown with three short films by Farocki, including Inextinguishable Fire.
