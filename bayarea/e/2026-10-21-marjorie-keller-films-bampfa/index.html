Marjorie Keller Films

BAMPFA · Wed, Oct 21 · 7pm

The late Marjorie Keller’s films ranged from social and political critiques to intimate portraits of people who populated her world. Her intricate visual and sound editing is pointed while also being poetically suggestive. On the occasion of a new book about Keller’s films, contributor Steve Anker presents five of her films.
