ASL Interpretation for OMCA Garden and Amphitheater stage will be provided.

This fall, OMCA welcomes the community back for its 32nd annual Día de los Muertos Community Celebration, a vibrant day honoring the people, traditions, and stories that connect us across generations. Join the Día de los Muertos Committee for a procession that opens the celebration with music, movement, and a collective act of remembrance.

Throughout the day, the Museum comes alive with danza, ceremonia, live music, hands-on activities, and food. Across the campus, discover ofrendas created by community members, each offering a personal reflection on the lives and legacies of loved ones and ancestors.

Browse the mercado for handmade goods, altar items, and Día de los Muertos–inspired art from local makers. Bring a picnic, gather with family and friends, and make a day of it in OMCA’s gardens.
