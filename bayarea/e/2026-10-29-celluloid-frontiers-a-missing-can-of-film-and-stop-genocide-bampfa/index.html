Celluloid Frontiers: A Missing Can of Film and Stop Genocide

BAMPFA · Thu, Oct 29 · 7pm

Co-sponsored by the Institute for South Asia Studies, UC Berkeley and the Chowdhury Center for Bangladesh Studies, UC Berkeley, and the South Asia Art Initiative at UC Berkeley.

In conjunction with the exhibition Private Frontiers, BAMPFA presents Naeem Mohaiemen’s A Missing Can of Film (2025) and Zahir Raihan’s Stop Genocide (1971)—a film edited in the style of Soviet Realism and Third Cinema during the 1971 Bangladesh Liberation War.
