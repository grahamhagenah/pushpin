Performance / Entanglement: Steffani Jemison and Leslie Cuyjet on Maren Hassinger and Senga Nengudi

BAMPFA · Sat, Oct 31 · 1pm

Tickets required. Gallery admission included with ticket purchase.

Artist Steffani Jemison and choreographer Leslie Cuyjet perform a collaborative piece that reflects on the dynamic artistic relationship between Maren Hassinger and Senga Nengudi, the intense intimacy and attunement involved in longstanding artistic collaborations, and the powerful intergenerational legacy of the artists' collective works.
