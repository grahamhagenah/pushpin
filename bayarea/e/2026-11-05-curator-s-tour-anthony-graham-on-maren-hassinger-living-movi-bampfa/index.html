Curator’s Tour / Anthony Graham on Maren Hassinger: Living Moving Growing

BAMPFA · Thu, Nov 5 · 1:15pm

Senior Curator Anthony Graham offers a tour of Maren Hassinger: Living Moving Growing which he co-curated with Chief Curator Margot Norton. Author of the lead exhibition catalogue essay, Graham will explore the evolution of Hassinger's practice through her material experiments in sculpture, her long-standing engagement with movement and performance, and her work with the moving image.
