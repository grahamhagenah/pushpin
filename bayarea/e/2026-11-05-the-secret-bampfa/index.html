The Secret

BAMPFA · Thu, Nov 5 · 7pm

Digital Restoration

Loosely based on a real-life double-murder case, Ann Hui’s grisly debut feature traces the labyrinthine investigation by one of the victim’s friends, played by Sylvia Chang. At once procedural and spectral, The Secret “thrives on pure cinematic wizardry” (Faith Everard, Senses of Cinema).
