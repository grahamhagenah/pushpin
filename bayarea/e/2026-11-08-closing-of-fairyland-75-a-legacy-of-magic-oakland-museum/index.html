Closing of Fairyland @ 75: A Legacy of Magic

Oakland Museum · Sun, Nov 8

Closing of Fairyland @ 75: A Legacy of Magic With just a few weeks left to experience it, OMCA welcomes you to explore Fairyland’s rich history and impact on Oakland’s cultural landscape with Fairyland @ 75: A Legacy of Magic.

Explore a timeline of Fairyland’s journey, a “then-and-now” look at beloved park attractions, and a glimpse into the park’s bright future. Visitors will discover miniatures created by local artists, photos of Magic Keys through the decades, and one of Fairyland’s original Jolly Trolly cars for kids and families to climb aboard. Don’t miss this celebration of Fairyland’s lasting legacy and role in shaping Oakland’s cultural landscape before the feature closes.
