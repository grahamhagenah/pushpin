Waikiki Brothers

BAMPFA · Fri, Nov 13 · 4pm

A mediocre nightclub cover band strums its way across the forgotten hinterlands of South Korea in Yim Soon-rye’s now-cult classic—simultaneously deadpan and admiring, sorrowful and life-affirming—with lead roles for future superstars Hwang Jung-min and Ryoo Seung-bum.
