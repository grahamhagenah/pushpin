Artists’ Conversation: Emory Douglas and William Rhodes

BAMPFA · Sat, Nov 14 · 1pm

Tickets required. Gallery admission included with ticket purchase.

In a conversation moderated by Sita Kuratomi Bhaumik, Emory Douglas and William Rhodes discuss their collaborative piece Revolution in Our Lifetime, featured in Some Particular Heaven, and unpack how art can foster solidarity, reimagine collective power, and help realize new political possibilities.
