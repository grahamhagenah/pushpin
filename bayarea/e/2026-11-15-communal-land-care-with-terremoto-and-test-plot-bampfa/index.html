Communal Land Care with TERREMOTO and Test Plot

BAMPFA · Sun, Nov 15 · 4:30pm

Landscape architecture design studio TERREMOTO and nonprofit Test Plot discuss their approach to radical communal land care that cultivates beauty and biodiversity, and explore the belief that ecological and climate crises require a repairing of kinship with our lands and a return to the commons and to care.
