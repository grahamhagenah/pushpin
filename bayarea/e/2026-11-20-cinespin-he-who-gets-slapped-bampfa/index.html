CineSpin: He Who Gets Slapped

BAMPFA · Fri, Nov 20 · 7:30pm · Free

Free admission. Tickets available at the admissions desk beginning at 6:30 PM.

Join us for CineSpin, the BAMPFA Student Committee’s annual event featuring terrific UC Berkeley student musicians providing original live accompaniment to great movies. This year the Berkeley Nu Jazz Collective will accompany Victor Sjöström’s He Who Gets Slapped, starring Lon Chaney.
