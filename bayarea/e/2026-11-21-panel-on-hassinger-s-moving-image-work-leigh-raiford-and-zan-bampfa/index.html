Panel on Hassinger’s Moving Image Work: Leigh Raiford and Zana Sanders in Conversation with Robyn Farrell and Kristin Juarez

BAMPFA · Sat, Nov 21 · 1pm

Tickets required. Gallery admission included with ticket purchase.

BAMPFA will present several of Maren Hassinger’s video works, from documentation of her early performance works to her later, more cinematic works. Following the screening, a panel will discuss Hassinger’s unique approach to the moving image across her career.
