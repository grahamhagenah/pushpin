CRYSTAL LIZARD / PLUS OR MINUS

The Lilypad · Fri, Sep 18 · 7:30pm

$10 admission / 7:30 doors / 8pm show / seated & standing BIG night of original rock trios! CRYSTAL LIZARD and PLUS OR MINUS join forces for an instrumental tour-de-force you won't wanna miss!

Introducing CRYSTAL LIZARD, a new Boston power trio composed of veterans John Sheeran, Gavin McCarthy and Andrew Stern from Township, Beat Circus, Karate and a LOT of other bands/projects in/around Boston. Their first record, "Crystal Lizard", was released in June 2026 Crystal Lizard by Crystal Lizard PLUS OR MINUS Instrumental music leaves room. No lyrics to tell you what to feel, no words to get in the way - just sound and room for your own story. We've always believed that visual art doesn't just explain that space, it expands it.

Plus or Minus is how we explore that - a Boston-based trio writing and playing music built for the space between sound and image. Everything is played live with overdubs, never programmed.

We're longtime bandmates and collaborators Eliot Hunt (Chauncey / Kristin Cifelli) and Jon Bistline (Chauncey / Session Americana), alongside drummer Mike Piehl, formerly of Expanding Man and Reverse.
