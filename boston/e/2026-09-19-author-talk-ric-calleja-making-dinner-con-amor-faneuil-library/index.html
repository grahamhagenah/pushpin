Author Talk: Ric Calleja - Making Dinner con Amor

Faneuil Library · Sat, Sep 19 · 3pm

Local Brighton author Ric Calleja has written another book: Making Dinner con Amor: Stories, Poems and Recipes about Food, Cooking and Family.

Join us at this author event in which Ric will read passages from the book and discuss his family's culinary traditions.
