JOURNEYGLO ft. Micah Yoo

The Lilypad · Sat, Sep 19 · 5pm

$10 advance / $12 day of show / $8 student / 5pm doors/ 5:30 show / seated An intimate early evening set from JOURNEYGLO, featuring original songs performed with special guest Micah Yoo on acoustic guitar, plus a few collaborative moments.

<a href=" class="sqs-block-button-element--small sqs-button-element--tertiary sqs-block-button-element" data-sqsp-button > Get Tickets JOURNEYGLO is a Dallas-based singer-songwriter creating intimate indie folk-pop with cinematic, ambient sensibilities. Her debut album, Limp By Your Side, explores grief, faith, love, and the unresolved spaces between what was and what might be. For this stripped-down acoustic set, JOURNEYGLO brings the songs into a more vulnerable setting, centered on voice, guitar, haunting melodies, and honest storytelling that feels like sitting beside someone in the dark without rushing them toward resolution.

A classical violinist by training, a songwriter by hobby, and a licensed music therapist God willing — Micah Yoo is best described as an overall music enthusiast. Having taken an extended break from both writing and performing, Micah is excited to return to the stage alongside JOURNEYGLO and rediscover the joy of sharing music with a live audience.
