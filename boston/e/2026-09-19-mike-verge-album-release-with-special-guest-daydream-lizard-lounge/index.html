Mike Verge Album Release with special guest Daydream

Lizard Lounge · Sat, Sep 19 · 8pm · $15

Mike Verge celebrates the release of his latest album "Human Era" Sept 19th with special guests Daydream!

$15 adv/$20 door · Doors 7

Mike Verge is a singer, songwriter, and guitar player whose music has been compared to acts such as Tom Petty, John Mayer, the Black Crowes and the Jayhawks. This Berklee College of Music graduate and Boston native has played gigs from Portland, Maine to the Caribbean, from coffee shops to arenas.Mike and his band was handpicked by Hootie & the Blowfish front man Darius Rucker to open for the Blowfish’s sold-out New England appearances. Mike’s songwriting style has attracted the attention of The Band drummer Levon Helm, who offered him an opening slot on the Boston leg of his recent tour. Verge has also opened for several other big-name acts, including Dave Davies (The Kinks), Livingston Taylor, Buck Cherry and Cheap Trick.

Daydream is a Roots Jam Band that blends Americana, soulful songwriting, and hard-driving Rock n’ Roll with the free improvisation of Jam pioneers like the Grateful Dead and the Allman Brothers. Since 2023, Daydream has been honing a sound that harkens back to these formative influences, while carving a new path wholly its own.

After a long period in the Boston and New England jam scene playing covers with various groups, bandleader David Rubin started Daydream as an outlet for his own original music. David has won over audiences with his prowess and by playing with the some of most recognizable acts in the scene, including his own band Diamond Blues, at residencies, tour dates, and festivals in the Boston area. He’s played with Members of Hot Tuna, Ryan Montbleau, New Riders of the Purple Sage, Dark Star Orchestra, Guerilla Toss, Marble Eyes, Swimmer, Brown-Eyed Women, and Bearly Dead.

The band, along with David Rubin on guitar/vocals, is rounded out by on-the-rise powerhouses Liam Oulton on bass, Douglas Appleman on keyboards, and Robbie Parrish on drums/vocals.
