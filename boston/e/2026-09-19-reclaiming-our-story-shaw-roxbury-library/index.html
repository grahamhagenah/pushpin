Reclaiming Our Story

Shaw-Roxbury Library · Sat, Sep 19 · 2:30pm

Join lecturer Vicki Meredith-Hasan, a scholar of Africans in World History for a series of discussions about the evolution and various historical expressions of African classical culture throughout the ages.

Saturday, June 20th - 2:30 pm to 4:30 pm An exploration of the development of African classical culture.

Saturday, July 18th - 2:30 pm to 4:30 pm A review of ancient Kemet's cultural journey and it's current influence on modern society.

Saturday, August 22nd - 2:30 pm to 4:30 pm A look at the European cultural debt to the genius of African classical culture.

Saturday, September 19th - 2:30 pm to 4:30 pm An overview of the continuing saga of indigenous black Americans experience and influence in the USA.
