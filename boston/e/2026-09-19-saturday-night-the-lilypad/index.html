Saturday Night

The Lilypad · Sat, Sep 19 · 10pm

$12 advance / $15 at the door / 10pm doors / 10:30 start / all ages Three bands, one night, and a lot of good music. We’re all Berklee musicians bringing our own mix of rock, blues, and funk to the stage. Come hang, listen to some great players, and enjoy a night of live music with a few different sounds and plenty of energy.

My name is Lorena Ometto and I’m a Brazilian musician and Berklee-trained artist with a love for anything with a good groove. Growing up in Brazil has always been a big part of how I hear and feel music, and I bring that influence into everything I play. I love the energy of a live show, connecting with people, and keeping the music honest, soulful, and fun.

Kate Moore is a blues-rock artist and songwriter from San Diego, CA, currently studying at Berklee College of Music. Known for powerhouse, passion-filled vocals, Kate brings raw energy and soul to every performance.
