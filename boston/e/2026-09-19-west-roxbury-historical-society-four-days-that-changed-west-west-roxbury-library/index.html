West Roxbury Historical Society: Four Days That Changed West Roxbury

West Roxbury Library · Sat, Sep 19 · 2pm

The West Roxbury Historical Society presents: "Four Days that Changed West Roxbury: The Billings' Field Tot Lot.

Presented by members of the Community Playground Project.

Did you know? Over 40 years ago, neighbors came together to imagine and build an inclusive playground for West Roxbury's and Roslindale's children. The stone in the middle of the playground at Billings Field is a lasting reminder of the power of community.

Planning over the course of a year, funded and built by 2,000 West Roxbury and Roslindale neighbors, designed by our own children, this playground brought people together and proved the strength of People Power!!!
