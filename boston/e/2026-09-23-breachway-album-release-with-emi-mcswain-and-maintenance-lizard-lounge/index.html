Breachway Album Release with Emi McSwain and Maintenance

Lizard Lounge · Wed, Sep 23 · 8pm · $15

Breachway celebrates the release of their latest album with Emi McSwain and Maintenance September 23rd at 8!

$15 adv/$20 door · Doors 7

Breachway is Noah Barreto, an alt-country singer-songwriter from Providence, Rhode Island that now lives in Brooklyn, New York. His storytelling and joyful stage presence has generated buzz across the Northeast, leading to collaborations with the Newport Folk Festival and The Wild Honey Pie. His recent single “Giving Tree”, was selected for Tidal’s Spotlight program and placed at top of their “Indie Folk & In-Between.” That single was also covered in Glide Magazine, who described him as “well on his way to becoming a name to remember in alt-country.” He will release his debut self-titled album this September, supporting it with over 20 shows this fall. He was formerly a wild bird doctor, and his favorite bird is the Common Loon.

Emi McSwain is a singer-songwriter and content creator in Boston, Massachusetts. After receiving her degree in songwriting from Berklee College of Music in 2022, she took to Instagram to show tBoston’s hidden gems and highlight the music scene in the city.

Her debut record, “Them Apples”, is set to release in the fall of 2026. Inspired by 90s alternative bands, and she approaches complicated topics with an obvious sense of humor.

Maintenance is a 4 piece Indie Rock band from Bristol, Rhode Island. Influenced by Alternative/Rock/Folk, They try to push themselves to write songs that don’t fall into one category. A perfect Maintenance show has a good low to high energy dynamic and some tomfoolery. The band has been working on music nonstop and they are constantly evolving their sounds.
