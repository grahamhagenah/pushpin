Rebecca Black

Royale · Wed, Sep 23 · 8:45pm · 18+

In an age where people crave authenticity, all eyes are on Rebecca Black. The pop star holds a mirror up to the question of what’s real and what’s fake with Age of the Exhibitionist, her self-released sophomore album out now. Led by the delicious, catchy and full-throttle bangers “Speakerphone”, “Hot Wet Delirious”, “Michael.” and “Anaheim Star,” Black has never been more ambitious, daring and herself. Her latest comes off the heels of a crowd-pleasing run opening for Katy Perry’s Lifetimes tour as well as her Boiler Room debut. This year, the singer/songwriter/DJ/producer celebrates 15 years since her viral debut single “Friday” and is marking the anniversary by continuing to surprise the world with her most audacious release yet.
