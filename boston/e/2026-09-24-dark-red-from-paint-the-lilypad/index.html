DARK RED FROM PAINT

The Lilypad · Thu, Sep 24 · 10pm

$15 admission / 10pm doors / 10:30 start / standing / all ages “Dark red from paint” is the exploration of unorthodox sounds in order to comprehend unintelligible feelings. This mix of alt rock groups bring ethereal soundscapes and transgressive distortion to take the audience into unnamed yet understood memories. This wide range of bands provide a space where everyone has a place. Through energetic and heartfelt music be ready for an ecstatic rock set into the night.

Tracing is a post-rock project by Almeda Tracing.

La Manik is a 5 piece art rock band that creates catchy hooks and transgressive noise to connect with viewers through raw emotional expression. Originally formed with the goal to soundtrack Latin American short stories that narrate this society’s outliers, the band has now grown into a creative vehicle for the members to express themselves through songs that touch on: lost love, identity, and dark desires difficult to express in the day to day.

From Sonic Youth sprawls to 6/8 Isley Brothers choruses, Beatles inspired basslines and Descartes a Kant theatrics. La Manik puts into dialogue diverse influences in the songs themselves, showing the beauty of being different and the malleable nature of musical heritage.

Sikayda is a young, Boston-based band that focuses on finding beauty within the ordinary. Our sound combines distorted guitars and gentle, echoing vocals creating music that subverts expectations and floods the audience with raw emotion.
