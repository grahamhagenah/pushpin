Rebel with a Clause

West Newton Cinema · Thu, Sep 24 · 7pm

Come hang out with Brandt and Ellen at this National Punctuation Day grammar extravaganza! Ellen will set up her nationally acclaimed Grammar Table an hour before the show to answer your most pressing grammar questions, resolve long-standing family grammar disputes, and take complaints. After the screening, please stick around for a Q&A with Brandt and Ellen, followed by a book signing of Ellen’s national bestseller, also called Rebel with a Clause.

Rebel with a Clause is a charming and insightful documentary that follows grammarian Ellen Jovin as she travels the country with her “Grammar Table,” sparking spontaneous conversations about commas, clauses, and the quirks of English with curious passersby. What begins as a playful educational project turns into a moving exploration of civility, curiosity, and the unexpected ways language brings people together.

Directed by Brandt Johnson · 1h 26m
