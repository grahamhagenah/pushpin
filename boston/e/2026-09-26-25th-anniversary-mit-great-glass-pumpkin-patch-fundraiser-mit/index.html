25th anniversary MIT Great Glass Pumpkin Patch fundraiser

MIT · Sat, Sep 26 · 10am · Free

Over 2000 glass pumpkins of all sizes, shapes and colors are for sale on Kresge Oval to fund ongoing operations of the MIT Glass Lab.. Preview opening (no sales at the proview) is Friday, September 25th from 5PM to 8PM and the sale is on Saturday, September 26th from 10AM to 3PM. The sale is open to the public and admission is free.
