The Artist’s Voice: Lucy Raven with Ruth Erickson

ICA · Sun, Sep 27 · 10am · Free

Lucy Raven’s work explores the physical and mythical landscapes of the American West, as seen in her mesmerizing film installation—Murderers Bar. Witness the transformation of a landscape and embrace the subsequent release of energy and force through the largest dam removal project in North America. Join Raven and Ruth Erickson, Barbara Lee Chief Curator and Director of Curatorial Affairs at the ICA, in East Boston’s ICA Watershed for a morning conversation about her ongoing work centering industry and the American landscape. Coffee and breakfast snacks will be provided, as supplies last.
