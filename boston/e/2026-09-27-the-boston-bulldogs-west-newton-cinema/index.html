The Boston Bulldogs

West Newton Cinema · Sun, Sep 27 · 1pm

“The Boston Bulldogs” is a documentary film interviewing 5 people from a running club for addiction recovery, interconnected by one woman’s story from finishing the Boston Marathon after her first full year of sobriety to her relapse 24 hours later, and now her recovery 10 years later.

The West Newton Cinema will be donating $4 from every Adult Ticket and $2 from every Child and Senior Ticket to the Boston Bulldogs Running Club.

The screening will be followed by a panel discussion composed of leaders in Boston's recovery community. More info to come!
