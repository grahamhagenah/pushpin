Dumpster Demons Tour feat. Candi Carpenter and Moselle

The Rockwell · Mon, Sep 28 · 7pm · $15–$20 · All ages

Dumpster Demons Tour feat. Candi Carpenter and Moselle Join Candi Carpenter and Moselle for an evening of indie and alternative pop music celebrating Candi’s latest EP, Biblically Accurate Angel, and Moselle’s forthcoming album, Gargoyle. Through powerhouse vocals and fearless storytelling, both artists explore themes of toxic religion, trauma, identity, and self-discovery with equal parts vulnerability, humor, and heart. Expect an unforgettable evening of cathartic songs, intimate moments, and dynamic performances that invite audiences to laugh, heal, and celebrate the beautifully messy human experience.

Candi Carpenter (they/them) is a queer, non-binary, and openly autistic singer, songwriter, producer, and multi-disciplinary artist unafraid to shatter expectations. A true rock and alternative musician’s musician that is endorsed by Dolly Parton and Brandi Carlile, Candi writes songs that cut through bone; whether grappling with toxic religion, sexuality, identity, or the existential beauty of existence. Throughout their career, Candi has played major festivals such as CMA Fest, The Long Road Festival, and The British Country Music Festival as well as completing a successful cross country tour powered by their loyal fans.

Spotify/ Instagram / TikTok / Youtube / Website / EPK Moselle is an LA-based folk artist that has gathered over 50k followers on Instagram and 60k TikTok followers for their soulful melodies. Highlights include Highlonesome Music Festival, opening for Olive Klug, surpassing 1 million streams on single Pesticides, and the release of Jackalope, a vulnerable, but playful expression of one’s path post-trauma and the continual journey towards healing.
