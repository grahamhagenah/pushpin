The Moth: Boston StorySLAM: DIVAS

Arts at the Armory · Tue, Sep 29 · 6:30pm

Join us the fourth Tuesday of each month for the Boston Moth StorySLAM! This community-focused, open-mic storytelling competition is open to anyone can share a story on the night’s theme (changes every month).

Tonight’s theme is… Divas: five-minute stories about main character energy. From power plays to personal awakenings, glamorous to chaotic. Backstage meltdowns, ruthless comebacks, or the day you discovered your inner superstar. Tell us how you simply refuse to apologize for taking up space. High heels optional, confidence required. And now the nominees for best leading actor in a drama series are…

6:30pm Doors Open | 7:30pm Stories Begin *Seating is not guaranteed and is available on a first-come, first-served basis. Please be sure to arrive at least 10 minutes before the show. Admission is not guaranteed for late arrivals. All sales final.
