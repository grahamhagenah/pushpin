Director Spotlight: Situations

West Newton Cinema · Thu, Oct 1 · 7pm

Join us on October 1 to watch SITUATIONS and stay after for a Q&A with the Director, Greg Vrotsos.

Three months after a painful breakup, a Los Angeles based photographer drifts through Silverlake’s creative chaos—dodging well-meaning friends, career pressures, and fleeting flings—until an unexpected connection forces him to confront what he truly wants from love, art, and himself.

Greg Vrotsos is a Norwood-born, bi-coastal filmmaker known for his dynamic storytelling and keen eye for character-driven narratives. With over a decade of experience in the film and television industry, Greg has built a reputation for collaborating with some of the most respected talents in Hollywood.

His acting work spans acclaimed television series such as Netflix’s Orange Is The New Black, HBO’s Vinyl, and David Lynch’s Twin Peaks: The Return, as well as major studio films including Empire State and The Boston Strangler. Greg’s creative vision extends behind the camera, where his short films have been recognized on the festival circuit for their authenticity and emotional depth.

With SITUATIONS, Greg makes his feature debut as a writer and director, bringing a fresh, insightful perspective to stories of love, ambition, and self-discovery in Los Angeles.

Nina MacLaughlin is the author of Wake Siren: Ovid Resung, a finalist for a LAMBDA Literary Award and the Massachusetts Book Award, the acclaimed memoir Hammer Head: The Making of a Carpenter, and the companion to Winter Solstice, Black Sparrow’s Summer Solstice. She lives in Cambridge, Massachusetts.
