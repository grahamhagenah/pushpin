An Evening with Chef

The Lilypad · Fri, Oct 2 · 10pm · $10

$10 admission / 10pm doors / 10:30 start / standing Chef: a creative, expressing oneself through food.

At Chef Inc., we strive to foster community and love through face-melting jams and songs handcrafted to perfection. Drawing inspiration from rock n’ roll legends such as Grateful Dead, Phish, and Goose (yes, we’re a jam band), as well as country artists like Jason Isbell and indie artists like Noah Kahan, we aspire to bring music that resonates with every listener and live performances that linger in the audience’s heart, mind and soul.
