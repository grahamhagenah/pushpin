Community Read: Working with Octopuses

Lower Mills Library · Sat, Oct 3 · 2pm

Ever wondered what it’s like to work with octopuses, squid, and cuttlefish? Join undergraduate students Vivian Ives and Julian Hidalgo of Northeastern University as they dive into their research experience at the Marine Biological Laboratory in Woods Hole. Discover how these incredible creatures change colors, solve puzzles, and adapt to their environment. Hear what it’s like behind the scenes of marine research and aquarium work, and how you can take part in the science.

Both Ives and Hidalgo spent six months in Woods Hole, Ives conducting a study on how housing enrichment affects laboratory octopuses and Hidalgo working on husbandry and raising both octopuses and cuttlefish, as well as enrichment impacts.
