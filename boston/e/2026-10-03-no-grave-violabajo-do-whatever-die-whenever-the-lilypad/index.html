No Grave is the creative outlet for Bear Brown (Ho-Ag, Handsy). Their music evokes grainy VHS bootlegs of horror films seen through a smudged CRT pane. The images flicker with an ethereal glow, each frame imbued with the ghost of a past moment, beautifully distorted and distant.

VIOLABAJO is a Viola-Bass-Drums punk trio from Arlington and Cambridge.
