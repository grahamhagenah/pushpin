Songbuilders’ Soiree

Arts at the Armory · Sat, Oct 3 · 6:50pm

The Soirée celebrates music, community, and the spirit of play in an immersive and spontaneous fashion.

Come for the excitement, stay for the unified self-expression, and keep coming back for the delightful community. Songwriters bring us the tender fruits of their labor: songs! YOU, the amazing audience choir, will listen, sing along & even provide ideas for how we might arrange the song. You’ll witness the improvising house band figuring out arrangements in real time. In this intimate Armory Cafe edition, we’ll feel extra cozy and close to the magic.
