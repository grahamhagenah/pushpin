Live Nation Presents Santiago Cruz

Arts at the Armory · Sun, Oct 4 · 7pm

Santiago Cruz is one of the most important pop artists in Colombia and Latin America. With more than 20 years of career, the singer-songwriter from Ibagué, Tolima has become a key figure in the international pop music scene, earning multiple recognitions over the years. With 10 albums released to date, Santiago has received six Latin GRAMMY nominations. The “Sigo en Pie” Tour arrives alongside the release of his eleventh studio album, “Fragmentos,” which will feature 11 brand-new songs.
