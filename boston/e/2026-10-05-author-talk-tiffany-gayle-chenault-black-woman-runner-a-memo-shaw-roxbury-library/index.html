Author Talk: Tiffany Gayle Chenault - Black Woman Runner: A Memoir

Shaw-Roxbury Library · Mon, Oct 5 · 6pm

Author and Black Girls Run Boston member Tiffany Gayle Chenault discusses her new book, Black Woman Runner: A Memoir, a powerful story about grief, healing, identity, and the experience of being a Black woman in the running world; moderated by Jeff Davis of Black Men Run Boston.

In collaboration with Frugal Book Store.
