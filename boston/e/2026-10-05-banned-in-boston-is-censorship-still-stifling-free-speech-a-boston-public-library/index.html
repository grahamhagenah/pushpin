Banned in Boston: Is Censorship Still Stifling Free Speech? (A Talks for Better Living Event)

Boston Public Library · Mon, Oct 5 · 6pm

Join us in partnership with the Citywide Friends of the Boston Public Library for a timely and thought-provoking conversation featuring New York Law School Professor of Law Emerita Nadine Strossen and Harvard Law School Professor Randall Kennedy —two of America's foremost authorities on free expression and the First Amendment. After the hour-long main program, a book signing with both speakers will be facilitated by our local independent bookstore partner for this program, Trident Booksellers.

Why is some speech still banned not only in Boston, but also around the United States? What lines does the First Amendment draw between permissible and impermissible speech restrictions? Should those lines be redrawn in light of new technologies and their associated new dangers?

What speech the First Amendment actually protects – and does not protect – in contrast with widespread misunderstandings on point.

Why the Supreme Court Justices, all across the ideological spectrum, strongly protect speech that is deeply unpopular in the general public, including “hate speech,” “extremist speech,” and “disinformation.”

What history teaches us about the dangers of powerful new communications technologies –– and the dangers of censoring them.

How each of us can help foster a culture of open dialogue, intellectual curiosity, and democratic resilience.

For everyone who cares deeply about civil liberties, loves books, enjoys spirited debate, or simply wants to better understand one of the defining issues of our time, this is a conversation you will not want to miss.

Come prepared to be challenged. Come curious. Leave with a deeper understanding of why free speech matters to everyone.

Nadine Strossen: New York Law School Professor Emerita and Senior Fellow at FIRE (the Foundation for Individual Rights and Expression), was President of the American Civil Liberties Union from 1991 to 2008. An internationally acclaimed free speech scholar and advocate, who regularly addresses diverse audiences and provides media commentary around the world, Strossen serves on the advisory boards of the ACLU and several academic freedom/free speech organizations. She is the Host and Project Consultant for Free To Speak, a 3-hour documentary film series distributed on public television in 2023.
