2026-27 Alan Andres Writing Fellows Reading & Celebration

Boston Public Library · Tue, Oct 6 · 6pm

Celebrate the Associates of the BPL's 2026-27 Alan Andres Writer-in-Residence and Alan Andres Picture Book Writer Fellow.

The Associates of the Boston Public Library is hosting our annual Alan Andres Writing Fellows Reading to welcome our 2026-27 Writer-in-Residence, Janaea Eads, and 2026-27 Picture Book Writer Fellow, Jade Hawes. We will also be celebrating our outgoing writers, Zarlasht Niaz and Jessica Ruan.

General Admission (in-person): Join us at the Boston Public Library's Rabb Hall, located on the lower level of the Boylston Street Building in Copley Square. Seating is first-come, first-served.

For questions, contact the Associates of the Boston Public Library at hello@ or (617) 536-3886.

Accessibility Notice: We strive to make our events accessible. To request a disability accommodation and/or language services, contact the department listed in the "Contact Info" pane on the right. Please allow at least two weeks to arrange accommodation.

Programming presented by the Associates of the Boston Public Library is funded through the generous contributions of our supporters. To learn more about supporting the Associates, please visit the Associates of the Boston Public Library website.
