Behind the Screen: Primetime

West Newton Cinema · Tue, Oct 6 · 6:30pm

Primetime is a 2026 American psychological crime thriller film directed by Lance Oppenheim in his narrative feature film debut. Based on the 2007 Esquire article "Tonight on Dateline This Man Will Die" by Luke Dittrich, the film follows the production of the reality television series To Catch a Predator. Robert Pattinson stars as series host Chris Hansen, and also serves as a producer. Merritt Wever, Skyler Gisondo, and Phoebe Bridgers play supporting roles.

Luke Dittrich is a National Magazine Award-winning journalist and the author of the New York Times bestseller, Patient H.M.: A Story of Memory, Madness, and Family Secrets. His on-the-job experiences have included running a marathon in Antarctica, tracking a bull elephant across Northern Kenya, and walking 340-miles of the United States/Mexico border. His article “Tonight on Dateline This Man Will Die” was the basis for the film Primetime.
