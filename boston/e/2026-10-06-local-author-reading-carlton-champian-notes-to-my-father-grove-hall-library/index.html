Local Author Reading: Carlton Champian — Notes to My Father

Grove Hall Library · Tue, Oct 6 · 6:30pm

Join local author, Carlton Champian, for a reading and audience conversation about his new memoir, Notes to My Father. Told through a series of notes, this memoir explores fatherhood, absence, identity, and ultimately forgiveness and self-definition.

Mr. Champian will read several selected notes, share the related stories behind them, and open the floor for discussion and Q&A.
