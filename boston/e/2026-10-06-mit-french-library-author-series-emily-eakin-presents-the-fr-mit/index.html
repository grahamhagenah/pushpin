MIT / French Library Author Series: Emily Eakin presents The French Men, Or My Life in Theory

MIT · Tue, Oct 6 · 6:30pm

When Emily Eakin arrived at Harvard in the late 1980s, she fell under the spell of French theory. In her acclaimed new book, she intertwines her own youthful fascination with theory with the remarkable story of its rise in American intellectual and cultural life.
