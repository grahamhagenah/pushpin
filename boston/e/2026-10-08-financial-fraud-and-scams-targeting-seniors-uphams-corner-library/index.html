Financial Fraud and Scams Targeting Seniors

Uphams Corner Library · Thu, Oct 8 · 6pm

Financial scams continue to evolve and often target older adults. On October 8, join the CFA Society Boston lecturers as they explain how common scams work and why seniors are frequently targeted. The goal is to build awareness and confidence so participants can better protect themselves and others.

Understand common types of financial fraud and scam tactics Learn a simple framework for spotting warning signs and red flags Know practical steps to reduce risk and respond to suspected scams Live session. Presenters are neutral subject matter experts from CFA Society Boston, delivering community centered education with nonprofit partners. This session is educational and not sales oriented; no products or firms are promoted.
