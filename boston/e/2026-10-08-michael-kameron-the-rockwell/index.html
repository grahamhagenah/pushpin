Michael Kameron

The Rockwell · Thu, Oct 8 · 7pm · $12 · All ages

SHOW DESCRIPTION: Michael Kameron celebrates his biggest headline show to date. An alternative pop artist from a small town in Wisconsin, Michael Kameron makes music that turns everyday chaos, vulnerability, and confession into something that feels like a movie soundtrack. Built alongside his best friend in a bedroom studio, his music lives somewhere between crashouts and clarity, late-night honesty and emotional release.

Joining him are Dexter Suhn and Rowan Grace, rounding out a night of emotional, story-driven performances and songs that linger long after they end.

Michael Kameron is cinema. An alternative pop artist from a small town in Wisconsin, he never remains still, finding himself in new foreign places, feeding his inspiration of making life feel like a movie and creating the soundtrack to it. Michael Kameron is often described as a bold statement layered with vulnerability and confession. Producing music with his best friend in a bedroom, the music is genre fluid, with cinematic elements, and built around mood and storytelling. He has built a whole universe to play in and explore. It’s complex but simple: Crashouts + confessions + long socks = your favorite alt character. — IG: @the.michaelkameron Dexter Suhn is a 21 year-old alternative r&b singer from the San Francisco Bay Area. While still a teenager, he stumbled into producing after landing a cut on SZA’s SOS Deluxe album, generating buzz as a rising producer, guitarist, and gifted songwriter in the West Coast. Now, Suhn is returning to his artist journey in full steam, with plans to release an extended body of work in the near future. — IG: @dextersuhn Rowan Grace is a South Dakota-born singer-songwriter blending soulful vocals, honest storytelling, and pop driven melodies. Her sound, rooted in indie pop and soul, has been compared to artists like Yebba, Remi Wolf, and Lizzy McAlpine. A Top 10 finalist from Season 22 of The Voice, Rowan is now based in Boston while attending Berklee College of Music, creating music for anyone who has ever overthought, overfelt, or overshared.
