Sol Fyah Sessions with Safiya

Lizard Lounge · Thu, Oct 8 · 8pm · $15 · 21+

Vocalist Safiya returns to the Lizard Lounge October 8th for Sol Fiya Sessions!

Must Be 21+ · $15/$20 door · Doors 7

Sol Fyah Sessions is a live music experience led by Boston Music Award–nominated vocalist Safiya, blending jazz and Caribbean-inspired sound into a night that feels like listening to a record.

Think Side A with neo-soul, jazz, and R&B. Smooth, intimate, and reflective.

Then Side B with reggae, calypso, and world rhythms. Warm, rhythmic, and groove-forward.

This is a show where you can relax, sip, and unwind, while still finding yourself nodding your head, swaying, and catching the pocket. A night rooted in feel-good energy, live musicianship, and timeless grooves.

Safiya is a Boston Music Award–nominated vocalist bringing a rich blend of jazz and Caribbean-infused sound to the stage. Her music lives at the intersection of neo-soul, jazz, R&B, reggae, calypso, and global rhythms, delivered with warmth, groove, and intention. Safiya’s performances invite listeners to settle in, feel the rhythm, and move at their own pace. Equal parts laid-back and deeply soulful.
