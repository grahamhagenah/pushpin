The AJ Foss Band / The Glory Dogs / Madmax and the Perpetrators

The Lilypad · Thu, Oct 8 · 10pm

$15 admission / $12 advance / 10pm doors / 10:30 start / standing Enjoy a spectacular night of blues and rock n roll! Our line up of talented musicians will have you grooving and dancing the night away with hits of the past/present and creative originals. Each band has unique qualities and virtuosity to offer.

AJ, a musician from Maine, is a versatile guitarist, vocalist, and songwriter. A student at Berklee College of Music, he aspires to be a prolific and influential artist. AJ has years of experience playing gigs alone and with a full band at many different venues. Genres include blues, pop, rock, folk, country and more!

Hailing from Boston, The Glory Dogs are bringing Southern rock and classic country back into the spotlight. With roots across the American South, Midwest, and Northeast, the band brings grit, soul, and a little rowdy charm to every stage. Blending the spirit of timeless songs with an original sound of their own, their live show pulls at nostalgic heartstrings while giving fans every reason to raise a glass, hit the dance floor, and stay for one more song.

Mad Max and the Perpetrators are a blues, rock, and soul band from the Boston area. Max Milowski, an accomplished blues musician from the San Francisco Bay Area, fronts the band on vocals, guitar, and harmonica. With Nick Spencer on guitar, Brody Copple on bass, Jordan Posnik on drums, Domenic Rigazzi on tenor sax, and Philipp Schwarz on trumpet, the band plays an exciting variety of original music that blends blues, soul, jazz, rock, and more! The band also performs covers of songs by artists such as Albert King, Jimi Hendrix, and the Blues Brothers. They have performed at various New England venues such as the Middle East, the American Legion Post 440, and the Common Room.
