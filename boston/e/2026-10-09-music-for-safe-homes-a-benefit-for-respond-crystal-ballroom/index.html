Music for Safe Homes: A Benefit for RESPOND

Crystal Ballroom · Fri, Oct 9 · 7:30pm

Music for safe homes, a concert to benefit Respond, Inc. It's been a bit, but The Ames Curve is back. Grab a supportive pair of shoes and come join us for a special night of music also featuring Alexayndria...Go!, and The Rockery. Come support local art and win prizes from local businesses. The best part? All proceeds will be donated directly to RESPOND, Inc. RESPOND, Inc., based here in Somerville, is New Englands first domestic violence agency, dedicated to partnering with individuals, families, and communities to end the serious public health issue of domestic violence through prevention, intervention, and direct services. Come out, enjoy good music, and help provide life-saving shelter to survivors of domestic abuse. Secure your tickets now and be part of this impactful event.
