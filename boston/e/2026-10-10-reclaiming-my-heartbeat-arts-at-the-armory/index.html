Reclaiming My Heartbeat

Arts at the Armory · Sat, Oct 10 · 7:30pm

A live storytelling experience about transplant, resilience, and patient advocacy.

A second chance at life was only the beginning.

In Reclaiming My Heartbeat, Kyreé Miller brings humor, honesty, and hard-earned perspective to the stage as he shares his journey through five open-heart surgeries, two LVADs, and ultimately, a heart transplant.

But this isn’t simply a story about surviving. It’s about what happens afterward—reclaiming your body, finding your voice, advocating for yourself, and building a life that feels like your own again.

Part live storytelling experience and part audience conversation, the evening will include Kyreé’s signature humor, an interactive Q&A, and time to connect after the show.

Whether you’re a patient, caregiver, healthcare professional, or someone rebuilding after life changed unexpectedly, Reclaiming My Heartbeat is a celebration of resilience, self-advocacy, and learning how to truly live after survival.

Presented by Kyreé Miller and Five Heartbeats Productions LLC.
