John Lebanon is a Boston-based indie-folk project blending intimate songwriting with elements of folk, alternative, and indie rock. The music explores memory, identity, relationships, displacement, and the strange beauty of everyday life. Kite Without a String, released in June 2026, is the project's newest album. For this Lilypad performance, John Lebanon will perform as a five-piece band featuring Roy, Marc, Karl, Matt, and Stefanos.
