Renan Teuman Quintet

The Lilypad · Sun, Oct 11 · 6pm

$15 admission / 6pm doors / 6:30 start / seated The repertoire for this performance will include compositions by the band members, Thelonious Monk, Dave Holland, Ralph Alessi, and Pheeroan Aklaff, among others.

Renan Teuman is an architect and drummer from Istanbul, Turkey, who moved to New York in the fall of 2017. He has studied with renowned musicians Ferit Odman, Cem Aksel, Dave Allen, Vijay Iyer, Mark Ferber, Greg Hutchinson, and Steven Crammer. He has been performing in various clubs across the USA, Canada and Turkey.
