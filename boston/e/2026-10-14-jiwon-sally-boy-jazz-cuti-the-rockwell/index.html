Jiwon, Sally Boy, & Jazz Cuti ARTIST BIO: jiwon is from nyc and grew up immersed in a diverse musical environment. proficient in piano, cello, bass guitar, and drums, Jiwon found music to be his expressive refuge. his style, an eclectic mix inspired by artists like Lou Reed and Jai Paul, defies easy categorization. while studying philosophy at harvard, he deepened his musical commitment, and during the pandemic, major label interest affirmed his career choice. jiwon’s recent single “telephone” received critical acclaim for its introspective and authentic sound. as he continues to innovate, jiwon remains dedicated to his craft, distinguishing himself with genuine passion and sincerity in a crowded musical landscape.
