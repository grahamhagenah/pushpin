Reporting from Contemporary Italy: Q&A with Jason Horowitz, New York Times Europe Correspondent As Rome Bureau Chief for the New York Times for the last eight years, Jason Horowitz reported from the front lines of Italy and the Mediterranean region. In his new book The Italian Job: A Memoir of an American Reporter in Rome, he shares an insider’s view of politics and culture in Italy today, including the rise of Giorgia Meloni and the election of the first North American Pope.

Signed copies of the book will be available for sale.

Jason Horowitz, New York Times Europe Correspondent Serenella Sferza, Co-Director, MIT-Italy Program
