Au Revoir les Enfants

West Newton Cinema · Thu, Oct 15 · 4pm

In 1943, Julien is a student at a French boarding school. When three new students arrive, including Jean Bonnet, Julien believes they are no different from the other boys. What Julien doesn't know is that the boys are actually Jews who are evading capture by the Nazis. While Julien doesn't care for Jean at first, the boys develop a tight bond -- while the head of the school, Père Jean, works to protect the boys from the Holocaust.
