Producer's Circle: Untitled RV Family Documentary

West Newton Cinema · Thu, Oct 15 · 6:30pm

As her family travels America in an RV, teenage Leilani comes of age torn between her father’s dream of freedom and desire to live a normal teenage life.

A family of five sells everything and moves three kids into an RV—convinced they will learn more from experience than school. At the heart of the story is twelve-year-old Leilani and her dreamer father Eric. Set against some of America’s most spectacular landscapes, we watch Leilani grow up from 12 to 19—as Eric keeps reaching for more freedom and she begins craving the very things he is trying to escape.

Filmed over seven years, this intimate father-daughter story becomes a larger exploration of freedom, family, and what happens when the people you love begin wanting different lives.

This Producers Circle fundraising screening presents a work-in-progress cut. Stay afterward for a conversation with the filmmakers; audience feedback will help shape the final film.

VANESSA CARR — Director / Cinematographer Vanessa Carr is a documentary director and cinematographer known for intimate, character-driven cinéma vérité. Her work has appeared on HBO, Netflix, Disney+, and PBS and screened at Sundance, SXSW, Tribeca, TIFF, IDFA, and other major festivals. She was named to DOC NYC’s 40 Under 40 and is a LEF/CIFF and Film Independent Documentary Story Lab Fellow. Vanessa specializes in filming people through moments of profound change, bringing the intimacy and visual storytelling of her cinematography to her work as a director.

JOSH GLEASON — Director / Sound Josh Gleason is a documentary director and producer whose work explores American life through intimate, deeply observed stories. He co-directed the feature documentary The Disrupted and has directed and produced work for PBS, Showtime, The Atlantic, This American Life, and NPR. His television credits include Finding Your Roots, American Experience, and The Circus. Across film, television, and radio, Josh’s work is defined by long-term access and an interest in how family, work, and social change shape people’s lives. Josh is a LEF/CIFF Fellow, a Film Independent Story Lab Fellow, and a member of the DGA.
