Starr Forum: What Would the US Do in a Middle Power Order?

MIT · Thu, Oct 15 · 4:30pm · Free

Anne-Marie Slaughter, Incoming Dean of Public Policy at the London School of Economics A globally recognized scholar, policy innovator, and public leader, Anne-Marie Slaughter is the incoming dean of public policy at the London School of Economics, and had previously served as the CEO of New America, and as the director of policy planning at the US State Department.

Fotini Christia, Ford International Professor of the Social Sciences; Director, MIT Institute for Data, Systems, and Society Taylor Fravel, Arthur and Ruth Sloan Professor of Political Science; Director, MIT Security Studies Program Noelle Selin, Professor, MIT Institute for Data, Systems and Society and the Department of Earth, Atmospheric and Planetary Sciences (EAPS); Director, MIT Center for Sustainability Science and Strategy Refreshments will be served after the event in the main lobby.
