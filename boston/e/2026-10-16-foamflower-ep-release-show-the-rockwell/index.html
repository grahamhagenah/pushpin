Foamflower EP Release Show

The Rockwell · Fri, Oct 16 · 7pm · $15–$20 · 21+

Join us at The Rockwell for a curated night of Indie Pop/Rock, featuring Foamflower’s EP release, and special guests!

Foamflower has been a band for almost four years. Starting in February of 2023, the group met and started performing under the band name “Knee Socks.” Three years and three drummers later, foamflower is celebrating their success throwing an album release party, with their new album “Without a Reason” coming out this October. They are so thankful for everyone that has supported them all this time, and can’t wait to share their music.
