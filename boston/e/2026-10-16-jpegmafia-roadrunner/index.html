JPEGMAFIA

Roadrunner · Fri, Oct 16 · 8pm · All ages

JPEGMAFIA (Barrington DeVaughn Hendricks, "Peggy") is a multi-talented experimental rapper, producer, performer and leader in the underground rap scene. After producing six tracks on Kanye West’s latest album and a very successful 2023 with “SCARING THE HOES!” JPEGMAFIA returns with a new album – title TBD – and headline tour named LAY DOWN MY LIFE.

This run follows his very successful co-headline tour alongside Danny Brown for their collaborative album “SCARING THE HOES!” which included sold-out shows nationwide including landmark venues like The Hollywood Palladium. The album debuted at number 84 on the Billboard 200 and entered at position 39 on the Top R&B/Hip-Hop Albums chart, while also charting in six other countries including Canada and the UK. In recent years, JPEGMAFIA has released critically acclaimed projects such as "Veteran" (2018), "All My Heroes Are Cornballs" (2019), and "LP!" (2021) – with praise from The Guardian, XXL, Office Mag, Complex, and many more.

JPEGMAFIA’s other recent performances include Primavera Sound, Pitchfork Music Festival, CDMX, Listen Out, and more, with upcoming festivals including Portola and Outbreak Fest on the horizon.
