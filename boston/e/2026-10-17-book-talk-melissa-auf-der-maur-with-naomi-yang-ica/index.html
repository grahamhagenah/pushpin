Book Talk: Melissa Auf der Maur with Naomi Yang

ICA · Sat, Oct 17 · 2pm · $10–$15

*Event price waived with online book pre-purchase; books are available from the ICA Store.

Talk + book: Melissa Auf der Maur: My ’90s Rock Photographs, $44.95 for members / $49.95 for nonmembers Talk + book: Even the Girls Will Cry: A ’90s Rock Memoir, $29.25 for members / $32.50 for nonmembers Talk + both books: $74.20 for members / $82.45 for nonmembers “What’s funny is I mostly picture Melissa with a bass in hand back then and don’t remember her having a camera in her hands at all. Perhaps that’s the point? Her incredible capture of so many innocent and unguarded moments.” -Billy Corgan, Smashing Pumpkins The ICA presents photographer and musician Melissa Auf der Maur—former member of bands Hole and Smashing Pumpkins—in a conversation and book signing to celebrate her latest hardback photobook, Melissa Auf der Maur: My ‘90s Rock Photographs (Art Gallery of Ontario and DelMonico Books). Auf der Maur will be joined by Naomi Yang, multi-disciplinary artist and former member of the ’80s indie band, Galaxie 500. A book signing follows with Auf der Maur autographing copies of the photobook and her memoir, Even the Girls Will Cry: A ’90s Rock Memoir (DeCapo).
