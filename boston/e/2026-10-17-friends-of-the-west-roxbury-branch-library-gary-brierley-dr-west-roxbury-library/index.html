Friends of the West Roxbury Branch Library: Gary Brierley "Dr. Mole": The History of the Hoosac Tunnel in Western Mass.

West Roxbury Library · Sat, Oct 17 · 2pm

Built to connect Boston and eastern markets to the Hudson River Valley, it was the longest tunnel in North America upon its completion.

Dr. Brierley has more than 50 years of experience with both technical and nontechnical aspects of underground engineering and construction management. He has worked on more than 1,000 soil and rock tunneling projects involving design, construction, and consultations. He is the author of more than 200 professional papers and magazine articles and holds a PHD in Civil Engineering from the University of Illinois.
