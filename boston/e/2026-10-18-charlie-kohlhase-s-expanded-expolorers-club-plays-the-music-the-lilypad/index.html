Charlie Kohlhase's (Expanded) Expolorers Club Plays The Music Of John Tchicai

The Lilypad · Sun, Oct 18 · 6pm

$20 admission / 6pm doors / 6:30pm start /seated The Explorers Club will be recording an album of John Tchicai compositions in December and January. We'll be recording Tchicai compositions from his days in the 60's with the New York Contemporary Five and the New York Art Quartet, from the 70's, 80's, 90's and aughts.
