Gallery Talk: Meghan Clare Considine on Cynthia Daignault: Valley

ICA · Sun, Oct 18 · 2pm

What does it mean to be alive at a specific point in time?

Baltimore-based artist Cynthia Daignault meditates on this question in her monumental, multi-panel installation featured in the exhibition Cynthia Daignault: Valley. Join Curatorial Assistant Meghan Clare Considine for a conversation about this never-before-seen work and learn more about Daignault’s practice of “long-form painting.”
