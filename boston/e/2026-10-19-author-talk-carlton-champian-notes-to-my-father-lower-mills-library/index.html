Author Talk: Carlton Champian — Notes to My Father

Lower Mills Library · Mon, Oct 19 · 6pm

What happens when a father’s absence becomes part of who you are? Join Boston-area author Carlton Champian for a reading and discussion of his book, Notes to My Father, a deeply personal exploration of fatherhood, abandonment, identity, and the journey of confronting what we inherit.
