Yandy Garcia live at the Lizard Lounge October 21st at 8pm!

Yandy García is a Cuban drummer, singer, songwriter, and producer whose music bridges the rich traditions of Cuban music with the energy of R&B, funk, jazz, and contemporary sounds. Drawing from his roots while embracing a modern approach, he creates performances that are both deeply rhythmic and emotionally expressive.

Yandy has performed at renowned venues including Blue Note New York, as well as other major theaters and stages, captivating audiences with his dynamic musicianship and distinctive artistic voice.

Backed by a dynamic band, Yandydelivers an engaging live experience that blends original music with fresh interpretations, celebrating the spirit of Cuban music through a unique and contemporary voice.
