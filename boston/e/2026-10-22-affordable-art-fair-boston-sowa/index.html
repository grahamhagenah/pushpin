Affordable Art Fair Boston

SoWa · Thu, Oct 22

After a hugely successful first edition in 2025, Affordable Art Fair is thrilled to be returning to Boston in Fall 2026! Join us at SoWa Power Station for our spectacular second edition. The fair will showcase galleries from Boston and beyond with a mixture of emerging and established artists. With thousands of artworks for sale – all priced under $12,000 – we’re sure you’ll find something to suit your taste, space and budget.
