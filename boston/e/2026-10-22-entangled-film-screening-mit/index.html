"Entangled" Film Screening

MIT · Thu, Oct 22 · 7pm · $5–$15

Join us for ENTANGLED (75 minutes), an award-winning, feature-length film about climate change’s acceleration of the collision between critically-endangered right whales, North America’s most valuable fishery, and the federal agency mandated to protect both.

This film screening will be followed by a conversation with filmmaker and reporter David Abel, and Erica Fuller, Senior Counsel in the Ocean Program at the Conservation Law Foundation.

A limited number of free tickets are available for full-time students with ID. Please reach out to museumregadmin@mit.edu.

This program is presented as part of the MIT Museum’s thematic focus on OCEANS. Image courtesy of NOAA.
