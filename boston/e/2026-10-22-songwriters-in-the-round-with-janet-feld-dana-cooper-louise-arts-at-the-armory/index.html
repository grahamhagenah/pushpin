Songwriters In-The-Round with Janet Feld, Dana Cooper & Louise Coombe

Arts at the Armory · Thu, Oct 22 · 7:30pm

Join Louise Coombe, Dana Cooper & Janet Feld for an intimate night of songwriting magic in the round!

Songwriters, Louise Coombe, Dana Cooper & Janet Feld are excited to be performing their original songs in-the-round style at the Rooted Cafe. Sharing their work in this way with an audience is icing on the cake, the cake being spending time together swapping songs, sharing stories and harmonies.

Dana Cooper's 32nd Release "The Ghost of Tucumcari" was Voted #1 Album of 2024 on Americana Highways Readers Poll. This album features Lyle Lovett, Hayes Carll, Susan Gibson, Darden Smith, Shake Russell and more. It takes listeners on a stirring musical journey across genres from Folk and Rock to Country and Americana. Charting #7 on Folk Chart & #9 on Alt Country Radio Charts. Receiving Rave Reviews in American Songwriter Magazine, Glide Magazine, M Music Magazine & more.

Dana Cooper is a poetic songwriter, an insightful storyteller, and a rousing musician whose performances engage & inspire audiences around the world. Central to Cooper's unique artistry is his ability to connect with audiences through his authentic stories, sense of humor and warm stage presence.

His vast career started with his 1st self-titled Release on Elektra Records in the 70's. The studio band consisting of legendary players -"The Section" with Leland Sklar & Russ Kunkel AKA "The Immediate Family." Since then, he worked with Shake Russell in Texas, releasing several albums together and performing on "Austin City Limits." Dana has also collaborated with several established artists - Kim Carnes (Betty Davis Eye's), Don Henry, Hal Ketchum, Kim Richey, Tom Kimmel & more. His 2022 CD "I Can Face The Truth" garnered much praise and attention, charting on 5 different Radio Charts.

"Dana has a poet's eye for beauty & detail, a blue's man's soul and a songbird's gift. He is remarkable." - Hayes Carll Louise Coombe (formerly Mosrie) grew up in rural west Tennessee on a cattle farm - riding horses and writing poetry. Her folky/Americana pop songwriting has won top awards at Kerrville Folk Festival, Wildflower! Festival, Telluride Bluegrass Festival, SolarFest and Falcon Ridge Folk Festival.
