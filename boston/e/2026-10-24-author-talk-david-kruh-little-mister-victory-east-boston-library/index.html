Author Talk: David Kruh - ﻿Little Mister Victory

East Boston Library · Sat, Oct 24 · 2pm

On August 15, 1945, as Bostonians celebrated the end of World War II, a well-dressed woman asked a teenage boy to watch her newborn baby. She promised to return after performing an errand but never did. Little Mister Victory: A Daughter's Search for the Woman Who Abandoned Her Father on VJ Day is the true story of a lifelong quest, by the baby's daughter, to find the woman who abandoned her father.

Join us when author David Kruh visits to speak about this fascinating book which also has an East Boston connection.
