Rescheduled 2026 MIT Great Glass Pumpkin Patch

MIT · Sat, Oct 24 · 10am · Free

Originally scheduled for Saturday 9/26, and now rescheduled for Saturday 10/24, the 25th annual MIT Great Glass Pumpkin Patch offers glass pumpkins of all colors, shapes and sizes for sale to the general public. Proceeds from this event fund the ongoing operations of the MIT Glass Lab, a program that teaches classes in team-based glassblowing to members of the MIT community.
