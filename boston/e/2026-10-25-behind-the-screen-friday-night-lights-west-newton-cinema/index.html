Behind the Screen: Friday Night Lights

West Newton Cinema · Sun, Oct 25 · 1pm

Based on H.G. Bissinger's book, which profiled the economically depressed town of Odessa, Texas and their heroic high school football team, The Permian High Panthers.

Steve Burton is the Sports Director for WBZ-TV and WSBK-TV in Boston. He also anchors the weekly programs Sports Final, Patriots Game Day, All Access, and the Patriots 5th Quarter show. Steve is a graduate of and a former quarterback for Northwestern University holding a bachelor of science degree in Communications and a master's degree in Broadcast journalism. Football is in Steve’s DNA, being the son of former Boston Patriots player Ron Burton.

Dan Shaughnessy is a sports columnist and associate editor at the Boston Globe. He has been named Massachusetts Sportswriter of the Year 14 times and 12 times has been voted one of America's top ten sports columnists by the Associated Press Sports Editors. In 2016 Shaughnessy was the recipient of the BBWAA Career Excellence Award for "meritorious contributions to baseball writing,'' presented at the Baseball Hall of Fame in Cooperstown, New York. Shaughnessy has written thirteen books, including New York Times bestseller "Francona,'' "The Curse of the Bambino,'' and "Senior Year." He most recently wrote "Wish It Lasted Forever -- Life With The Larry Bird Celtics", a book about his days covering the 1980s Bird Celtics.

Nick Capodilupo is the Head Football Coach at Newton North High School. A Dean of students at Newton North, he has been teaching and coaching for twenty years. Growing up in a Football family, Nick’s dad Peter served as head coach of the Tigers for three decades. Nick played Football and Lacrosse at Salve Regina University in Newport, Rhode Island before pursuing a post secondary career as a coach, special educator, and administrator.
