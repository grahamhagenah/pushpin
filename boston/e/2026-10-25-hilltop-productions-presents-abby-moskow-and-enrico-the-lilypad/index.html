Presented by Hilltop Productions, this show will also be in the Halloween spirit. Costumes encouraged!

Rooted in emotional honesty and innate curiosity, Abby Moskow blends dreamy R&B production and harmony stacks with introspective lyricism in her music, creating a fairy-like sound space that feels both ethereal and grounded. Currently based in the Boston area, Abby has spent the past few years bringing her music to live stages across Massachusetts, Rhode Island, New York and Los Angeles, while continuing to write, record and share her music online. With each release, Moskow continues to build a discography that invites listeners to slow down, feel deeply, and connect with themselves and the world around them. Linktree.

Enrico’s sound consists of acoustic ballads to indie pop jams with lyrics that make a listener feel as if they were walking through their own memories of love and loss. Taking influence from Daniel Caesar, Dijon, Dominic Fike, and Frank Ocean - Enrico’s story, told through his music, hopes to provide comfort and reflection like that of a best friend.
