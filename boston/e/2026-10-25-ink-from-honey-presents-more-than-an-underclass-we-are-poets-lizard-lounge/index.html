Ink From Honey Presents: More than an Underclass, We are Poets; Community Outreach: The Resource to Freedom is Through You & I.

$10

The fourth installment of our incarcerated poets project, where the cold, silent steel of prison bars melts against the raw warmth of human voice.

Incarcerated and formerly incarcerated poets pour their heartbreaks and triumphs onto the stage, demanding that we feel the heavy, hollow ache of their absence from our streets. Their words remind us that society bleeds from a fractured rib cage as long as our neighbors are locked away in solitary shadow. This unforgettable night shatters the distance between us, proving that true liberation cannot grow in isolation—our only resource to freedom is the living, breathing bridge built between You and I.

Hello, my name is Artem Vaskanyan. I am an Armenian refugee from Azerbaijan. During the war between Armenia and Azerbaijan, my family and I, at age six, migrated to the Russian parts of the former Soviet Union, and years later, in 1993, we migrated to the United States. At age twenty, in the year 2000, I was arrested and imprisoned. During my years of incarceration, my life set me on the path of spiritual and intellectual growth. Because of my drive to persevere, I was able to graduate from Boston University with a bachelor’s degree in liberal arts in 2020, become an activist, a writer & Poet, and a published author of three books.

My name is Amos Don, and I am a dedicated scholar, creative author, and community advocate currently enrolled in the Emerson Prison Initiative of Emerson College. Despite serving a life sentence for a wrongful conviction, I channel my resilience into civic engagement, literature, and leadership. I am a published poet, children’s book author, and the Head of Community Outreach for the African American Coalition Committee (AACC). Alongside my wife, Joanna Don, I am the Co-Founder of DonofCreation/Ink from Honey LLC, using the power of words and community action to inspire change, foster connection, and create impactful events.

My name is Oasis Pritchett. I am a young, black, and gifted aboriginal man. I am currently incarcerated and in my last year as a student of Emerson College.
