Watsky x Infinity

Somerville Theatre · Mon, Oct 26 · 7pm

In 2016, Watsky, a genre-bending rapper and poet known for verbal gymnastics, capped his creatively ambitious record x Infinity with a visual album comprised of 18 interconnected music videos. To celebrate its tenth birthday, Watsky performs the album in its entirely alongside a synced projection of the album film.
