Ethan Rossiter Band/Lady Lupine

Lizard Lounge · Wed, Oct 28 · 8pm · $15

Ethan Rossiter and Lady Lupine live at the Lizard Lounge October 28th!

Local singer/songwriter Ethan Rossiter (and his killer band) are a fun live crew! Their tunes stretch many styles. From jangly rock to folk to reggae, his toe-tapping songs take on themes of love and aging. Rossiter has been recording and performing his songs for many years (including a run for a few years creating kids/family music with his band The Jamberries). The band hopes you will catch them soon and enjoy some original, upbeat music.

Lady Lupine is a Boston based heavy soul band that delivers high energy performances of unique original material. The band creates an engaging live experience by cultivating visceral connections through sound. Fronted by vocalist Christina Lacoste, Lady Lupine draws inspiration from the soul of Amy Winehouse, and the sonic eclecticism of Portishead. Traces of Vulfpeck’s tight, technical funk, and Jimi Hendrix’s fuzz rock grooves can be heard in the band’s sound as well. Front woman Christina Lacoste, guitarist Fran McConville Jr, drummer Josh Cuadra and Bassist Jon Dusoe met in 2010 while performing in a band called The Elements. The four were drawn together by their compatibility as performers, and continued to play music throughout the years.Together, they make up Lady Lupine!
