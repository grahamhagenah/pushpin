Meg Gehman

The Lilypad · Wed, Oct 28 · 5:30pm

$20 admission / 5:30 doors / 6pm start / seated / all ages Meg Gehman brings her soulful voice and original music, a mash-up of soul, blues, folk, and rock, as she promotes the first single, The Dawn, from her forthcoming album, LoveSister.

Meg Gehman is a Nashville-based Americana Soul artist. Her songs are real and intimate; an amalgam of folk, blues, funk, soul and rock. A captivating singer and songwriter, Meg is a warm and engaging performer whose lyrics often pay tribute to the the resiliency of the human spirit and the power of love over fear. While Meg has her own style, comparisons can be drawn to artists such as Bonnie Raitt, Susan Tedeschi, Joan Osborne, Annie Lennox, Rickie Lee Jones or Indigo Girls.
