Bog Fancy Magazine Presents: the HAG ATTACK COMMENCEMENT

The Lilypad · Thu, Oct 29 · 7pm

$10 admission / 7pm doors / 8pm start / all ages Bog Fancy Magazine features their twisted authors reading strange tales to improvised soundscapes by haunted sound artists like Mute City and Death Shepherd. Followed by full sets from hip-hop zombies Z.W.A., coven-wave band Do Whatever Die Whenever, and horror-synth video butcherer NO GRAVE.

Z.W.A is the futuristic variants of Mac and TD (of Big Digits) and Karen (of UV Protection) who find themselves woken up from their eternal graveyard slumber by nuclear bombs. In their undead forms, they have found each other, started making heavy beats and witty rhymes, and decide to go back in time to warn the music appreciators of today about the upcoming apocalypse. Their sound—a fusion of hip-hop horror, dance floor grit, and theatrical flair—is inspired by the likes of Gravediggaz, Blondie's "Rapture," and Michael Jackson's Thriller video. They are the zombie screaming rapping prophets the world never asked for.

Crystal shop loiterers DO WHATEVER DIE WHENEVER are the guitar and synth duo of CC OhNo-Bates and Ulf Rattenhelfer. Their Voices from the Dark EP swerves from dancey dark-wave to punky deathrock, while covering topics of high strangeness, pseudo-esoterism, and philosophical pessimism.

NO GRAVE is a dread-synth multimedia onslaught of electronic scores performed over warped and smeared vintage slasher-flick video-collage.

BOG FANCY is a regular magazine featuring WEIRD STORIES and WEIRD ILLUSTRATIONS, and when performed live it also features WEIRD MUSIC from the area's best soundscapesmiths. Embrace every feeling a person can have, as long as it's SPOOKY, SAVAGE, and STRANGE.
