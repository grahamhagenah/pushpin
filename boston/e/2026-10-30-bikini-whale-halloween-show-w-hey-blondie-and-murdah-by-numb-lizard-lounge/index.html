Bikini Whale Halloween Show w/ Hey Blondie and Murdah By Numbahs!

Lizard Lounge · Fri, Oct 30 · 8pm · $20

Bikini Whale 15th Anniversary Halloween Show with Hey Blondie and Murdah By Numbahs! All Hallows Eve B-52s, Blondie, Police dance party!

$20adv/$25 door · Doors 7

Bikini Whale is Boston’s tribute to innovative and influential party band legends, The B-52s! They’ve been wigging up and playing the B’s most beloved songs to devoted fans around New England since their first show in October 2011.

Hey Blondie! is a Boston based Blondie tribute band, bringing all the attitude and style of this Rock and Roll Hall Of Fame band to the stage. One Way or Another, Hey Blondie! will rock the house and pack the dance floor with a truly Atomic set.

Murdah By Numbahs is Davina Yannetty’s Police tribute. Davina Yannetty is a piano bar entertainer, bass player, and vocalist from East Boston, MA. While she is experienced in performing in a variety of genres, she especially loves the major flex of singing and playing the bass guitar at the same time, just like Sting.
