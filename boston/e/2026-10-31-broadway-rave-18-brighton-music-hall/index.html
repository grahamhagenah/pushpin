Broadway Rave (18+)

Brighton Music Hall · Sat, Oct 31 · 8pm

THIS EVENT IS 18+ WITH A VALID I.D. | DOORS: 8:00 PM | SHOW: 8:30 PM Calling all Musical Theatre nerds, drama geeks, actors, actresses, theatre lovers, and more! Welcome to Broadway Rave: Masque Rave Edition, a Broadway Dance Party celebrating the best in show-tunes and musical theatre, with a spooky twist! Come sing along to all of your favorite Broadway hits while dressed up as your favorite character, Broadway star, or Halloween icon. Get ready for a night of theatrical tricks and treats. Live out your Broadway fantasies at Broadway Rave, now with a Halloween twist! Masks encouraged and drama required. There is a seating area available for those with mobility limitations. If you require accommodation, we encourage you to reach out to information@ as soon as you have purchased your GA ticket.
