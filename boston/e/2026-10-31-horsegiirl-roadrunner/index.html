horsegiirL is with you because Gaia is with her She has trotted on the path ahead of you and left the gate open.

Her guidance is simple: follow your body, feel the beating pulse, Trust the rhythm, and listen to what mother earth has to say Surrender to the cycles that guide animals and humans alike Rest now. Touch the ground. Fall into the sound. Feel your ego slip Let the Earth finish the sentence you started.

With her galvanizing charisma and galloping club tracks, horsegiirL is the first half-horse, half-human to captivate mankind as a star singer/songwriter/DJ/producer. Known for her rallying live sets packed with her irreverent, breakneck hits “My Barn My Rules” and “eat, sleep, slay, repeat” the artist born Stella Stallion has commanded festivals across the globe with her fantastical performances where absurdity and debauchery thrive—breaking open the “natural order” of the animal kingdom and creating spaces for all species to come together.

Now, horsegiirL invites her followers, affectionately known as Farmies, to take the next plunge into communion and self-awakening with her debut album, nature is healing. It sees her stripping back the worldliness of her 2025 acclaimed EP, v.i.p. - very important pony, which was informed by the materialistic, hedonistic life she adopted after relocating to Berlin, as she moves towards a more globally-minded sound that shifts and breathes like the varied landscapes she explored as a young pony growing up in Sunshine Farms. Still rooted in her love for cutting-edge electronics, euphoric house, and hard, fast club beats, nature is healing is horsegiirL’s vision of dance music that inspires true connection instead of empty escapism.

Throughout nature is healing, horsegiirL’s witty, thought-provoking wisdoms are disguised through punchy dance-pop hooks—a literal trojan horse, if you will—as the music traverses from balearic grooves, electroclash, new-age downtempo, and thumping gabber. She invited some of electronic music’s most innovative producers to join her on crafting this sonic journey: A.G. Cook, Nomak (Charli xcx), Casey MQ (Oklou), Margo XS (Kim Petras, Zara Larsson), Elof Loelv (Icona Pop), FREE Jimi (Dorian Electra), Suena (Apache 207), as well as her trusted close collaborator Luvhuntah.
