Concessions

Somerville Theatre · Sun, Nov 1 · 6pm

As the Royal Alamo Cinema closes its doors for good, jaded staff, eccentric patrons, and an unhinged manager stumble through one last night at the movies-grappling with busted dreams, bad popcorn, and hard goodbyes. Stay after the screening for a Q&A with director Maz Bouzidi and actor Jonathan Lorenzo Price!
