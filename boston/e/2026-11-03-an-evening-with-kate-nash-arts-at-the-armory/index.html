An Evening with Kate Nash

Arts at the Armory · Tue, Nov 3 · 7pm

Join Brit Award winner Kate Nash live in concert.

Her upcoming album, Cailín is described as “a love letter to her Irish heritage” and “a raw, raucous album combining traditional Irish sounds with punk energy, nods to hip hop production and Nash’s knack for a killer hook.”

Listeners might just think they dropped into an impromptu pub performance complete with fiddles, flutes, the bodhrán, harp and banjo, along with Nash herself playing the tin whistle.

Doors open at 7:00PM · Show begins at 8:00PM
