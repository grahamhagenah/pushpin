Songs & Stories: Ava Rush, Talia Sporkin, Samantha Kira

The Lilypad · Tue, Nov 3 · 10pm

$10 admission / 10pm doors / 10:30 show / seated & standing Join Samantha Kira, Talia Sporkin, and Ava Rush for a night of heartfelt songwriting, catchy melodies, and genre-blending sounds. Bringing together roots-rock, pop, country, and folk influences, these three singer-songwriters offer unique perspectives on love, heartbreak, growing up, and everything in between. From powerful vocals and energetic instrumentals to intimate storytelling and cheeky lyricism, this is a show full of raw emotion, relatable lyrics, and plenty of personality.
