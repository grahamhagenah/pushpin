“Feel no guilt in your desire:” 90 YEARS Short Film Program

ICA · Thu, Nov 5 · 6pm

Drop in for a looping series of short films in conjunction with 90 YEARS. Each of the following films were presented at the ICA during the 1980s, a dynamic period for video art in Boston and beyond.

Isaac Julien’s This Is Not an AIDS Advertisement is an impressionistic and lush riposte to dominant moralizing and fearmongering advertising languages, and was included in the program Video Against AIDS that screened in the ICA’s theater in 1989.
