Worlds Collide Showcase

Arts at the Armory · Thu, Nov 5 · 7:30pm

An annual concert, competition, and fundraiser centered around cross-genre collaboration.

The Pandora Consort is hosting the fifth annual Worlds Collide Showcase! This concert, competition, and fundraising event focused on cross-genre collaboration brings together artists from different musical backgrounds to perform original songs/covers combining different genres of music (for example: EDM and Baroque, hip hop and jazz, etc).

The Worlds Collide Showcase is meant to facilitate cross-genre musical collaboration in the Greater Boston area, as well as collaboration between musicians and charitable organizations serving Eastern Massachusetts.
