A Night To Be Heard: B.Heard World Gala

Arts at the Armory · Fri, Nov 6 · 7pm

Join us for an unforgettable Night To Be Heard— delicious food, stories, dance, music, and voices from beheard.world's talented artists!

Get Ready for A Night To Be Heard!

Dance. Poetry. Film. Music. Food. Community.

Join the dancers, poets, filmmakers, educators and youth and community members of beheard.world for an evening celebrating the power of art to bring us together—and the voices that deserve to be heard.

Enjoy delicious food, live performance, storytelling, documentary film, and a first look at some of the exciting work beheard.world is creating in communities around the country.

Bring a friend and come be part of the converstion. We look forward to sharing this meaningful and memorable evening with you!

Arts at the Armory serves delicious locally sourced food and drink at the Performance Hall bar when doors open (usually one hour before the show).
