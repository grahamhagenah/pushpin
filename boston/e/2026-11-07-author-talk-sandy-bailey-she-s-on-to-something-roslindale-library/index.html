Author Talk: Sandy Bailey - She's On To Something

Roslindale Library · Sat, Nov 7 · 1pm

Sandy Bailey joins us to discuss her debut novel She’s On To Something. When hard-charging SEC prosecutor Jaye Brannigan stumbles onto a church-based investment scam draining millions from working-class families, she knows the case could make—or end—her career.

Queer, outspoken, and already targeted by a hostile administration, Jaye follows the money from Boston to New Mexico, uncovering a web of religious patriarchy, financial fraud, and offshore secrets. As the danger mounts, so does the temptation to guard her heart—especially when a magnetic woman pulls her toward connection just as everything else threatens to unravel.

Sandy will appear in conversation with her wife, Liz Nania, the inspiration for the character of Rose. This program is presented in partnership with Rozzie Bound. This program is presented in partnership with Rozzie Bound. Copies of the book will be available to purchase at the event.

Sandy Bailey has been a freight and passenger train engineer, a carpenter, and a prosecutor before building a career investigating white-collar crime in New Mexico, Boston, and Washington, D.C. Her experience uncovering financial fraud informs the high-stakes world of her fiction. She lives in Boston, where she organizes community events, explores local trails, plays basketball with her lesbian hoops squad, and shares a love of queer dance with her wife.
