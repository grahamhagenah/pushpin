Collective Measures

The Burren · Sat, Nov 7 · 1:30pm · $19.95

Collective Measures is a Boston-based, semi-professional, all gender a cappella group working tirelessly to add their own flavor to the thriving local a cappella scene. They pride themselves on creating challenging arrangements of music ranging from Jukebox the Ghost, to the Steely Dan, to Maroon 5. They have a weakness for killer mashups, and you can be sure to hear a few when you catch them performing at different venues around Massachusetts. Made up of 13 members with a variety of day jobs—and even more diverse hobbies—the group is united by their love of making music. Collective Measures is a founding member of the Contemporary A Cappella League (CAL).
