VOILÀ Presents: Moonbloom

Royale · Sun, Nov 8 · 8pm · 18+

VOILÀ has partnered with PLUS1 so that $1 per ticket goes to Solving Kids' Cancer and their work finding, funding, and advocating for breakthrough treatment options to cure children with the most fatal childhood cancers. $.25 from each ticket purchased will go to The Shout Syndicate, a Boston-based, volunteer-run fundraising effort who raises money to help fund youth-led arts programs at proven non-profit creative youth development organizations in Greater Boston. Housed at The Boston Foundation, The Shout Syndicate works in partnership with the Mayor's Office of Arts & Culture's creative plan, Boston Creates.
