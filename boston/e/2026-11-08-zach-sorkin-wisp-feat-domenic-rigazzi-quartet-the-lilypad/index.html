Zach Sorkin "Wisp" feat. Domenic Rigazzi Quartet

The Lilypad · Sun, Nov 8 · 10pm

$15 admission / 10pm doors / 10:30 show / seated & standing Drummer/composer Zach Sorkin presents "Wisp", a new Quartet featuring Joaquin Valdebenito on Tenor Saxophone, Devin Thai on Piano, and Andrea Amato on bass. The group will premiere lots of Sorkin's new original music, with an opening set from Domenic Rigazzi and his quartet, featuring Andrew Fogel, Albin Sundstrom, and Sam Cruz.
