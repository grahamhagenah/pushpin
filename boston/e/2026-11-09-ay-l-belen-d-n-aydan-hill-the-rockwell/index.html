AYĖL, Belen, DÀN, & Aydan Hill

The Rockwell · Mon, Nov 9 · 7pm · $12–$20

AYĖL, Belen, DÀN, & Aydan Hill Join us at The Rockwell for an evening of intimate songwriting, cinematic soundscapes, and genre-blending performances. Featuring AYEL, Belen, DÁN, and Aydan Hill, the night brings together four independent artists with distinct musical backgrounds and perspectives, moving between folk, pop, alternative, and cinematic influences. Through personal songwriting, expressive vocals, and immersive arrangements, each artist offers a different take on identity, vulnerability, and human connection.

AYEL is a French singer-songwriter, producer, pianist, and film composer based in Boston. Her sound blends pop, R&B, soul, rock, electronic, and cinematic influences, bringing together intimate songwriting and expansive arrangements. Currently studying Film Scoring at Berklee College of Music, she moves between writing for her own project, collaborating with other artists, and composing for film. Her music explores vulnerability, self-expression, and the search for connection, with the intention of making listeners feel seen, heard, and less alone. Her recent work includes the release “Better” and the placement of “The Story Never Ends” in All American.

Belen is a francophone German-Filipino-American folk-adjacent singer-songwriter and multidisciplinary artist based in Boston. Known for her acoustic textures and tender songwriting, she draws on her multicultural upbringing and visual-art background to create music rooted in vulnerability and emotional storytelling. Born in Berkeley, California, and raised between a rural village in southern France and Germany, she brings a distinctive perspective to her work. She taught herself guitar at 16 before moving to Paris to study music, and now attends Berklee College of Music on a full-tuition scholarship. Her cover of “Ma Meilleure Ennemie” from Netflix’s Arcane reached over three million views across platforms, and she is currently preparing her upcoming EP, From Above.

DÁN is an Italian songwriter, vocalist, producer, and composer based in Boston. Originally from northern Italy, his musical journey has taken him from festivals and live performance to Berklee College of Music, where he expanded his craft in music production, recording, and engineering.
