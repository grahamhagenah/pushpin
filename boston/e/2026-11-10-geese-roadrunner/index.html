Geese

Roadrunner · Tue, Nov 10 · 8pm · All ages

Ripping up the expected blueprint Geese set with their 2021 debut Projector and the cowboy rock follow-up of 2023’s 3D Country, Getting Killed puts the very idea of conventional rock structures in the band’s crosshairs. Working at a feverish clip with renowned producer Kenneth Blume over a month-long stretch, Geese emerged wielding a sharp, novel new vision for the genre, sporting wry smiles and a resolute confidence. The album was crowned the number one album of the year on numerous Best of 2025 lists including TIME, The New Yorker, The New York Times and more. The band began 2026 right where they left off, with an unwieldy, vexing SNL debut, a surprisingly intimate Tiny Desk concert and winning a BRIT Award for International Group of the Year.
