KSJ + STS Fall Lecture - The Fight for Facts: Evidence in an Age of Doubt

MIT · Tue, Nov 10 · 4:30pm

The Program in Science, Technology, and Society and the Knight Science Journalism Program invite you on Tuesday, November 10th at 4:30 pm in the Nexus at Hayden Library (14S-130) for a special lecture, "The Fight for Facts: Evidence in an Age of Doubt," that explores the rise of evidence-based thinking, the power of scientific consensus, and the forces that work to distort, undermine, and dismiss facts.

Helen Pearson, KJS Fellow, Senior Editor at Nature, and Author of “Beyond Belief.”

Naomi Oreskes, Professor of the history of science at Harvard University and author of “Why Trust Science?”

Lecture: 4:30 – 5:45 pm
