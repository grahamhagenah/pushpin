Boston T Party

The Rockwell · Wed, Nov 11 · 6pm · $10–$25 · 18+

The Boston T Party is a celebration of queer joy and pushing the boundaries of gender norms. This one night only extravaganza features local drag, music and dance centered around inclusivity and inspiring authenticity. Look fierce, grab your girlfriends and join us at the Rockwell for a show you won’t forget.

PERFORMERS: Olivia Gullick, Fran Darling, Mad Jupiter, Sariyah Glamour & Nikko The Nymph.

@olliegullick — Wearing his jet black 6-inch gogo boots (and not much else), Ollie Gullick is carving out his own world in the long tradition of boundary-pushing queer artists through provocative performances, conversational songwriting and devilish charm.

@mad_jupiter_music — Mad Jupiter is a trans bassist and artist based in Boston, MA. He writes alt neo soul and rock infused music for frogs and fairies that primarily explores themes of identity, existentialism and whimsy. With his music, he creates a space where people feel comfortable to be freaks with other freaks. After all, we are the best version of ourselves when we embrace the ugly.

@itsfrandarling — Fran Darling (Kayla) is the girl you meet in the bathroom of the club. She hypes you up and tells you about the toilet paper on the bottom of your shoe. Her artistic vision is a haze filled with glitter, cheetah print, and auto tune. A hyper-pop drag artist, Fran Darling writes and produces herself, exploring themes of what goes on in the party scene: love, lust, and loss, all mish moshing together into one big party. She is looking to find her girls that are ready to dance, laugh, and cry on the dance floor!

@sariyah_yarbo — SARIYAH YARBO‘S MISSION IS TO PERFORM, INSPIRE. From a galaxy far✨️this intergalactic Entertainer will leave you glamourized. Take a sip and enjoy the Glamoůr!

@nikkothenymph — Nikko the Nymph is a they/them, fey/femme who can often be spotted shaking their ass and shedding clothes around New England. Known for their high energy and camp, this drag performer is always serving up something unconventionally unexpected!
