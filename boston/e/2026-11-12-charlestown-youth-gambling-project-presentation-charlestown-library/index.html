Charlestown Youth Gambling Project Presentation

Charlestown Library · Thu, Nov 12 · 6pm

Join the Mayor’s Office of Human Services and the research team behind the Charlestown Gambling Project for an in-depth presentation on their latest findings. This session offers a direct look at the data, economic analysis, and community impacts detailed in the newly released report.
