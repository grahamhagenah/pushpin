Jake Klar with Noble Dust & Hayley Sabella

The Rockwell · Thu, Nov 12 · 7pm · All ages

Join us at The Rockwell for a curated evening of Folk Rock and Alt-Country, celebrating Jake Klar’s album release, with special guests Noble Dust and Hayley Sabella.

Years Go Through Me Now, a new collaborative album by singer-songwriter Jake Klar and folk-rock trio Blue Ranger, is a quiet triumph of such glimpses. In its nine songs and two interludes, I hear the motions of weathered hands, the gentle rush of country roads, the laughter of old friends and the silence of rumination — the quilt of dreams and doings we call a life.

GoldFlakePaint describes Klar’s music as “An urgent meeting of folk, roots and good old rock and roll that carries with it an unbreakable spirit.” A fixture in New England’s folk scene, Klar has toured nationally, played festivals including Otis Mountain Get Down and FreshGrass, and supported the likes of Cut Worms, Antwaun Stanley, and Al Olender.

Klar has spent the past decade working as a letterpress printer, art store clerk, bartender, carpenter, illustrator, graphic designer, musician (touring DIY on his own and as a member of Prewn and Hannah Mohan), live sound engineer, merch guy, and custodian. His songwriting, from the misty-eyed title track to the down-home boogie-woogie of “Driving Home Through the Country” (here recast murmurously from an earlier electric version), dwells in a rustic realm borne by his crackling voice and hearthside acoustic guitar. “That Forever” is a haunted, fragmented road-movie that emerges from its dust like a lost Basement Tapes recording, while the last-call piano ballad “Louisa” wouldn’t sound amiss on After The Gold Rush.
