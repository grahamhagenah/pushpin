JoJo

Royale · Fri, Nov 13 · 7pm · 18+

JoJo is one of the most accomplished and respected vocalists at the intersection of pop and R&B, celebrated for her powerhouse voice and remarkable longevity. Breaking through as a teenager, she made history with chart-topping hits like “Leave (Get Out),” “Too Little Too Late,” “Baby It’s You,” and “Disaster,” becoming the youngest solo artist ever to reach #1 on the Billboard Mainstream Top 40 chart.

Over the course of her career, JoJo has earned multi-platinum sales, billions of global streams, and critical acclaim, while influencing the next generation of singers with her unmistakable voice and fearless artistry. As an independent artist, she has continued to evolve creatively, starting her own label, Clover Music, and paving the way for other's to re-record their albums and own those masters. She has collaborated with an eclectic range of artists across genres, earning a reputation as one of the most versatile vocalists and writers.

A true multi-hyphenate, JoJo is also a New York Times bestselling author, having shared her (unlikely and imperfect) road to success on her own terms with remarkable candor in her memoir Over the Influence. She acted in cult-classic movies, "Aquamarine" and "RV". And recently, her lifelong dream come true as she expanded her acting into Broadway. She starred as Satine in the Broadway production of Moulin Rouge! The Musical and the originated the leading role of Tess McGill in the world-premiere musical adaptation of Working Girl, featuring an original score by Cyndi Lauper.

Currently, she is working on her first full-length album in six years, ushering in a new chapter that reflects both her artistic evolution and enduring commitment to growth. She has been enjoying using her social media platform to share what she knows about the music industry and to hopefully serve the next generation on their own journey.
