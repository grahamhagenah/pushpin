Risa Lavelle, Album Release Show

The Burren · Fri, Nov 13 · 7pm · $29.95

Risa Lavelle celebrates her first album, Let My Heart Lead at The Burren. Risa is an emerging singer-songwriter crafting a mesmerizing sonic tapestry that bridges vintage Americana nostalgia with contemporary depth. Lavelle writes music that feels lived-in and unguarded, shaping raw, honest melodies and textured soundscapes that draw listeners closer and offer a sense of shared experience.

Lavelle will be backed by her band and additional musicians featured on the album, including Mickey Roache on pedal steel, mandolin, and guitar; Tom Murray on drums and vocals; Ed Newton on bass and guitar; Robert Sevigny on guitar and vocals; Greg Hurren on bass; Bob Adams and Neil Kay on saxophone.
