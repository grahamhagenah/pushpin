FFO: Kimbra, Sungazer, Snarky Puppy, KNOWER, Genevieve Artadi, Hiatus Kaiyote Praised for her depth and dimension, Sirintip creates at the intersection of avant-pop, jazz, and electronic music, combining songwriting and improvisation with electronics, technology, and an ongoing curiosity about the natural world. Her interdisciplinary work expands music beyond the stage through collaborations with scientists, visual artists, and dancers, exploring environmental sustainability and new ways of turning research and data into sound and performance. Her previous albums include Carbon (Ropeadope), named one of DownBeat’s Best Albums of 2022, and Tribus (GroundUP Music), produced by Snarky Puppy’s Michael League and featuring Mark Guiliana. She has toured internationally as a bandleader and alongside Snarky Puppy and Sungazer, with appearances at SFJAZZ, the Nobel Museum, Joe’s Pub, National Sawdust, and Winter Jazzfest.
