A World of 3 Zeros

West Newton Cinema · Sat, Nov 14 · 4pm

Join us on November 14th to watch A World of 3 Zeros and stay after for a Zoom Q&A with the Co-Directors, Áine Clark and Michel Van Der Veken.

Poverty, unemployment, and carbon emissions are global problems. A World of 3 Zeros explores the ability of social entrepreneurship to address these issues and bring them to zero. This documentary takes the audience on the journey undertaken by filmmakers Áine and Michel, to shed light on the works of this new type of business. A concept purposely designed to address social and environmental problems with entrepreneurs who joyfully aim for impact over profit maximization, with solutions that can be shared or adopted elsewhere in the world for the common good.

ÁINE CLARKE (born 1963, Belfast, Northern Ireland) Graduated from the University of Ulster with a degree in Environmental Physics in 1987. Until 2016, Áine served as the commercial and marketing business developer for Azimut Company, building client relationships and managing business growth. Her passion for listening, and commitment to authentic representation though storytelling drives her approach to documentary filmmaking.

MICHEL VAN DER VEKEN (born 1961, Liège, Belgium) Graduate of L'IAD Film Institution (1985). Michel brings technical precision and creative vision developed through 25 years of commercial filmmaking to documentary work. His cinematography reveals the humanity in each subject while creating visual cohesion across diverse global settings.
