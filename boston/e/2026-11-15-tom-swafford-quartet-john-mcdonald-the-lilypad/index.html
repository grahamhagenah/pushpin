Tom Swafford Quartet / John McDonald

The Lilypad · Sun, Nov 15 · 3pm

$15 admission / 3pm doors / 3:30 show / seated / all ages Composer/violinist Tom Swafford presents a set of his solo piano compositions performed by John McDonald. The second set will feature Tom, violin, Brian Price, clarinet and saxophone, Nate McBride, bass, and Curt Newton, drums, performing improvisations based on selected themes and motives from those pieces. The concert will include a set of piano variations on Joann’s Green Satin Dress by Butch Morris, which the improvisors will interpret as well.

Tom played in series of a large ensemble performances led by the esteemed conductor and composer Butch Morris in the fall of 2011. In that series, Morris incorporated his notated works into his practice of Conduction™ (conducted improvisation). This is an inspiration for today’s concert.

John McDonald recently retired after 36 years as composition professor at Tufts University. John was a great inspiration for Tom, who studied with him at Tufts from 1991-1995. In addition to composition instruction, John co-led the NME (New Music Ensemble), which was Tom’s first exposure to free improvisation. Tom has been involved in free improv along with his fully notated composition work since that time.

Prior to Tufts, Tom studied jazz piano and played in his high school jazz ensemble in Seattle. The jazz tradition continues to be an important factor in his compositional approach. Another fun fact is that Tom and Nate McBride played in the band in a Seattle Children’s Theatre production in the late 1980s, when Tom was in 8th grade and Nate was in high school!

Tom is excited to once again collaborate with John, Nate, and this group of excellent Boston area improvisors.
