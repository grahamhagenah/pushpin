Ms. Ezra Furman Doing What She Wants

The Rockwell · Tue, Nov 17 · 7pm · $12–$25 · All ages

Ms. Ezra Furman Doing What She Wants SHOW DESCRIPTION: Ezra Furman will perform music, poetry, or anything she wishes, with or without other performers, depending on how she feels.

Ezra Furman is a critically acclaimed American singer, songwriter, and author known for her raw, folk-punk style and vulnerable lyricism exploring queer identity, anxiety, and religion. She gained widespread recognition for composing the original soundtrack for the hit Netflix series Sex Education.

Mairead Guy (they/them) and Maddy Simpson (she/her) met at Berklee College of Music in 2018, bonding over a shared love of Simon & Garfunkel and Gillian Welch & Dave Rawlings during a rehearsal delay. That connection grew into Sweet Petunia, a duo now twice nominated for Best Folk Act by the Boston Music Awards and a performer at festivals like Between Days and Nice, a Fest.

Sweet Petunia’s debut LP, Foggy Mountain Mental Breakdown, arrives March 13th via Righteous Babe Records. The album explores breakups, outgrown friendships, and self-discovery, with a title that nods to Flatt & Scruggs’ classic bluegrass tune. It blends the traditional folk that shaped the duo with the punk and DIY influences of their local scene.
