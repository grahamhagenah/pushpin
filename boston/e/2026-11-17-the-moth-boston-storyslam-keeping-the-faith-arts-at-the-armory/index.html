The Moth: Boston StorySLAM: KEEPING THE FAITH

Arts at the Armory · Tue, Nov 17 · 6:30pm

Join us the fourth Tuesday of each month for the Boston Moth StorySLAM! This community-focused, open-mic storytelling competition is open to anyone can share a story on the night’s theme (changes every month).

Tonight’s theme is… Keeping the Faith: five-minute stories about that small, still, inner voice. Tell us about a time when you believed without seeing, threw a Hail Mary pass, or were surprised by a stranger’s kindness. Small miracles, deep rituals, storms weathered, and courses stayed. Were your beliefs sharpened or shaken along the way?
