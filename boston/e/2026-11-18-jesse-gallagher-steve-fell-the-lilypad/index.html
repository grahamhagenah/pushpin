Jesse Gallagher + Steve Fell

The Lilypad · Wed, Nov 18 · 7pm

$10 admission / 7pm / seated show Jesse Gallagher began living above the Lilypad in 2006, just a few blocks from where his grandfather grew up on Inman st. At the time Jesse was in the middle of what would be about a decade of regular touring, performing & recording with his psychedelic rock band Apollo Sunshine. The band was featured in the New York Times, Rolling Stone, Spin & shared the stage with legendary acts Wu-Tang Clan, Toots & The Maytals, The Flaming Lips & Dr. Dog. Jesse has been involved with the Lilypad for 20 years and the full time talent booking agent since 2013.

Breaking Bad used Jesse’s song “We are Born When We Die” (2008) to dramatic effect. “Today is the Day” (2005) was in international Nike commercials while his “Singing to the Earth to Thank Her For You” was used in a large GAP campaign. Gallagher has collaborated, recorded & performed extensively w/ Sam Cohen, Willy Mason, Lilys, Nick Nicely, Doug Tuttle & Edan. Gallagher is a certified Yoga & Meditation teacher and hosts classes at the Lilypad & online. Since 2018, he has released 40 pieces of instrumental music which have been used in millions of YouTube videos ranging from NBC NY to Tesla and most recently a string of viral videos by Emma Chamberlain. Gallagher has a very active YouTube channel, making lots of psychedelic music videos and building a community of mindful listeners.

Steve Fell is a guitarist, composer, and educator whose performing and recording career over the last twenty five years has reached across the boundaries of jazz, experimental, funk, free form improvisation, rock, electronic, reggae/calypso, hip-hop, psych, and many others.

He co-leads the groups Clear Audience, Quantum Banditos, Dome Room, and Astronomico, leads a trio under his own name, and collaborates with numerous bands and artists.
