GKit & Magnificent Danger

The Rockwell · Thu, Nov 19 · 7pm · $12–$15 · All ages

SHOW DESCRIPTION: Join us for a night of reimagined arrangements of both classic and underrated video game music, brought to you by fusion big band GKit and renowned brass band Magnificent Danger.

GKit is an up-and-coming fusion big band that plays reimagined arrangements of both iconic and niche video game tunes. They take inspiration from jazz, latin, funk, rock and many other styles to breathe new life into game music, joining the movement of bringing video games into the live music world with a progressive and creative touch.

Magnificent Danger is an energetic collective of brass and percussion dishing out funky mashups of iconic tunes from your favorite video games and anime. The band’s eclectic fusion of big band, New Orleans second line, riot jazz, funk, and hip-hop creates a bizarre but danceable party appreciated by musicians and nerds alike.
