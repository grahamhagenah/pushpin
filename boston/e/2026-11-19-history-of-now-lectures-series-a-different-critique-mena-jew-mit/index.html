MIT · Thu, Nov 19 · 5pm · Free

History at MIT invites you to join us on Thursday, November 19th at 5 pm in E51-095 for the final lecture in the History of Now series, featuring Orit Bashkin, the Mabel Greene Myers Professor of Modern Middle Eastern History and Director of Graduate Studies, Modern Fields at the University of Chicago. Her talk is titled "A Different Critique: MENA Jewish Approaches to Zionism and Imperialism, 1830-1948."

Orit Bashkin is a historian who works on the intellectual, social, and cultural history of the modern Middle East. She got her Ph.D. from Princeton University (2004), writing a thesis on Iraqi intellectual history under the supervision of Professors Robert Tignor and Samah Selim, and her BA (1995) and MA (1999) from Tel Aviv University. Since her graduation, she has been working as a professor of modern Middle Eastern history in the Department of Near Eastern Languages and Civilizations at the University of Chicago. Her publications deal with Iraqi history, the history of Iraqi Jews, the Arab cultural revival movement (the nahda) in the late 19th century, and the connections between modern Arab history and Arabic literature. Her current research project explores the lives of Iraqi Jews in Israel.
