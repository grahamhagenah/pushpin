Lost Rivers: Mapping the History of Charlestown's Miller’s River

Charlestown Library · Thu, Nov 19 · 6pm

Where were the original rivers in the Boston area? When and why did Miller’s River, once located near Charlestown, disappear? Join the Leventhal Map & Education Center for a deep dive into the history and geography of Miller’s River. Come learn about the river, the history of other bodies of water in Charlestown, and the urban transformations that contributed to their disappearance.
