The Mail emerged from the vibrant music scene of Boston in 2016, born out of a shared passion for sonic experimentation and storytelling. Composed of members Jeremy Van Cleave, Joe Campbell, Nathaniel Diamond-Jones, Matt Hines, and Curtis Hartshorn, this ensemble quickly made waves with their distinct blend of folk rock and alternative influences.

Driven by a mutual desire to carve out their own musical niche, The Mail’s sound is characterized by Jeremy’s introspective lyrics, Curtis’s unique drumming, Matt’s creative chord voicings, Joe’s deep bass pocket, and Nathaniel’s distinctive approach to fiddle. Together, they create a sonic landscape that is both emotive and immersive, weaving together layers of sound to evoke a range of emotions.

Since their formation, The Mail has been a fixture in Boston’s music scene, captivating audiences with their electrifying live performances and dynamic stage presence. Their debut album, “Lights On,” released in 2018, received widespread acclaim for its poignant lyricism and infectious melodies.

With each release, The Mail continues to push the boundaries of alternative music, exploring new sonic territories while staying true to their roots. As they embark on their musical journey, The Mail invites listeners to join them on a voyage of self-discovery and sonic exploration.

Wood & Bone is an Americana roots band based in Massachusetts, led by singer-songwriter Jesse Joseph. The band features a rotating cast of collaborators, with core members Joe Wilkins (guitar, mandolin, vocals), Dennis Monagle (drums, vocals), and Dylan Sullivan (bass, vocals). Their sound is driven by soulful vocals, earthy grooves with improvisational energy. They play an ever evolving mix of folk, soul, blues.

Objects in Motion is a Boston-based original indie group reinterpreting early 90s shoegaze into something darker, lusher, and appropriately reflective of modern chaos.

Formed in the summer of 2024, Objects in Motion blends social commentary, lush textural soundscapes, and alt-rock sensibilities to create music that feels both independent and thoroughly listenable.
