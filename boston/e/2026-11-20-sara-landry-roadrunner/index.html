Sara Landry

Roadrunner · Fri, Nov 20 · 8pm · 18+

Sara Landry is a DJ, producer and record label head who transforms hard dance into high-pressure, transcendent experiences through her driving, divinely feminine lens.

More than just an artist, Landry is a cultural architect operating at the edges of hard dance. Her work is built on physical, emotional and sonic pressure, resulting in a sound that favors intensity and demands attention. As one of the most in-demand names in music, her boundary-breaking vision has transformed the scene, pushing hard dance beyond genre into an authored vision. Landry’s productions are not designed to decorate a room, rather, to transform it with structured intention, rupture and momentum.

After emerging from the underground with her viral breakout 2023 Boiler Room set – which remains one of the highest streamed Boiler Room of the past two years with over 10 million views – the multi-hyphenate has amassed a devoted, cult-like following as she continues to defy convention with her unadulterated ferocity and indisputable originality. Taking her artistry to unprecedented levels, Landry’s live concepts – ETERNALISM, her high-octane physical manifestation of communion and catharsis; and BLOOD OATH, her trailblazing set concept, carefully curated as a testament to her affinity for the unconventional and her uncompromised commitment to fostering the often overlooked female community in electronic music – offer audiences one-of-a-kind experiences as she transforms performance into a collective act, entwining sound with ritual, restraint and release. Her sets are fully immersive rituals, built to be shared, endured and remembered. With a commanding presence and a raw power that’s impossible to ignore, Landry has solidified herself as a mainstay on some of the world’s most influential stages including Coachella, Tomorrowland, EDC Las Vegas, Creamfields and more. Beyond her own output, the multi-hyphenate remains fiercely committed to creating spaces for artists she believes in through her label, HEKATE, where she pushes past the confines of traditional power structures and uplifts artists in the category.
