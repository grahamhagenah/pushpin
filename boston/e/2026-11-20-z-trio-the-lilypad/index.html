Z trio

The Lilypad · Fri, Nov 20 · 5:15pm

$10 admission / 5:15 doors / 5:30 start / seated / all ages This show brings some of local artist Z's previously released and upcoming album music to the stage in the form of a trio (piano, upright bass, and drums) in a rich, jazz-inflected trio arrangement.

Z is a local singer songwriter, in a variety of styles and languages. She will be playing with Boston-based Jazz musicians Jon Dreyer (double bass) and Gary Fieldman (drums).
