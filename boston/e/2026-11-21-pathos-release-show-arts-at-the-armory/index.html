Pathos Release Show

Arts at the Armory · Sat, Nov 21 · 3pm

An afternoon with Jason Oberstein and special guest Hilltop Sunset.

Pathos represents a sampling of songs I've been working on and emotions I've been working through over the past couple years. These songs mean a lot to me, and I'm stoked to put them out there in album form and even more excited to share them live with you all! You can expect to hear the album in full, plus a couple surprises:) My friend Hilltop Sunset will open up the afternoon with some of his amazing songs. Come hang, snag a scone from the cafe, and listen to some heartfelt music!
