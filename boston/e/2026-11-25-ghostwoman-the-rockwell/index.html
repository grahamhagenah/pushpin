GHOSTWOMAN

The Rockwell · Wed, Nov 25 · 7pm · $17–$22

GHOSTWOMAN itself only exists because Uschenko and van Dessel were the ones “stupid enough to commit to it”. Conceived by Uschenko after cutting his teeth as a touring multi-instrumentalist, the project was brought to life in an abandoned farmhouse in Diamond City, Alberta. There, for two months, he would make the self-released, self-titled debut album which was produced and dubbed directly to custom-made cassette tapes. The music has always been beautifully inhospitable, earned only by those curious enough to seek it out. — Dine Alone Records Presented by Get To The Gig & MassConcerts Ages: Show age differs by the day.
