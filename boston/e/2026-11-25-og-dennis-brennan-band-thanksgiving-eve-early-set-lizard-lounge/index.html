Lizard Lounge is pleased to welcome back the classic Dennis Brennan Band feat Duke Levine, Kevin Barry, Richard Gates and Billy Beard for a very special Thanksgiving kick off!

Dennis Brennan could be considered Boston’s own Leonard Cohen or Elvis Costello. His songwriting is respected by critics and musicians alike. Not only adept with the pen, but Dennis Brennan is also a highly accomplished performer known for his ability to easily span musical genres, styles, and eras. His emotive and passionate voice is comfortable tackling classic country and R&B as well as classic jazz, rock, and blues.
