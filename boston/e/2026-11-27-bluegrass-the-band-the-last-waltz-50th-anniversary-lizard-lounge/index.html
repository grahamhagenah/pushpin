Bluegrass: The Band, The Last Waltz 50th Anniversary

Lizard Lounge · Fri, Nov 27 · 8pm · $20

Bluegrass The Band presents "The Grass'd Waltz" a bluegrass tribute to the Band's legendary "Last Waltz" Nov 27th.

$20 adv/$25 door · Doors 7

It started as a concert, it became a celebration.

In honor of the 50th anniversary of The Band’s famous 1976 farewell concert, The Last Waltz, Boston’s own “Bluegrass The Band” will be reuniting, throwing “The Grass’d Waltz”. Putting a bluegrass spin on some of the best music to come out of the 1970’s, the members of Bluegrass The Band—joined by some of New England’s finest musicians—will perform an unforgettable night of music. From classic songs such as “The Weight” and “The Night They Drove Old Dixie Down”, to deeper cuts like “Caledonia Mission” and “Don’t Do It”, this is one night not to be missed. Friday Nov 27th, doors at 7pm, show at 8pm at the Lizard Lounge in Cambridge MA. Grab those tickets today!
