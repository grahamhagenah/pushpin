Author Talk: Carlton Champion - Notes to My Father

Adams Street Library · Wed, Dec 2 · 6pm

Join Boston author Carlton Champian for a reading and conversation centered on Notes to My Father, a personal collection exploring fatherhood, absence, grief, and healing. Participants can expect heartfelt storytelling, space for reflection and discussion, and an opportunity to write a letter of their own. Sharing is optional.

Carlton Champian is a Boston-based author, storyteller, and founder of Champian Studios. His book, Notes to My Father, explores his relationship with his father and his journey toward healing. Through readings and conversation, he invites others to reflect on their own stories.
