Take a stroll down the bong-lined sidewalks of Duke Levine’s musical youth. His all-star band is gonna help him travel back to a time when The Lord’s Prayer, Strauss, Isaac Hayes and Charlie Daniels were all on the Top 40. Instrumental arrangements of a bunch of tunes you know, a couple you maybe never heard, and a few you may have wanted to forget.

Kevin Barry – Guitar & Lap Steel Mike Rivard – Bass Paul Schultheis – Keys Yahuba Garcia-Torres – Congas & Percussiom Dean Johnston – Drums
