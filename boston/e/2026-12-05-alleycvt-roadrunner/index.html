ALLEYCVT

Roadrunner · Sat, Dec 5 · 8pm · 18+

Since debuting her project in 2021, ALLEYCVT’s quick rise to stardom can be attributed to the rare trifecta of talent she boasts as a skilled producer, singer/songwriter and DJ. Her alluring pop-adjacent vocals blend seamlessly with her heavy bass-filled drops, cementing her as a force to be reckoned with as she continues her meteoric rise. In a short span, she’s quickly found home on tastemaker labels like Deadbeats, Wubaholics and independent releases on her own label, garnering over 7 million streams across her catalog on Spotify alone. With influences like Flux Pavillion, Zeds Dead, and Subtronics, ALLEYCVT’s brilliance will endure through her siren-like melodies and captivating lyrics.
