The Nightflies

The Burren · Sat, Dec 5 · 7pm · $29.95

New selections, first-time by us, from Gaucho, The Nightfly, The Royal Scam, Katy Lied..and more.

A 10-piece band from the Boston/Merrimack Valley area that began as an informal get-together, for the love and challenge of playing the intricate arrangements of Donald Fagen and Walter Becker. Our name is a tip-of-the-hat to the 1982 standard-bearer Donald Fagen solo album,The Nightfly.

The Nightflies perform an ambitious medley of those recognizable, danceable, foot-tapping, 70's-80's hits and plenty of Becker/Fagen's deep-cuts. Hear faves from...Can't Buy a Thrill, Countdown To Ecstasy, Pretzel Logic, Katy Lied, The Royal Scam, Aja, Gaucho, and last but not least Fagen's solo effort, The Nightfly. The Nightflies are available for venues, private parties, and corporate events.
