Country 102.5's Acoustic Christmas

Roadrunner · Mon, Dec 7 · 8pm · All ages

Join Country 102.5 for a special acoustic evening featuring country superstars Cole Swindell, Scotty McCreery, Chase Matthew, and Jackson Dean - December 7 at Roadrunner in Boston. Experience intimate performances, unforgettable stories, and stripped-down sets from some of country music's biggest stars in a unique setting at one of Boston's best concert venues. A portion of ticket proceeds will benefit Christmas in the City, a nonprofit organization dedicated to helping Boston-area families experiencing homelessness and poverty. Through year-round programs and holiday initiatives, the organization works to ensure that every child can experience the magic of the season, regardless of their family's financial circumstances. Come together with Country 102.5 for an unforgettable night of music, community, and giving back. Enjoy performances from your favorite country artists while helping make a meaningful difference for local families this holiday season.
