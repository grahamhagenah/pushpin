Homeboy Sandman, NAHreally, Loman, & Jesse The Tree

The Rockwell · Wed, Dec 9 · 7pm · $20–$25 · All ages

Homeboy Sandman, NAHreally, Loman, & Jesse The Tree NYC indie rap legend Homeboy Sandman is joined by NAHreally, Loman, and Jesse The Tree for a night of hip-hop at The Rockwell.

Homeboy Sandman is a Hip-Hop artist from Queens, NY. He is highly revered for his thought-provoking lyrics, impeccable flow, and unmatched authenticity. Over his long-standing career, he has received critical praise from Rolling Stone, Associated Press, Pitchfork, The FADER, XXL Magazine, Complex, NPR, and more. Homeboy Sandman has released albums with notable record labels such as Stones Throw, Rhymesayers, and Mello Music Group, as well as his own imprint Dirty Looks. In addition to his accomplished solo career, Homeboy Sandman is also 1/2 of the legendary Hip-Hop duo Lice alongside Aesop Rock.

NAHreally is a Jersey City-based rapper, beatmaker, and show organizer from Massachusetts. Known for his conversational lyrics and laid back production, he’s steadily built a catalog of 10+ releases since 2016. In that same span, his DIY approach to performing, touring, and putting on shows has made him a fixture in the NYC indie rap scene. His music has been covered by Cabbages, Bandcamp Daily, Hearing Things, Okayplayer, and more. His latest album Extra Cheese is out now on Bandcamp via Three Dollar Pistol.

Jesse The Tree is a rapper and producer from Providence, RI. His clever, layered lyricism and introspective storytelling offer a balance of poetry and relatability rarely found in an MC. Tree has built a name around New England and beyond through a steady stream of independent releases and relentless touring. His solo catalog alongside his work as 1/2 of sleepingdogs with rapper/producer Andrew has turned him into a mainstay in indie rap. His music has been covered by Bandcamp Daily, The Rap Music Plug Podcast, Secret House Against, and more. His latest releases Worm in Heaven (Strange Famous) and Dog Complex (Three Dollar Pistol) offer a glimpse into his versatility and craftsmanship both on the mic and behind the boards.
