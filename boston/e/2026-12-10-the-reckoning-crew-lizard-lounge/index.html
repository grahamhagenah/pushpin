The Reckoning Crew

Lizard Lounge · Thu, Dec 10 · 8pm · $15

The Reckoning Crew returns to the Lizard Lounge December 10th at 8 o’clock!

$15/20 door · Doors 7

The Reckoning Crew brings together stalwart members of the Boston Grateful Dead scene to present an authentic emulation of the Dead’s iconic acoustic performances captured on the “Reckoning” album. Not limited to just this period of the Dead’s career, The Reckoning Crew, featuring David Rubin & Ian Brenckle from Diamond Blues, Mark Munzer of Playing Dead, Mike Verge from Torn and Frayed and Ben DeUrso of Citizens’ Dead, has a deep love for all of the material of the storied songbook, and showcases that along with a fierce focus on improvisation & communication for a real good time!
