LET'S ROCK CANCER! 2026

Crystal Ballroom · Fri, Dec 11 · 6:30pm

LET'S ROCK CANCER! 2026 Dedicated to Linda Pardee Hosted by the one and only Henry Santoro Featuring Live Music by Jeffery Robbins of Orbit - with celebration of Linda Pardee (bass & vox for Orbit, Chelsea Curve) Muck & the Mires The Ray Liriano Experience...GO! and special guests Proceeds from this all ages event benefit the Cancer Care Access Program at Dana-Farber, which aims to reduce disparities in cancer outcomes among local communities. Established in 2012, the Program broadens access to historically marginalized patient populations and joins our community partners in the pursuit of equitable care across the spectrum of cancer-related diseases.
