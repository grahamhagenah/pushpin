JGB Tribute, The Diamond Blues

The Burren · Sun, Dec 13 · 8pm · $21.95

Diamond Blues, delivers high-energy, soulful, and exploratory performances in tribute to the one-of-a-kind, rock-gospel-soul-rhythm and blues group, Jerry Garcia Band.

Since 2021, Diamond Blues, an eclectic group of accomplished musicians passionate about honoring the legacy of Jerry Garcia, have taken New England by storm.

Led by David Rubin on Vocals & Guitar, Diamond Blues features Robbie Parrish on Drums, Ian Brenkle on Bass, Caroline Killoh on Vocals & Keys, and Christopher Belden on Sax and backing vocals. An ensemble capable of furnishing any era of the Jerry Garcia Band story.

DB, as it’s often called by fans, mines their repertoire from the Jerry Garcia Band songbook and blends other fan favorites consistent with the original sound of JGB. Their uniquely crafted setlists touch the entire catalogs of JGB, Legion of Mary/Merl Saunders, and Jerry Garcia Solo, leaving audiences inspired and electrified. DB blends deep cuts with hits that will have you respectfully asking, “why didn’t Jerry play that more often?”

$17 in advance. $22 at the door.

This Is An All Ages Show Doors are at 7:30. Showtime is 8pm. Dinner and drinks available at all tables.
