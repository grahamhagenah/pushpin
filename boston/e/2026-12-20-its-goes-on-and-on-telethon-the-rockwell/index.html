Its Goes On and On – Telethon

The Rockwell · Sun, Dec 20 · 6:30pm · $15–$30 · 18+

Its Goes On and On – Telethon “It Goes On and On! Telethon” is a vintage variety drag spectacular with a great cause! Inspired by iconic telethons such as The Jerry Lewis Telethon, this show will feature a wide array of performances, from drag to musical theater to burlesque.

This winter, we’ll be supporting LGBTQIA+ people in need of warmth and love, with proceeds from the event being donated to BAGLY, a Boston-based charity.

Hosted and produced by Boston drag icon Briar Blush!

Briar Blush is a Boston-based drag queen well known for competing on RuPaul’s Drag Race Season 18. With her fainting beauty and quick wit, she’s back to producing drag excellence, bringing together a wild array of some of Boston’s most amazing performing talents!
