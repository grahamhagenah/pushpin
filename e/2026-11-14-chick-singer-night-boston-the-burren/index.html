Chick Singer Night Boston

The Burren · Sat, Nov 14 · 7pm · $29.95

Chick Singer Night (CSN) Boston returns to one of our very favorite rooms, The Burren Backroom! Saturday, November 14th at 7:00 PM. This all-ages live music showcase will feature a talented line-up of established and emerging female artists including CSN Boston Director Jennifer Truesdale, Co-Director Emma Moreau, Connecticut based Zully Ramos, and Boston area artists Jennifer Tefft, Jillian Ann and Dovetaile. More information on Chick Singer Night Boston plus photos and bios for each of the performers can be found at The evening will be hosted by Chick Singer Night Boston Directors Jennifer Truesdale and Emma Moreau as well as the incredible CSN Boston House Band, made up of some of Boston's finest: Juli Finn on guitar, Justin Kolack on bass, Andrew Beckman on keys, and Jerry Velona on drums.

Proceeds from this show will support The Greater Boston Food Bank
