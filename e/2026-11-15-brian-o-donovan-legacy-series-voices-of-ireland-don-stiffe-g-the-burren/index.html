Brian O’Donovan Legacy Series: VOICES OF IRELAND - Don Stiffe • Ger O’Donnell • Trevor Sexton

The Burren · Sun, Nov 15 · 5pm, 7:30pm · $44.95

Voices of Ireland brings together three of Ireland’s finest performers — Don Stiffe, Ger O’Donnell and Trevor Sexton — in a powerful celebration of Irish music, song and storytelling.

Each artist brings his own unique style, experience and passion to the stage, creating a rich blend of traditional Irish songs, contemporary favorites, beautiful harmonies and unforgettable performances.

From heartfelt ballads and timeless Irish classics to lively songs that have audiences singing along, Voices of Ireland captures the warmth, spirit and character of Ireland in every performance.

With exceptional musicianship, powerful vocals and plenty of Irish charm, Don, Ger and Trevor create an entertaining and memorable experience for audiences everywhere.
