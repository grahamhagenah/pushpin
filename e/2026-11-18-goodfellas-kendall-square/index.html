Goodfellas

Kendall Square · Wed, Nov 18 · 7pm

Based on the true story of an Irish-Italian youth (Ray Liotta) who is adopted by the Mafia and becomes one of their own, Martin Scorsese's violent and humorous film explores criminal life like no other movie. Robert De Niro (as Jimmy "The Gent" Conway), Joe Pesci, Paul Sorvino and Lorraine Bracco ("The Sopranos") star. From the director of THE IRISHMAN, GANGS OF NEW YORK and RAGING BULL.
