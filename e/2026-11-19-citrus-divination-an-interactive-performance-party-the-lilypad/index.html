Citrus Divination: An Interactive Performance & Party

The Lilypad · Thu, Nov 19 · 7pm

$10 suggested (pay what you can) / 7pm doors / 7:30 show / seated & standing Tonight, we celebrate the magic of lemons with an evening of zesty ephemera. Come learn about the budding practice of citrus divination, a new magical practice involving divining futures from the random textures of lemons. The evening will feature a live citrus divination performance, as well as a range of interactive lemon installations interacting with and perusing. Also featuring an Ambient DJ set by CoZy (Cory Zografos).
