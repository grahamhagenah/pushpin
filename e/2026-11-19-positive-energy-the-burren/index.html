Positive Energy

The Burren · Thu, Nov 19 · 7pm · $24.95 · All ages

Positive Energy is Richard Curzi on keyboards and vocals, Jim Wooster on guitar and vocals, Roger Brown on percussion, Paul Judge on guitar and vocals, Rick Dimino on bass, and Kate Donovan and Roger Read on vocals.These local legends (veterans of local bands including The Marsels, Courage Brothers, Radio Musikola, Blood Mountain Brothers, and others) bring their eclectic collection of original songs by Richard and Jim based on the amazing lyrics of Roger Brown and John McAlpin, as well as creative covers paying musical tribute to iconic artists we've recently lost.

$20 in advance. $25 at the door.

This Is An All Ages Show Doors are at 6pm. Showtime is 7pm. Dinner and drinks available at all tables.
