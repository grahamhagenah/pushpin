Unseen and Unhoused: The Crisis of Sex Trafficking in Boston (Panel Discussion with Boston HEAT)

Boston Public Library · Thu, Nov 19 · 6pm

A panel discussion hosted by Boston HEAT: Unseen and Unhoused: The Crisis of Sex Trafficking in Boston.

This session examines how housing instability, homelessness, and substance use intersect with commercial sexual exploitation in Boston, focusing heavily on high-risk areas like Mass and Cass.

Rev. Liz Walker, MDiv - Founding Director of the Can We Talk... Network, Author and Activist.

Abigail Judge, PhD - Co-Director of BOSTON HEAT, Instructor at Harvard Medical School and MGB.

Jim O’Connell, MD - President of Boston Healthcare for the Homeless Program, Assistant Professor at Harvard Medical School, the subject of Tracy Kidder’s non-fiction book Rough Sleepers.

Detective Marivelle Crespo - Human Trafficking Unit, Boston Police Department.

Glennie Hill - Visual Artist, Former BOSTON HEAT client, lived experience expert.
