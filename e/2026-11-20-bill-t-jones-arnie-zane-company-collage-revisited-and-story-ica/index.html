Bill T. Jones/Arnie Zane Company: Collage Revisited and Story/

ICA · Fri, Nov 20 · 8pm · $45–$60

The Bill T. Jones/Arnie Zane Company return to the ICA with a program reflecting on history, legacy, and continued experimentation.

As recently announced by New York Live Arts, the internationally acclaimed Company will bring its 45-year history of performance and touring to a close in December 2027. This engagement offers Boston audiences one of the final opportunities to experience the Company live before its final season.
