The Real School of Music Ensemble Showcase

The Burren · Sat, Nov 21 · 11:30am · $14.35

The Real School of Music in Burlington has been providing music experiences for students of all ages and experience levels for over 15 years. Our Youth and Adult Ensembles spend four months increasing their skills and learning songs they love. At the end of each session, our Student Ensembles take to the stage for their friends & family to showcase all they have learned and experience performing for a crowd. Join us at the Burren in Somerville for an afternoon of amazing music performed by some of our youth and adult Fall ensembles! Family and friends can enjoy what students have been working hard on all session long, while also enjoying a bite to eat! Get ready for a fun and rock-filled experience!
