Club d’Elf returns to the Lizard Lounge Nov 28th for two post Thanksgiving shows! The line up features Rob Compa (guitar), Mister Rourke (turntables), Paul Schultheis (keys) Mike Rivard (bass & sintir), Vicente Lebron (congas) and Dean Johnston (drums). The band played its first show at The Lizard in Feb 1998 and began a residency that ran until March 2020. The Lizard has always been home for d’Elf and the band loves to return whenever the opportunity arises.

What begins as a groove in Club d’Elf’s world often becomes a doorway into someplace older, stranger and unshackled by time.The band has spent twenty seven years helping audiences lose track of time with its shape-shifting fusion of Moroccan trance, dub, electronica, jazz and improvised funk. Built around bassist and composer Mike Rivard, each performance features a rotating cast drawn from Boston, New York and beyond, creating a constantly shifting ecosystem where trance forms the central core of the band’s aesthetic. Under the tutelage of longtime member and Casablanca native Brahim Fribgane, the group absorbed Moroccan trance music deeply into its DNA. Fribgane passed away in early 2024, but his spirit remains embedded in the band’s sound, especially through Rivard’s commanding sintir work, learned under the guidance of Fribgane and Gnawa masters Hassan Hakmoun and Their new album, Loon and Thrush (released 4/10/26), recorded entirely live in the studio with minimal overdubs, channels that lineage into a raw, improvisatory flight.
