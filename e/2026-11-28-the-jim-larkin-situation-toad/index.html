The Jim Larkin Situation

Toad · Sat, Nov 28 · 4pm

Toad Revival Series — Saturdays, 4–7 PM Every Saturday from 4 to 7, we rewind the clock and let the old sparks fly. The Toad Revival Series is our tribute to the bands that built this stage, note by note, night after night. It’s a laid-back, early evening show filled with familiar sounds, longtime favorites, and the spirit of a scene that never really faded—just waited for the right time to plug back in.

These are the artists who gave Toad its heartbeat, returning for intimate, no-frills sets that remind us why we’re here. Think front-porch energy with barroom volume, old songs with new life, and a crowd that knows when to sing along.

Come early. Stay as long as you like. This is Toad, the way it was—and still is.
