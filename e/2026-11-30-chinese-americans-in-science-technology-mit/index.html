Chinese Americans in Science & Technology

MIT · Mon, Nov 30 · 4pm

MIT-China presents the China Diaologue Series. Panelists Professors Xiaoxing Xi and Gang Chen, moderated by Professor Yasheng Huang, will explore how professional achievement can intersect with heightened national-security considerations. Drawing on personal experiences and institutional perspectives, the discussion will address evolving policies and the broader implications for academia, scientific research, and international collaboration.
