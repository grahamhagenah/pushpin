Eliza McLamb with Lily Konigsberg

Arts at the Armory · Wed, Dec 2 · 7pm

Eliza McLamb is a little bit of everything.

She is an artist and a writer. She is a producer of music, podcasts, and stories. She is a hardscrabble girl from North Carolina. She uses the Oxford Comma. She writes records and essays and pages of research about niche feminist theory. Brevity is not her strength.
