Jay Bragg Presents: Christmas in New Vaudeville

The Burren · Sat, Dec 5 · 1pm · $24.95

From Nashville, Jay Bragg's beloved "Christmas in New Vaudeville" returns to The Burren in Somerville, MA bringing The Merriest Show On Earth — now in its 6th annual year — to his home base of New England. Jay, whose grandparents performed in 1930s vaudeville, transforms into bandleader King Kazoo from the North Pole, adorned in his signature bright red frock coat, top hat, brass kazoo, and a dose of whimsy that only the Wizard of Whimsy can muster — leading The Reindeer Band through a joyful, theatrical night of original and holiday music built for the whole family. Equal parts vaudeville throwback and Christmas spectacle, this is a holiday show like you've never experienced and will never forget.
