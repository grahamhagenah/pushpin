LOUDLANDS Holiday Showcase

The Rockwell · Sat, Dec 5 · 12pm · $15 · All ages

Celebrate the holiday season with friends and family supporting our Loudlands students as they show off the songs they’ve been working on! Our Holiday Showcase features individual performances from drummers, guitarists, vocalists, bassists, pianists, and more, giving you the greatest gift of all: a day full of live music. Everyone’s invited to cheer on our growing Loudlands artists. Come enjoy an inspiring performance and close out the year with us!

DOORS: 11:45 AM · SHOW: 12:00 PM
