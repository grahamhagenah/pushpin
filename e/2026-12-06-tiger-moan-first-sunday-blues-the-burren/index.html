Tiger Moan First Sunday Blues

The Burren · Sun, Dec 6 · 7:30pm · $24.95

Amy Kucharik and Tiger Moan invite special guests each month and push the tables aside to make room for dancing.

Tiger Moan: Specializing in vintage blues with sass and class, Tiger Moan is in the fifth year of their residency playing first Sundays every month at the Burren Backroom. Tiger Moan has entertained blues and swing dancers at events such as Sweet Molasses Blues, Blues Union, Yale Swing & Blues and MIT's Bread & Bones fusion dance. The band is known for covering the best blues standards and for funky originals with innovative lyrics.
