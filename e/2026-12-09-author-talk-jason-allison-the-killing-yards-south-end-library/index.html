Author Talk: Jason Allison—The Killing Yards

South End Library · Wed, Dec 9 · 6:30pm

The Fall 2026 Author Talk Series, presented by the Friends of the South End Library, will wrap up with Jason Allison sharing his debut novel, The Killing Yards. Please join us on Wednesday, December 9 starting at 6:30 pm at Union Church (Connection Room, 485 Columbus Ave.) for a fun and lively conversation in this author talk series!

In 1993 New York, reformed drug-trafficker Jimmy Lettner runs Abattoir, Manhattan’s hottest nightspot. All he wants is a clean business, but a city rife with corruption makes that impossible.

Unbeknownst to Jimmy, his right-hand man is running an ecstasy ring inside the club, drawing heat from ruthless rivals and the NYPD. Rookie cop Ben Hopkins goes undercover to make a name for himself, only to get swallowed whole by the gritty scene. As the body count rises and the mayor closes in, Jimmy must embrace his dark past to protect his surviving daughter and save his empire.

Bronx-born and raised, Jason Allison spent twenty years with the NYPD, mostly as a homicide detective. His debut novel,The Killing Yards, will be published by Crooked Lane Books in the fall of 2026. His award-winning fiction has been featured in Fighting Words: Bruisers, Brawlers, and Bad Intentions, Rock and a Hard Place, and New England’s Best Crime Stories.
