State of the Library with BPL President David Leonard

Boston Public Library · Wed, Dec 9 · 12:30pm

Join Boston Public Library President David Leonard for the State of the Library.

In his sixth-annual address, David will explore the ways in which the Library keeps its promise of free to all in today’s complex world. He’ll highlight the BPL’s goals to foster community engagement, empower lifelong learning, serve as a trusted resource, and create welcoming destinations across the city.

Wednesday, December 9 · 12:30-1:30 p.m.

Central Library in Copley Square, Rabb Hall and livestreamed online Whether you’re a longtime supporter, a frequent patron, or someone who believes in the power of public libraries, this conversation offers a front-row seat to the BPL’s essential role in the city. You’ll gain insight into the BPL’s priorities — from protecting the freedom to read to reimagining the McKim Building — and learn how public and private partnerships will continue to shape the Library for generations to come. With opening remarks from Marta Garcia, Chair of the BPL Board of Trustees.
