Book Launch & Author Talk: Michelle A. Gabow & Kate Rushin

Jamaica Plain Library · Thu, Dec 10 · 6pm

Michelle A. Gabow (playwright and author) will launch her fourth novel, a simple queer incorrigible heartbreaking miraculous life, an introspective work that exists in the space between fiction, memoir and prose poetry, exploring aging, grief, memory and falling in love at any age.

Kate Rushin (award winning poet and former fellow of the Cave Canem Foundation) will read from her poetry manuscript The President's Cook and Other American Concoctions, individual lives against the backdrop of history.
