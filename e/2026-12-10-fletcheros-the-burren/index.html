Fletcheros

The Burren · Thu, Dec 10 · 7pm · $24.95

Fletcheros is the long running cover band based out of the Fletcher School of Law and Diplomacy at Tufts, proving that diplomacy nerds can also rock out. From Ariana Grande to Nirvana to The Police, they play it all. Members may be transient, but Fletcheros transcends the test of time.
