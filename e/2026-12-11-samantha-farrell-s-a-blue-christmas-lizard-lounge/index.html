Samantha Farrell’s “A Blue Christmas”

Lizard Lounge · Fri, Dec 11 · 8pm · $25–$60

Join us for Samantha Farrell’s "A Blue Christmas" – a joyful holiday extravaganza at Lizard Lounge Dec 11th!

With special guest appearances by Abbie Barrett, James Rohr, Mark Lipman, Halley Elwell and more!

The houseband for the evening will be the incomparable Chris Hersch & the MoonRaiders.

Each performer will croon their favorite holiday classics and originals of the hall-decking and bell jingling variety.

Expect an evening imbued with jazz-tinged nostalgia, existential angst, warm and moody blues, and some rare, rootsy rock renditions of more obscure holiday gems.

Come embrace the spirit of the holidays with Samantha Farrell and friends, and make this Blue Christmas one to remember!
