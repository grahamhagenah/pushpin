CLAMB was born out of a Dadaist desire to create music free from societal norms of perfection. They dig deep into the groove of soulful American music from the infinite James Brown and Alice Coltrane to the aether of seventies European krautrock pioneers like Cluster and Harmonia to patiently sketch foreign landscapes from the ground up. Clamb are devoted to keeping an attitude of acceptance in their largely-improvised songs, choosing a direction and exploring its corners and nuances to tell stories without words. Their 2026 release “Quiche Mountain” expands on the band’s prior work with the addition of Samuel Crawford on guitar, and features a development of the group’s trademark improvisational style.

Targus Targus is a video game music (VGM)-inspired synth powerhouse. Originating out of frontman Dave Holmes’ deep-seated love for classic retro gaming and modular synthesizers, the project initially evolved in the Boston indie underground as a solo endeavor before expanding into a formidable, lock-stepped live band.

Characterized by complex lead synth lines and a relentless rhythm section, the band crafts sonic landscapes that make listeners feel like they are dodging lasers in an unreleased 16-bit space shooter. Shunning overly serious artistic pretension, Targus Targus labels their serialized discography with a sense of continuous play.
