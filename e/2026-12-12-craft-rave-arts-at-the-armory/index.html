Craft Rave

Arts at the Armory · Sat, Dec 12 · 7pm

Social space for artists to craft, yap, and dance while live DJs mix House/Techno/EDM. BYO project or start something new!

Socialize and create with other artists in the heart of Somerville at Boston's first-ever Craft Rave! Bring your own supplies to continue any projects you've been working on or to start something new. Donation supplies will be available for those who do not have their own, though limited. Please consider bringing extra materials if you are able:) All types of creations are welcome, wifi and outlets will be readily available, and the dance floor will be anticipating your arrival!

4 local upcoming DJs will bring their own craft to the table, live mixing a range of House/Techno/EDM throughout the entire event. DJs will have full creative freedom over their performances, so sets may be experimental. Photos and videos are encouraged!

Food, drinks, and snacks will be available for purchase at the Rooted Cafe, operated by the Arts at the Armory staff. Proceeds to the cafe will directly support this event and allow us to further compensate our DJs, as well as obtain supplies for future events.

Parking is available in the lot behind the Armory, and it's free!

This event is designed to be a safe and welcoming space of free forming creation, bringing artists together and building our community. Please encourage laughter, pure expression, and making new friends while you are here!
