Lyle Brewer’s Wintery Mix

The Lilypad · Sat, Dec 12 · 3pm

$20 admission / 3pm doors / seated & standing / all ages Guitarist Lyle Brewer will perform a set of Christmas music from his album Do You Hear What I Hear? as well as a handful of original songs. This event will also be a guitar drive for “Adam’s Music” run by Adam Dehner.

Adam's Music helps connect inner-city and new immigrant youth with instruments, lessons, and experiences that allow them to fully develop their expressive selves through music, connection, and experiential learning.
