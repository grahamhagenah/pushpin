Opera Untapped : Drunk Opera History: Messiah Sing

The Burren · Sat, Dec 12 · 11:30am · $29.95

Join Opera Untapped as we turn the New England Messiah Sing tradition on its head, adding the fun back story about Handel, a little trivia, and a casual atmosphere where you can sing along… with a beer in hand if that’s your vibe. Expect epic vocal soloists, hilarious anecdotes, and a joyful sprinkling of trumpet to tie it all together. By the end of this family-friendly afternoon, we'll have the whole crowd on their feet for a raucous Hallelujah Chorus!

Opera Untapped brings people together through bold, intimate, and innovative performances that showcase the beauty, drama, and humanity of the classical voice. Through immersive productions, interdisciplinary collaborations, and community-centered programming, Opera Untapped creates joyful artistic experiences that surprise, inspire, and connect audiences of all backgrounds.
