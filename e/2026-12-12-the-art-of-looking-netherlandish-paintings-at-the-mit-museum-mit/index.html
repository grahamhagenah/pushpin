The Art of Looking: Netherlandish Paintings at the MIT Museum

MIT · Sat, Dec 12 · 1:30pm · Free

Did you know the MIT Museum has a remarkable, but little-known collection of seventeenth-century Netherlandish art?

Find out more in conversation with MIT students who have been looking and thinking about the Museum's old master paintings in new ways. Join us for a series of short talks followed by a viewing of select Dutch and Flemish paintings in the Museum’s Anne Chase Allen Workshop.

This event is free with Museum admission, no registration required.
