All That Music Academy - Winter Showcase!

The Burren · Sun, Dec 13 · 10:30am, 12:30pm, 2:30pm, 4:30pm · $29.95

All That Music Academy is home to musicians of all ages and skill levels and we are so excited to host our annual Winter Showcase at The Burren! You will hear all genres of music from classical, to pop, rock, r&b and even metal! Our musicians range from ages 4.5 to over 80 and may have started taking lessons a few months ago, or started taking lessons many years ago. This show is open to all ages and we invite friends and families to join us for a great day of performances.
