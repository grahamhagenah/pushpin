BENEDICT KOH BIG BAND + durianhead

The Lilypad · Sun, Dec 13 · 4:30pm

$15 admission / 4:30 doors / 5pm show / seated & standing durianhead is a Boston-based Singaporean musician working with noise, glitch, ambient, drone, and experimental electronics. Using self-made digital instruments, they explore the increasingly blurred lines between the accessible and the avant-garde: a singular, egalitarian third-stream voice wrangling every last bit of sonic detritus into monolithic walls of sound, color, and light.

BENEDICT KOH BIG BAND presents new compositions and arrangements by Boston-based Singaporean musician Benedict Koh. Featuring an all-star cast of musicians, the group will feature dynamic new compositions and arrangements, blurring the lines of contemporary classical, jazz and improvised music.
