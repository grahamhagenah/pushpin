Gallery Talk: Ruth Erickson on Leilah Babirye: Embaga ya Naki ne Naki (Wedding of Naki and Naki)

ICA · Sun, Dec 13 · 2pm

You’re invited to Leilah Babirye: Embaga ya Naki ne Naki (Wedding of Naki and Naki)!

Inspired by the idea of the queer wedding, artist Leilah Babirye debuts a new body of sculptures, each embodying a role in a wedding ceremony and adorned in headdresses and jewelry. Join Barbara Lee Chief Curator and Director of Curatorial Affairs Ruth Erickson for a conversation on Leilah Babirye: Embaga ya Naki ne Naki (Wedding of Naki and Naki) and learn about Babirye’s vivid installation and inventive use of debris, wood, and ceramics.
