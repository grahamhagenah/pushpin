Longy's Ensemble Uncaged Presents: Ecstatic

The Lilypad · Tue, Dec 15 · 7pm

free admission / 7pm doors / seated Bookended by Taylor Brook's Ecstatic Music and Louis Andriessen's Workers Union, this program lives in the extremes of experience. Angélica Negrón's Dust is in the silent moments following a disaster, the dust settling, despair and possibility the possibility of growth simultaneously in the air, Eli-Eri Moura's Cânticos Meridionais is a tribute to Brazilian folk traditions, and works by Cara Haxo and Arvo Pärt have a trance-like quality, inviting is to slow down, look closely. The opposite of the ecstatic.
