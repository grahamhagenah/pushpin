Guitarist Ahren Shreeve returns to the Lizard Lounge with his trio Dec 16th at 8 o'clock!

Doors 7

Ahren Shreeve is a session/touring guitarist and educator known for his expressive, vocal-inspired playing. Blending blues, R&B, and soul, he brings a unique feel-driven approach shaped by years of live performance and collaboration. Utilizing his improvisational skills and melodic expression, Ahren performs his interpretations of classic and familiar tunes.

In addition to performance work, Ahren maintains a growing online platform sharing performance-driven guitar content, inspiring a global audience of musicians & collaborators.
