Evan Goodrow

The Burren · Fri, Dec 18 · 7pm · $24.95

Join the group in celebrating their 19th record "Time in Time Out" with Jake Walters on keys and Fabio Pirozzolo on percussion.

Evan Goodrow is an American singer-songwriter-guitarist who has written and produced 18 recordings of original soul, funk, blues and rock music. He’s won the Boston Blues Award, played concerts for Marines on Nato bases in Europe, charted in Amazon's top 10 indie artists, but most importantly had a beer named after him from the BackBeat Brewery.
