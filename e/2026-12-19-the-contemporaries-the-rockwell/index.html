The Contemporaries

The Rockwell · Sat, Dec 19 · 1pm · $20–$25 · All ages

Join us for The Contemporaries fourth bi-annual end of season concert! Come hear original a cappella arrangements, featuring covers of artists such as Olivia Dean, Shania Twain, Queen, Radiohead, Lady Gaga and Fleet Foxes! Keep an eye out on The Contemporaries’ social media for future performances and their first single to be released in 2027!

The Contemporaries are a semi-professional, gender-inclusive a cappella group established in January 2025 and based in the Somerville area. Their repertoire is entirely arranged by members of the group and focuses on contemporary pop, indie, rock, and R&B.
