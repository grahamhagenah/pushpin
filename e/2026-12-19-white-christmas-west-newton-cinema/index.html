White Christmas

West Newton Cinema · Sat, Dec 19 · 1pm

Join West Newton Cinema for a musical matinee! Our monthly pick will vary from beloved classics to jazz, from vaudeville to psychedelic rock, brought to you by the imaginative skill of artists able to make scenes look the way great music feels. We’ll feature films from the Golden Age of Hollywood musicals, with surprises that transcend the era and define others. The cinematic brilliance of these films has never left us. In fact, seeing them again, or for the first time, will reinvigorate your love for the movie musical and send you out of the theater tapping your feet.

With its timeless Irving Berlin musical numbers—including its iconic title track—White Christmas is a holiday classic for a reason. In scenes shimmering with superstar performances, we watch as Bing Crosby and Danny Kaye join forces, dancingly and romantically, with sister-act Rosemary Clooney and Vera-Ellen to save a struggling Vermont ski-resort owned by Dean Jagger’s retired Major General. Directed by Hollywood vet Michael Curtiz (Casablanca, The Adventures of Robin Hood), White Christmas is chock-full of memorable moments and infectious tunes, wrapping the holiday season into one perfect Hollywood package that’s sure to put anyone in the Christmas spirit.
