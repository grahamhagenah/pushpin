A Charlie Brown Christmas

The Burren · Sun, Dec 20 · 11:30am · $20.95

Come experience the magic of the season as a live jazz piano trio brings A Charlie Brown Christmas to life on stage! This heartwarming performance captures the nostalgic charm of Vince Guaraldi’s iconic soundtrack, featuring beloved tunes like “Linus and Lucy,” “Christmas Time Is Here,” and more. Performed with improvised, authentic arrangements and the warm energy of live musicianship, this show is a joyful celebration for all ages—perfect for fans of Snoopy, jazz lovers, and anyone who wants to embrace the holiday spirit. Bring your friends and family for an unforgettable day of timeless music and festive cheer!
