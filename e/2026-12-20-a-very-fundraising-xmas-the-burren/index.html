A Very FUNdraising Xmas

The Burren · Sun, Dec 20 · 3:30pm · $26.95

Boston’s ’90s band Fuzzy started a rock ’n’ roll Xmas tradition back in the early ’90s that has continued every year since. More than 30 years later, A Very FUNdraising Xmas remains one of Boston’s most beloved holiday shows, bringing together the city’s favorite rock musicians for an unforgettable evening of holiday songs, community, and cheer — all to benefit Rosie’s Place! Part concert, part reunion, and all heart, this annual celebration features live music, raffles, prizes, giveaways, and MCs Gene Dante, Angelle Wood, and Andrea Gillis. The all-star lineup includes Muck and the Mires // Merrie Amsterburg // Jen Trynin // Magen Tracy // David & Stephen Fredette // Hilken and Melissa Band // Chris Colbourn // The Handymen // Drew O’Doherty & Family // Other Girls // Thalia Zedek // Mike Gent, plus members of Buffalo Tom, Scruffy the Cat, and more! Come celebrate over three decades of Boston’s favorite rock ’n’ roll holiday tradition!
