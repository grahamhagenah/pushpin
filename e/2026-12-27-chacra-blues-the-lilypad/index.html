CHACRA BLUES

The Lilypad · Sun, Dec 27 · 7pm

$15 admission / 7pm doors / 7:30 start / seated / all ages CHACRA BLUES is a Peruvian fusion group blending blues, rock, and the musical heritage of the Andes and Latin America. Their sound combines powerful grooves, improvisation, and cultural roots, bringing the spirit of the Andes into contemporary music.

Fredy Guzmán is a Peruvian guitarist, composer, and arranger known for blending the musical traditions of the Andes with jazz, blues, and contemporary rock. A graduate of Berklee College of Music, he is the creator of Waijazz, a musical project that bridges Andean heritage, Quechua culture, and modern improvisation. His albums include Waijazz (2015), ¡Ay Vida! (2019), and Room to Be (2024), reflecting his ongoing exploration of Peruvian identity through music. Performing internationally, Fredy continues to share the sounds and spirit of the Andes with audiences around the world.
