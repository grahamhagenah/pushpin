Ward Hayden & The Outliers New Years Day

Lizard Lounge · Fri, Jan 1 · 4pm · $35

Ward Hayden & The Outliers ring in the new year with their celebration of the life, legacy and music of Hank Williams Boston is one of America’s great music towns. But among the various styles the city is known for that have launched many musical acts to success and stardom, country music wasn’t a truly thriving genre there, that’s until singer, songwriter and guitarist Ward Hayden and his band The Outliers (formerly known as Girls Guns & Glory) sang and played their distinctive take on C&W with a rock’n’roll kick to the top of the Boston scene and a thriving, critically-acclaimed and highly-awarded career across America and in Europe.Beginning in 2010 Ward and his collection of bandmates & musician friends began gathering together on Jan 1st to kick off every new year and honor the life and legacy of one of their biggest musical influences: HankWilliams.HankWilliams lived for just 29 short years, but what a lasting and meaningful legacy he left behind.Hank left this world on Jan 1, 1953 and every year Ward has assembled an incredible collection of songs from Hank’s catalog, with new surprises, country classics, & deep cuts added every single year.In addition to performing a full set of Hank’s music, each year the band also performs a set of Ward Hayden & the Outliers original songs.For fans of Hank, fans of Country Music, & fans of Ward & the Outliers, it doesn’t get much better than this. And it’s a rare chance to catch the band in the intimate environment of the Lizard Lounge, a venue in which the band has really cut their teeth & where they have returned to every year for this very special & unique show. Join us this year for Ward Hayden & the Outliers 18th Annual Celebration of Hank Williams at the Lizard Lounge.
