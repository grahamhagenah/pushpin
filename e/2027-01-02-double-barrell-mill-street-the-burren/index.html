Double Barrell, Mill Street

The Burren · Sat, Jan 2 · 7pm · $24.95

DOUBLE BARREL plays great rock and pop covers, from the past to present with a hard rock edge. An experienced local band that has been on the scene for over 30 years. Playing a variety tunes from past to current pop tunes. The band features Aaron Lewis (not of stained) on drums and some lead vocals / back round vocals. Mark Lewis (lead/ rhythm guitar). Gary Moulter (Bass). Eric Sowyrda (rhythm/lead guitar). Gabe Smith(Lead Vocals).

MILL STREET is a group of veteran Boston musicians that are also good friends. They play a broad range of country and rock covers. Think from JJ Cale, Linda Ronstadt and the Mavericks, and then all way to Ace and the Beatles. The band features Pete MacDonald (vocal, guitar), Norm MacDonald (guitar), Carrie Martin (Keyboard, vocals), Amy Cain (Vocals), Sheldon Mirowitz (guitar), Josh Hassol (Bass) and David Morse (drums).
