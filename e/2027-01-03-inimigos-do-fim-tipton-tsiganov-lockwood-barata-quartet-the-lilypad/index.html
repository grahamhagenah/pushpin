"Inimigos do fim!" - Tipton-Tsiganov-Lockwood-Barata Quartet

The Lilypad · Sun, Jan 3 · 6pm

$15 admission / 6pm doors / 6:30 show / seated / all ages "Inimigos do Fim!" will feature Brazilian jazz & samba, original jazz tunes, and standards played by a dynamic quartet, including Mark Tipton (trumpet), Alexei Tsiganov (piano & vibes), John Lockwood (bass), and Rafael Barata (drums). "Inimigos do Fim!" translates from Portuguese as "Enemies of the end!," and conveys a spirit of resilience and a passion for life - of refusing to give up! This spirit is at the core of the Tipton-Tsiganov-Lockwood-Barata Quartet's music.
