‘SLC Punk’ Watch Party + Unplugged Performances

The Rockwell · Sun, Jan 3 · 2pm · $12–$20 · All ages

‘SLC Punk’ Movie Watch Party + Unplugged Performances Featuring Kooked Out, Anemoia, & Katie and The Roses + Merch Swap!

The Scene Within The Scene Presents its first Movie Watch Party, Merch Swap and Acoustic Unplugged Series on January 3rd at The Rockwell in Davis Square.

Movie: The 1998 cult classic ‘SLC Punk’ Merch Swap: Bring your merch, trade, exchange, donate with local artists, musicians and friends.

Acoustic unplugged performances by Kooked Out, Anemoia and Katie and The Roses.

The kaleidoscopic sound of Kooked Out has evolved naturally throughout the years and draws influences from almost every genre and decade of music from the 1930s to present day, crafting a unique mix of styles and sounds that have developed Kooked Out a cult-like following in the skate and surf communities. Leaning heavily into the music of the ’50s, ’60s and early 2000s, Kooked Out’s genre-spanning sound has become a calling card, both on record and live on stage, appropriate for a creative outlet that lists its top influences as The White Stripes, The Beatles, Jimi Hendrix, Dick Dale, The Black Keys, Cage The Elephant, and Blink-182.” -Michael Marotta / Knyvet Members: John Fiore, Chris Laskey, Chris Caduto, Emily Trubey / ‘2024 Live Act Of The Year’ New England Music Awards Anemoia is a Boston based alternative band crafting a distinctive murky pop sound that blends atmospheric guitars, emotional vocals, and cinematic songwriting. Drawing inspiration from across generations of alternative music, the band is quickly gaining attention for their striking debut releases “Anemoia” and “Enviro Maniac.”

Katie and the Roses is a Boston-based alternative/indie band playing original music with a soulful, rock-influenced sound. Their songs combine expressive vocals, catchy melodies, and emotionally honest songwriting, moving between intimate moments and energetic, guitar-driven performances. With a warm, dynamic sound and a modern edge, Katie and the Roses bring an engaging original set to the stage.
