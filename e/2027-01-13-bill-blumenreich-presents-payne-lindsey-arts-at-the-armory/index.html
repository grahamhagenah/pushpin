Bill Blumenreich Presents Payne Lindsey

Arts at the Armory · Wed, Jan 13 · 6:30pm

Payne Lindsey is a filmmaker and the creator and host of Up and Vanished, the investigative podcast with more than 600 million downloads. His debut season brought national attention to the disappearance of Tara Grinstead, with two arrests made as the series unfolded. A co-founder of Tenderfoot TV, Lindsey is also known for Radio Rental, Atlanta Monster, and High Strange. Originally from Atlanta, he now calls Boston home.
