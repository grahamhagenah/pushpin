Embrace Honors MLK: One Night. One Boston. Fifth Annual Celebration

Roadrunner · Sat, Jan 16 · 7pm

Join Embrace Boston for the fifth annual Embrace Honors MLK, a celebration of the people, culture, and connections that bring our city together. This year’s theme, One Night. One Boston., invites us to celebrate across neighborhoods, generations, and experiences through the universal language of music, the joy of gathering, and the power of community.

Taking place at Roadrunner, this year’s celebration will feature a special guest headliner and honor the enduring legacy of Dr. Martin Luther King Jr. alongside the centennial of Mrs. Coretta Scott King. As part of the MLK Weekend coalition, Embrace Boston will also recognize changemakers whose leadership and contributions continue to advance justice and strengthen our communities.

Every ticket purchase is a donation to Embrace Boston. Your support goes directly to our work, from monuments, research and storytelling to neighborhood activations and building our new civic and cultural home for belonging at 33–41 West Street in Downtown Crossing.

Come celebrate Black culture, honor those moving our communities forward, and experience a night of music, connection, and collective joy. One night. One Boston. Be part of it.
