Andrew Schneider: NOWISWHENWEARE (the stars)

ICA · Thu, Jan 21 · $20–$40

Every point in space occurs once at each moment in time.

NOWISWHENWEARE (the stars) makes that literal. Inspired in part by Yayoi Kusama’s Infinity Rooms, the stars moves beyond an immersive gallery experience: an unseen narrator guides each visitor through an individualized journey into a matrix of light, of the cosmos, and of the self.
