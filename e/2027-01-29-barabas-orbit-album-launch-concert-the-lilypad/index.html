$22 admission / 7:15 doors / 7:30 show / seated & standing <a href=" class="sqs-block-button-element--small sqs-button-element--tertiary sqs-block-button-element" data-sqsp-button > Get Tickets Orbit is the new solo album by Hungarian trumpeter, pianist, and composer Lőrinc Barabás, who now performs his solo project under the name Barabas. The album is a cinematic and meditative musical journey where neoclassical piano, ambient soundscapes, and electronic textures come together.

The ten compositions explore the ideas of movement and cycles. As the world around us moves ever faster, the music invites listeners to slow down, turn inward, and discover moments of stillness.

Trumpet, piano, and synthesizers engage in a musical dialogue, creating a sound world that is both intimate and expansive, moving between ambient, neoclassical, and electronic music.

Throughout his nearly two-decade career, Lőrinc Barabás has released thirteen albums, performed regularly across Europe and the United States, and collaborated with artists including Erik Truffaz, Takuya Kuroda, Niklas Paschburg, and Valerie June.

His international appearances include the EFG London Jazz Festival at the Barbican, the Jazzycolors Festival in Paris, The Town Hall in New York City, Expo 2020 Dubai, and Expo 2025 Osaka.

The album launch concert at The Lilypad in Cambridge, just outside Boston, brings the world of Orbit to life on stage, where acoustic instruments and electronics merge into one continuous, immersive musical experience.
