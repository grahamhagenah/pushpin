Black Resilience and Jubilation

Berklee Performance Center · Thu, Feb 4 · 8pm

The Lowell Chamber Orchestra, in collaboration with Berklee College of Music, invites audiences to an electrifying evening of sound, story, and spirit with Black Resilience and Jubilation—a concert that honors the enduring power of Black creativity in the face of adversity and celebrates the joy that rises from strength, community, and artistic brilliance.
