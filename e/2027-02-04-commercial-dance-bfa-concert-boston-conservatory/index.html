Commercial Dance BFA Concert

Boston Conservatory · Thu, Feb 4 · 7:30pm

Boston Conservatory’s commercial dance majors perform world-premiere works choreographed by guest artists Alex Fetbroth, Asiel Hardison, and Reina Hidalgo, and by faculty members Jun Kuribayashi and Lindsey Klein—plus a reconstruction of Bob Fosse’s masterpiece Dancin’ Man.
