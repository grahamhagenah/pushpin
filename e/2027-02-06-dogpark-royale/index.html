Dogpark

Royale · Sat, Feb 6 · 6:30pm · All ages

Dogpark, an emerging East Coast band, was forged in the basements and backyards of University of Richmond in 2023. What began as a medley of jazz and a cappella club misfits turned into four-hour sets at beer-covered frat parties and, eventually, a high-flying and highly capable indie rock act cultivating audiences across the country. The band features Eamon Moore (lead vocals), Christian Conte (drums), Declan Harris (lead guitar), Billy Apostolou (guitar), and Will Harford (vocals/bass).

Their infectious stage presence and homage to the indie-rock of the 90s has elevated them from an unrefined cover band to a commanding force to be reckoned with. Their debut EP, ‘Breaking in Brooklyn,’ was released in January 2024, showcasing their ambition and tenacious musicality, all the while setting the stage for their next batch of releases that highlight the evolved taste and synergy of the young indie rock band. This includes the highly anticipated single “Caviar”, out September 17th. Dogpark is currently preparing for their 35-date headlining “Breaking in America 2024” Tour, which will run from September to December, marking a significant milestone in their rapidly ascending career.
