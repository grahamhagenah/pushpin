Kabir Sen & The Krush Factory

Lizard Lounge · Sat, Feb 6 · 9pm · $15

MC Kabir and Krush Factory at the Lizard Lounge for their monthly first Saturday residency!

$15/$20 · Doors 8

Emcee, multi-instrumentalist, and songwriter Kabir Sen is a veteran musician, hip hop artist, producer, and music teacher who has released songs with a wide range of established artists (Blackalicious, J-Live, Esoteric, Mr. Lif, Akrobatik, Lyrics Born, EDO G, Ryan Montbleau and many others).

KABIR SEN – MUSICIAN/PRODUCER | Instagram, YouTube, Spotify | Linktree Kabir will be joined by members of the Krush Factory – a rotating group of talented musicians from the Boston area featuring members of Morphine, Vapors of Morphine, The Slip, Ryan Montbleau, Pressure Cooker, Pop into the Chemist and Duppy Conquerors. This night will be a continuation of a long-running monthly show on the first Saturday of every month!
