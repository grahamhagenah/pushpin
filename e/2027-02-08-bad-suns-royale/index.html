Bad Suns

Royale · Mon, Feb 8 · 8pm · 18+

Bad Suns is Christo Bowman (vocals/guitar), Gavin Bennett (bass) and Miles Morris (drums). The band's catalog includes 2014’s Language & Perspective, which peaked at #24 on the Billboard 200, and 2016’s Disappear Here, which launched the LA-based trio onto tours with the likes of The 1975 and Halsey, festivals including Coachella, and the homepages of Alternative Press and NYLON — with the latter hailing Bad Suns as “the sort of rock that we just never get enough of.”

Following the release of their 2023 EP Infinite Joy, which earned considerable support from Alt Nation, and a full US tour supporting The Band Camino, Bad Suns entered their next chapter with the release of Accelerator in 2025. The album’s lead single “Communicating” received profound support at Alternative Radio, while Rolling Stone hailed Accelerator as “their most compelling in nearly a decade". Now, Bad Suns are celebrating the anniversary of Disappear Here with a special tour honoring the album that helped define their early career.
