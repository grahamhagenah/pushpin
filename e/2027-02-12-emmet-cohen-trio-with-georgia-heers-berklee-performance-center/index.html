Emmet Cohen Trio with Georgia Heers

Berklee Performance Center · Fri, Feb 12 · 8pm

Pianist Emmet Cohen has a rare gift for making a concert ticket feel like a party invitation. His beloved Live from Emmet’s Place webcasts welcomed millions to jazz with music that’s alive, joyful, and irresistible. Cohen brings that same spirit to the stage with his acclaimed trio, joined by vocalist Georgia Heers, whose velvety, luminous voice is steeped in jazz tradition yet entirely her own.
