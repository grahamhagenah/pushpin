Chelsea Cutler

Roadrunner · Sat, Feb 13 · 8pm · All ages

Chelsea Cutler captures the sound of vulnerability and self-discovery, leaning into discomfort and emerging with a confidence that can only be earned through growth. One song might dry the tears of heartbreak and disappointment with genuine empathy, while another could just as easily soundtrack an emotional comeback with a lyrical affirmation. She makes anthemic pop with singer-songwriter earnestness, infusing big feelings into bold hooks.

The multi-platinum singer, songwriter, multi-instrumentalist, and producer isn’t afraid to speak her mind or wear her heart on her sleeve. This approach has endeared Chelsea to a devout audience around the world. In addition to amassing billions of streams across her catalogue, she has garnered six Gold singles and two Platinum singles. Thus far, she has delivered three fan-favorite albums: How To Be Human [2020], When I Close My Eyes [2021], and Stellaria [2023], earning acclaim from Billboard, Variety, The New York Times, Los Angeles Times, Wall Street Journal, Associated Press, NPR, Forbes 30 Under 30, and more. In between her albums, Chelsea and Jeremy Zucker collaborated on the acclaimed brent EP [2019], brent ii EP [2021], and brent iii album [2024], followed by their first-ever tour together, which was completely sold out. She has also sold out worldwide headline tours, lent her voice to tracks with Noah Kahan, Kygo, Quinn XCII, Louis The Child, and others, and performed on The Late Show with Stephen Colbert, Jimmy Kimmel Live!, Late Night with Seth Meyers, and Today Show.

After the Brent Forever Tour with Jeremy, Chelsea spent much of 2025 at home in New York City, stepping away from the nightly rhythm of touring and into an expected period of her life that found her newly single after a serious relationship. She was experiencing a kind of post-breakup freedom that had rarely appeared in her music before, as she went out, met new people, dated, and moved through the highs and lows that came with figuring out who she was on her own. Along the way, she rediscovered inspirations in books, films, and artists, including M83, Bon Iver, Mk.gee, and Clams Casino, allowing creativity to return on its own terms without pressure or expectation.
