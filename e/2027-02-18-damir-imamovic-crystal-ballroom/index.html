Damir Imamovic

Crystal Ballroom · Thu, Feb 18 · 8pm

Vivo Performing Arts presents Damir Imamovic Bosnian singer-songwriter Damir Imamovic, with his trio, shares the narrative power, the gorgeous melancholy, and the yearning inherent in sevdah music. Sometimes called 'Bosnian blues' or sevdalinka, the music reflects Bosnia's rich cultural history: Roma, Slavic, and Sephardic Jewish influences meld with sounds from across the Ottoman Empire. Imamovic, a Sarajevo-born artist with deep family roots in Balkan music, preserves folk tradition while creating music that's evolving and vibrantly alive.
