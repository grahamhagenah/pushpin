Flor de Toloache

Crystal Ballroom · Sat, Feb 20 · 8pm

Vivo Performing Arts presents Flor de Toloache The talents of Latin Grammy winners Flor de Toloache cast a spell on audiences that can be felt down to the marrow. Fans and reviewers describe their shows as spiritual, their voices like healing crystals, their sisterhood on stage setting a new standard for what a concert can be. Led by Mireya Ramos and Shae Fiol, the NYC-based ensemble draws from ranchera, cumbia, salsa, jazz, and rock, weaving it all into something that feels like solidarity made sound. Bold, virtuosic, and completely themselves, Flor de Toloache push mariachi somewhere it has never been before.
