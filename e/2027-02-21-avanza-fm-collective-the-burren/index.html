Avanza, FM Collective

The Burren · Sun, Feb 21 · 7:30pm · $26.95

Avanza is a globally inspired band that is making a unique splash in the Boston music scene by introducing audiences to world musical stylings and international cultural influences they could never find. Our approach is to take contemporary pop and infuse it with traditional musical influences from Spain, Latin America, and all over the world. We sing in multiple languages, including English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, Arabic, Hindi, and more! We do this so as many people as possible can see themselves represented and share in our music. Our goal is to make a change in the Boston music scene by getting more people to fall in love with Spanish, Latin, and world music and bring people of all backgrounds together to hear a part of their identity represented in a way that everyone will love.

FM Collective is an award-winning Boston-based band blending Latin rhythms, jazz, funk, and contemporary influences into an energetic, danceable live experience. Winners of the 2025 New England Music Award for World Act of the Year, the group has performed over 200 shows across New England and beyond, bringing audiences together through original music, infectious grooves, and a multicultural sound.
