Libertango: Mario Stefano Pietrodarchi and String Quintet

Berklee Performance Center · Fri, Feb 26 · 7:30pm

Italian accordion and bandoneon virtuoso Mario Stefano Pietrodarchi makes his Boston debut with Libertango, featuring music by Morricone, Rota, Di Marino, and Piazzolla, and accompanied by a string quintet.
