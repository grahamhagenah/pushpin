Pushpin NYC puts concerts, films, and talks from venues across Brooklyn, Manhattan, and Queens on one page, day by day. It’s curated, with an emphasis on independent venues. No algorithms, no ads, no accounts.

Listings come from each venue’s own calendar every few hours. Times can change, so every listing links back to the venue, where you can check before you go or buy tickets.

How to use it
  • Tap a listing for its picture, price, showtimes, address and other dates.
  • Tonight and This weekend narrow the list to those days.
  • Pick a venue under Where to see only its events. The address keeps your choice, so you can share it.
  • Search matches names, venues and descriptions. On a keyboard, press / to jump to it.
  • A time struck through is sold out.

Questions

Who makes this?

I’m Graham Hagenah. I started Pushpin in Boston to keep track of what’s on at the theaters and concert venues I love, without relying on Google or visiting each venue’s website. This is the same for New York, starting with its independent rooms.

Why isn’t my favorite venue here?

It may not publish its calendar in a way that can be read automatically, or I may not have found it yet. Tell me about it and I’ll take a look.

Will you make this for my city?

I’d rather keep it to places I know well, since I add every venue by hand. If there’s interest somewhere else, and someone who knows its venues to help, get in touch.

How often is it updated?

Every few hours, from each venue’s own calendar, so check the venue’s page before you go.

Why do some listings have no time?

Some venues post only the date, or only when doors open. The venue’s page usually has the rest.

Does it track me?

Only anonymously: GoatCounter counts visits and which listings are opened and shared, with no cookies and nothing personal. Your venue and search choices live only in the page’s address.

Know a venue that should be here, or spotted a mistake? Get in touch.
