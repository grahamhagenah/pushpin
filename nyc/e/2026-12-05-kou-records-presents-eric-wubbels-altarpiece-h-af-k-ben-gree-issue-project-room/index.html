Kou Records Presents: Eric Wubbels’ Altarpiece H. af K. / Ben Greenberg

ISSUE Project Room · Sat, Dec 5 · 7:30pm

Kou Records (Randall Dunn & 2019 ISSUE AIR Charmaine Lee) presents the New York premiere of Eric Wubbels' Altarpiece H. af K. written for bassoon trio and performed by Dana Jessen, Ben Roidl-Ward, and Katherine Young. Nate Wooley (2011 AIR) will lead a conversation with Wubbels. Ben Greenberg (Uniform) opens the evening.
