LPR Presents: S. Carey

National Sawdust · Sun, Dec 6

S. Carey is the moniker of Eau Claire, Wisconsin-based multi-instrumentalist, songwriter, and producer Sean Carey, commonly recognized as the drummer, backing vocalist, and second longest serving member of Bon Iver. Over the past decade and a half, Carey has fostered his flourishing solo career via themes of nature and sustainability, songwriting built from jazz beginnings, and heartfelt, emotive lyricism. His latest release, Watercress, adds to a discography of four full-length releases, two EPs, and countless collaborations, including Unbound with Bizhiki and Shadowlands with John Raymond.
