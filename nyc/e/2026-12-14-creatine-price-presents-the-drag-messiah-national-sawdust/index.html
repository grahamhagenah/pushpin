Creatine Price Presents The Drag Messiah

National Sawdust · Mon, Dec 14

Lauded by the Huffington Post as "innovative" and "genre-defying" Hallelujah, gurl, the Drag Messiah 2026 is here! Creatine Price brings The Drag Messiah to National Sawdust for two nights of Handel’s Messiah like you’ve never seen before. The viral sensation is back and bigger than ever. Drag numbers, baroque opulence and glorious irreverence are what this holiday tradition are all about. In this queer reimagining of Handel’s Messiah, the grandeur of the music meets the wit and theatricality of drag. Joined by an ensemble of singers and instrumentalists, the operatic tenor and Drag Race France queen invites audiences into a celebration of community, self-expression, and the joy of making a beloved tradition your own.
