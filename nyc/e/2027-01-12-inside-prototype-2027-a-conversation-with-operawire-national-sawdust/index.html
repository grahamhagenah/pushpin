Inside PROTOTYPE 2027—A Conversation with OperaWire

National Sawdust · Tue, Jan 12

Join PROTOTYPE, National Sawdust, and OperaWire for a special festival preview celebrating the launch of the PROTOTYPE 2027 and the visionary artists, stories, and performances shaping this year’s lineup. Hosted at National Sawdust, this season preview will give audiences a first glimpse of the bold new works featured in this year’s festival. The evening will feature intimate performances and conversations with the composers, performers, and creative teams shaping the season’s most exciting projects—sharing the inspirations, processes, and stories behind their work.
