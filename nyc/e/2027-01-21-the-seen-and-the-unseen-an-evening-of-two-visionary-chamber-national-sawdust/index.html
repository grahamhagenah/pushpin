THE SEEN AND THE UNSEEN: An evening of two visionary chamber operas about women who listen for voices the world cannot yet hear.

National Sawdust · Thu, Jan 21

A National Sawdust production in collaboration with Yale School of Music and Yale School of Drama, presented as part of the PROTOTYPE Festival. Featuring the world premiere of HILMA (Music and Libretto by Daniel Zlatkin, Directed by Juliana Morales Carreño Hilma: Catherine Martin, mezzo-soprano) and a Work-In-Development Presentation of Red Thread (Music by Wang Lu, Libretto by Deborah Brevoort, Conducted by Anthony Cheung).
