Kyle Sanna: Everything Again Is New (Live Coding with Ensemble)

National Sawdust · Thu, Jan 28

Composer, guitarist, and live coder Kyle Sanna presents the full premiere of Everything Again Is New, a set of algorithmic compositions for electronics and ensemble centered on themes of cyclical regeneration and renewal. Joined by Kinan Azmeh (clarinet, bass clarinet), Lily Holgate (violin), Nathan Koci (accordion), and Marika Hughes (cello), Sanna (guitar, electronics) will use code to reanimate recorded fragments of his own chamber orchestra work, all while projections show the generative process itself, as text becomes sound.
