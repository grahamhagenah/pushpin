BB Wisely

National Sawdust · Sat, Feb 6

Hark! Behold: SLEDDING, the first full-length album from BB WISELY. You might listen to this album and think it sounds unusually seasoned for a debut, and you’d be dead on. That’s because the relationships between the three humans of this trio date back to the early 2000’s, when iPod’s had wheels and arguing about AI was something that happened in a Spielberg chat room. The musical and personal developments between Benjamin Lazar Davis, Bridget Kearney and Will Graefe have pushed forth since those days both in overlaps with each other and on their own circuitous career paths, but never has this trio acted as a unified whole until this very record: SLEDDING is here and it is one fully baked potato.
