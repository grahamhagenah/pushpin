The Marching Band

2 theaters (Amherst Cinema, Triplex Cinema) · Fri, Sep 18 · from 1:45pm

A world-famous orchestra conductor discovers the brother he never knew he had—a school cafeteria worker who plays trombone in a local marching band. Detecting his brother's exceptional musical talent, he takes it upon himself to rectify the injustice of fate.

A Cannes premiere and Audience Award Winner at Rendez-Vous with French Cinema, this equally hilarious and affecting crowdpleaser became a French box office sensation.

Directed by Emmanuel Courcol · NR
