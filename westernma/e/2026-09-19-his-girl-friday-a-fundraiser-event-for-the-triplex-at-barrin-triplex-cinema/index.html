His Girl Friday: A Fundraiser Event for The Triplex at Barrington Hall

Triplex Cinema · Sat, Sep 19 · 6pm · $100

We’re excited to invite you to our fall fundraiser event, a staged reading of the screwball classic His Girl Friday featuring stars of stage and screen — and real life couple! — Jack Davenport (Pirates of the Caribbean, Smash, The Morning Show) and Michelle Gomez (Doctor Who, The Flight Attendant) on September 19th at 6 P.M. at Barrington Hall!

Jack and Michelle will be stepping into the roles made famous by Cary Grant and Rosalind Russell as they read Charles Lederer’s whip-smart script about a fast-talking newspaper editor trying to lure his ace reporter ex-wife back into the business.

Tickets are $100 per person, which also includes the after-party at Barrington Hall, catered by Robbie's Community Market.
