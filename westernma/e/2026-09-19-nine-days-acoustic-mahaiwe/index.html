Formed on Long Island, New York, Nine Days was built on the creative partnership of John Hampson and Brian Desveaux. Combining heartfelt lyrics with catchy melodies, the band quickly rose through the ranks of the local music scene, gaining national attention with the release of The Madding Crowd. Anchored by the infectious charm of “Absolutely (Story of a Girl),” the album resonated with listeners worldwide, blending earnest storytelling with the buoyant energy of the era.
