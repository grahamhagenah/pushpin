Rock the Pantry

Colonial Theatre · Sat, Sep 19 · 7pm

The Rev Tor Band, a local fan favorite, will host the evening with an energetic and eclectic mix that’s sure to have everyone on their feet.

Wanda Houston returns by popular demand following last year’s standout performance, bringing her powerful vocals and dynamic stage presence.

Billy Keane, a gifted musician, shares his soul-stirring indie-folk sound and compelling storytelling.

Gina Coleman, widely known as a singer-songwriter with her band Misty Blues, brings her contemporary blues style to the stage.

Jeff Stevens, one of the Berkshires’ premier horn players, adds his trumpet mastery and musical flair.

Benny “Fingers” Kohn rounds out the evening as an extraordinary jazz pianist and versatile musician with a deep passion for his craft.
