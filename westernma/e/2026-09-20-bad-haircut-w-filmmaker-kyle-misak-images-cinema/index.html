Bad Haircut w/filmmaker Kyle Misak

Images Cinema · Sun, Sep 20 · 4:15pm

Purple Valley Productions presents a special screening of the award-winning feature film Bad Haircut, followed by a 30-minute discussion and audience Q&A with writer/director Kyle Misak.

Bad Haircut made its world premiere at Fantastic Fest and has since played film festivals across the U.S. and internationally, earning awards including Best Feature Film at PUFA (Pucela Fantástica), Best Feature Film at Another Hole in the Head, Best Performance for Frankie Ray at Midnight Fest, and Best Supporting Actor for Frankie Ray at FilmQuest.

Blending comedy, suspense, and coming-of-age storytelling, Bad Haircut follows an ordinary college student whose attempt to reinvent himself through a simple haircut spirals into the most unpredictable day of his life. Following the 1 hour, 49 minute feature, Misak will join the audience for a 30-minute Q&A exploring the making of the film, its characters and themes, the journey from independent production to the international festival circuit, and the process of bringing Bad Haircut to the screen.

Billy Crumpus is a college student who has never believed in himself or had the confidence to make a move. After an interaction with some girls at a party goes horribly wrong, Billy finally reaches his wit’s end. Discouraged by his goofy look and lack of charisma, his best friends, Sonny and Dee, make it their mission to help Billy find his confidence by taking him to their favorite barber—an eccentric and bizarre man named Mick.

What Billy thinks will be a quick haircut turns into the adventure of a lifetime when Mick becomes dangerously unhinged, pushing Billy to new heights through a series of thrilling twists and comedic turns. But through the chaos—and what is undoubtedly the worst day of his life—Billy discovers what he had been looking for all along.
