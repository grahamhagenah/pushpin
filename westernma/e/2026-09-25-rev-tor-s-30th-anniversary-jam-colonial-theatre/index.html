Rev Tor’s 30th Anniversary Jam

Colonial Theatre · Fri, Sep 25 · 7:30pm

Rev Tor returns to The Colonial stage to celebrate 30 years! Since 1996, Rev Tor has been a consistent presence on the East Coast club and festival circuit, performing from Maine to Key West. Tor and his band have shared the stage with members of The Grateful Dead, Dead & Co., Phish, The Allman Brothers Band and New Riders of the Purple Sage. Over his 30+ year career, he has also appeared at festivals and concerts alongside artists such as Leon Russell, Los Lobos, String Cheese Incident and many others.
