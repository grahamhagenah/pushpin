Angela Hassan is a vocalist and multi-instrumentalist specializing in guitar, zither, and harmonium. Most known for leading kirtan, she also offers meditative sound journeys and live music for yoga classes. Her music blends her angelic voice and devotion with transcendent sound to create an environment which softens the heart and invites deep connection.

With more than a decade of experience and certifications in yoga, meditation, and multiple energy healing modalities, Angela's offerings gently guide people back to themselves, to their breath, their heart, and the present moment. She has worked with various communities including youth, young adults, senior citizens, people in recovery, incarcerated individuals, and the general public. Angela has shared music across the globe, including the United States, Europe, India, and Africa.
