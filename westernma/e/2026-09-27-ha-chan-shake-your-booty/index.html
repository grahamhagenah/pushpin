Ha-Chan, Shake Your Booty!

2 theaters (Amherst Cinema, Triplex Cinema) · Sun, Sep 27 · from 10:45am

Haru and Luis love competing in Tokyo’s ballroom dance scene, but after tragedy strikes, Haru withdraws into isolation.

When friends coax her back to the studio, she develops an infatuation with the new instructor.

She must face what comes next as sparks fly.

The Sundance Directing Award Dramatic Winner, HA-CHAN, SHAKE YOUR BOOTY! stars Rinko Kikuchi, Alberto Guerra, and Alejandro Edda.

Directed by Josef Kubota Wladyka · R
