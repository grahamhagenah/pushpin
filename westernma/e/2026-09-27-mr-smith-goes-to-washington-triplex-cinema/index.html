Mr Smith Goes to Washington

Triplex Cinema · Sun, Sep 27 · 3pm

A stoic rebuke to the political corruption of the early 20th century, what does Mr Smith Goes to Washington to say about our current political climate?

Join historian Nicholas Marshall of Marist University for a discussion about the threats the American democratic process faces on its 250th anniversary. Does an historical context help to ease concerns about the present moment? Or does it suggest that we might be living in the end times?

Directed by Frank Capra · 2h 10m
