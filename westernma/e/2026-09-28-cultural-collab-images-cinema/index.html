Cultural Collab

Images Cinema · Mon, Sep 28 · 7pm

The Williamstown Cultural District and Images Lounge present Cultural Collab, a monthly artist meetup on the last Monday of each month, 7 - 9pm. At each meetup three artists will briefly present their work, followed by conversation and social time. Hosted by artist Julie Henderson. Snacks will be provided; drinks will be available for purchase. No ticket needed.

If you are interested in presenting, email williamstownculturaldistrict@
