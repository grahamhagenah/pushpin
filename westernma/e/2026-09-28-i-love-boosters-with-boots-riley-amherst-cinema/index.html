I Love Boosters with Boots Riley

Amherst Cinema · Mon, Sep 28 · 5pm, 8:45pm

Please note: Presales for both screenings are now sold out. Additional tickets will be released the day of the show, available only in person at the Amherst Cinema Box office, which will open at 12pm.

A crew of professional shoplifters take aim at a cutthroat fashion maven. It’s like community service. This raucous new social satire from the mind of Boots Riley (SORRY TO BOTHER YOU) stars Keke Palmer, Naomi Ackie, Taylour Paige, LaKeith Stanfield, Poppy Liu, Eiza González, Will Poulter, Don Cheadle, and Demi Moore.

One of Amherst Cinema's most popular films of 2026 returns for two screenings with writer/director Boots Riley in person! Presented in partnership with the Social Thought and Political Economy Program at UMass Amherst.

The 5pm screening is followed by a Q&A with Boots Riley. The 8:45pm show is introduced by the filmmaker.

Tickets to these screenings are free to any student with ID. Free student tickets are available only at the Amherst Cinema box office (not online) and can be picked up the day of the screening while supplies last. One free ticket is available per student with a valid school ID.

Tickets for non-students cost $18 for Amherst Cinema Members, $20 for general admission.
