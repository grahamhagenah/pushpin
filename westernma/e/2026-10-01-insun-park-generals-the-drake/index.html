General Admission - Standing plus seating available *Ticketing via UMass FAC Box Office We’re just going to say it: You have to see this band.

South Korea’s Insun Park & Generals deliver a visually entrancing, musically engrossing stage show that will lift your spirits and leave you buzzing for weeks.

The band blends Korean traditional music with a mix of contemporary pop forms — notably rock, funk, and hip-hop — to create a unique sound that is expansive and inescapably danceable. And bandleader Park infuses movement and storytelling into the live experience through her mastery of Gangnyeong Talchum, a centuries old mask dance designated as part of Korea’s national intangible heritage. Park stands out as one of the few female masters of an art form historically dominated by men.

Simultaneously preserving tradition and shaping the future of Korean performing arts, Insun Park & Generals come off as cultural ambassadors with a kick. Be sure to join us for this transformative experience.
