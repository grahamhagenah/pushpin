You Had to Be There

Amherst Cinema · Thu, Oct 1 · 2pm, 4:15pm, 7pm, 9:35pm

The 1972 Toronto production of the musical Godspell featured an eye-popping cast of future comedy titans: Martin Short, Eugene Levy, Andrea Martin, Gilda Radner, Dave Thomas—with Victor Garber as a hippie Jesus and Paul Shaffer as musical director.

YOU HAD TO BE THERE features interviews with all surviving members, Lin-Manuel Miranda, Mike Myers, and more, hilariously recounting a production that changed the course of comedy in North America.

“What a delight to hear Eugene Levy and Andrea Martin belting out songs with the gusto of hungry youth. And even better, to eavesdrop on Gilda Radner and Marty Short, who fell in love doing the musical, at the start of their careers riffing with each other at a party... Show business success depends on talent, but requires much more than that. YOU HAD TO BE THERE understands this, while never losing its sweetly sentimental streak.” Jason Zinoman. A New York Times Critic's Pick!
