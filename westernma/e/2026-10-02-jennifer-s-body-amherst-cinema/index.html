A newly possessed high school cheerleader turns into a succubus who specializes in killing her male classmates. Can her best friend put an end to the horror?

The victim of a misguided marketing campaign upon its initial release, this 2009 high school horror flick has since been embraced as a new cult classic. It stands among the best work by leads Megan Fox and Amanda Seyfried, as well as writer Diablo Cody (JUNO) and director Karyn Kusama (DESTROYER, THE INVITATION).

Directed by Karyn Kusama · R
