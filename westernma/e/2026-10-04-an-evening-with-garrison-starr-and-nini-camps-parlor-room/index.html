An Evening with Garrison Starr and Nini Camps

Parlor Room · Sun, Oct 4 · 7:30pm · $24

Garrison Starr & Nini Camps: An Evening of Songs & Stories Two close friends. Two acclaimed singer-songwriters. One unforgettable night.

Join Garrison Starr (touring nationally in support of her new album) and Nini Camps for an intimate, interactive evening where the line between concert and conversation disappears. Trading stories behind the songs, sharing the moments that shaped them, and joining each other throughout the night, these longtime friends and collaborators create a show that is spontaneous, heartfelt, funny, and completely unique. No two performances are ever the same—just great songs, honest storytelling, and the kind of chemistry that only comes from years of friendship. Come ready to laugh, listen, sing along, and be part of a one-of-a-kind evening.

With a highly anticipated new album, The Gospel Truth, arriving soon, Garrison Starr continues to cement her place as one of Americana's most compelling and respected artists. The new record, which she describes as "swamp gospel hymns for humans," is the latest chapter in her journey of recovery, healing, and freedom, following the critically acclaimed Girl I Used To Be.

A singer, songwriter, and Grammy-nominated producer, Starr has spent decades building a remarkable career defined by fearless honesty and unforgettable songs. Her music has been featured on television series including Monarch, Bull, and Walker: Independence, while her powerful live performances have taken her around the world alongside artists including Steve Earle, Emmylou Harris, Bruce Cockburn, Patty Griffin, Mindy Smith, Glen Phillips, Jason Isbell, and the Indigo Girls.

Praised by Glen Phillips as "a voice that goes straight to the gut" and by Mary Chapin Carpenter for writing and singing "her heart out," Starr has appeared at renowned festivals including Newport Folk Festival, AmericanaFest, Hawaii Songwriter Festival, and the 30A Songwriters Festival. Her forthcoming album, Garrison Starr and The Gospel Truth, promises to be her most personal and powerful work to date.

New York-based Americana singer-songwriter Nini Camps is known for her emotionally rich songwriting, cinematic sound, and raw, unmistakable voice.
