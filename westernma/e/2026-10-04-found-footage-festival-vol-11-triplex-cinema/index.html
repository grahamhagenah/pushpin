Found Footage Festival Vol. 11

Triplex Cinema · Sun, Oct 4 · 7pm

Join us, brothers and sisters, for a 90-minute VHS revival hosted by the Found Footage Trio: Joe Pickett (The Onion) and Nick Prueher (Late Show). Like the lord himself, these holy messengers emerge from their video-strewn cave in Brooklyn to resurrect their latest and greatest finds in a joyous analog celebration. You’ll see not one but two bigfoot videos. Can I get an amen? You’ll see cowboy butts in tight Wranglers. Testify! You’ll see a crafting guru paint a fish’s eyeball to make a pretty towel. Hallelujah, these VHS tapes are born again!

Directed by Nick Prueher, Joe Pickett · 1h 30m
