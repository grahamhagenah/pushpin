General Admission (Seated) - Doors open at 6:30PM ARHS Fundraiser: A Beatles Immersive Sing-Along Help! We need somebody. Not just anybody. We need YOU and your friends on Tuesday, Oct 6th at The Drake for Come Together: A Beatles Immersive Sing-Along!

Join us for a fully interactive, participatory show that turns us (the audience) into performers! Get ready to learn, laugh and SING! your hearts out with other Amherst people who know how to have fun and want to support the ARHS Performing Arts. So grab a friend (or ten), and get your tickets before they’re gone. All voices welcome. You don’t need any experience, all you need is love!

Plus, 100% of the proceeds go to supporting Amherst Regional Performing Arts.

Doors open at 6:30pm · Show starts at 7pm

Guided by ARHS Vocal Maestro Todd Fruth, we’ll learn, practice and perform two epic Beatles songs in three part harmony.

Chips, drinks and an intermission cookie sale available to boost our energy.

Wrap by 9pm.

This fundraiser format is inspired by Choir! Choir! Choir! Started in 2011, directors Daveed Goldman + Nobu Adilman have traveled the world and collaborated with artists like Patti Smith, David Byrne, Brandi Carlile, Rick Astley, Rufus Wainwright, and even Kermit The Frog at New York’s Lincoln Center, creating memorable videos that have garnered views in the millions. Now it’s your turn to SING! Grab your friends, get your tickets and Come Together for ARHS Performing Arts!
