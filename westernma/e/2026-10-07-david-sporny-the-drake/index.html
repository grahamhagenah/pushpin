General Admission (Seated) - Doors open at 6:30pm Celebrating the 30th anniversary season of the Amherst Jazz Orchestra (AJO), which began performing in 1996 in the venue below The Drake, at what was then called the Amherst Brewery. The Amherst Jazz Orchestra performs compositions and arrangements written for the AJO, including arrangements by Amherst College Instrumental instructor Dave Sporny. Performers include Dave Sporny, Bruce Diehl, Geoff Cunningham, Dick Poccia, Bob Ferrier and Rob Faulkner. FREE, open to the public. Doors open at 6:30 PM.
