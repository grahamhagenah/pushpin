$15 advanced, $20 at the door General Admission (*18+) - Doors open at 7PM Gulley is a 6 piece alternative rock band based in Amherst, MA. Made up of Terry Kenny (guitar), Jared Parks (guitar), Luke Tzilkowski (guitar), Will Fallon (bass), Kayla Gould (drums), and Nolan Lessard (lead vocals). Forming in late October of 2025, Gulley blends influences from bands like Stone Temple Pilots, The Smashing Pumpkins, and RadioHead to bring a loud, high energy sound.

Cherry Flavored is a five-piece Alternative rock band from UMass Amherst. Formed in the Spring of 2025, the group is made up of James Angell (guitar), Cat Chehwan (vocals), DJ Repetto (bass), Gabe Stevens (guitar), and Jack Twombly (drums). They play a mix of original songs and crowd pleasing covers, giving energetic performances wherever they play. The group plans to release an ep of original music this fall on all streaming platforms.
