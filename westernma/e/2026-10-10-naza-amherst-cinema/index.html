NAZA

Amherst Cinema · Sat, Oct 10 · 2:25pm, 4:55pm, 7:35pm, 9:40pm

In secret conversations on the rooftops of Tel Aviv at night, the systems behind Israel’s calculated mass killing of Palestinian civilians in Gaza are revealed by the people who designed and operated them.

Winner of four awards at the 2026 Venice Film Festival, including the Special Jury Prize.

“A shattering inquiry into Israel’s military actions in the war in Gaza, it was directed by the Israeli filmmakers Yuval Abraham and Rachel Szor, who also codirected the 2024 documentary NO OTHER LAND... The movie largely focuses on civilian deaths, and its title is shorthand for nezek agavi, which effectively means collateral damage.”

–Manohla Dargis, New York Times Critic's Pick “As the sources unfold the intentions, the methods, and the effects of Israel’s war in Gaza, they convey the enormity of rational, bureaucratic warfare. They emerge as ordinary people taking actions that should in no way be ordinary but which have been made ordinary by the system they work within... The literal darkness in which sources speak in NAZA turns the screen into a mirror of subjectivity, putting viewers face to face with their motives, their responsibilities, their identities.”

Directed by Yuval Abraham and Rachel Szor · NR
