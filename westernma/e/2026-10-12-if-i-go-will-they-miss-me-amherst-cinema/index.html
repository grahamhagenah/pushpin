If I Go Will They Miss Me

Amherst Cinema · Mon, Oct 12 · 2:15pm, 4:40pm, 7:20pm

In the Watts neighborhood of Los Angeles, twelve-year-old Lil Ant sees the world and his father the way all children should—full of myth, wonder, and unwavering belief.

IF I GO WILL THEY MISS ME is a breathtaking father-son story about imagination, family, and the grace we extend to the people we love.

“In these actors’ hands, the title’s question finds meaning and perspective. Each one asks themselves and the world whether they can be acknowledged as having existed, having lived, and having loved one another. Thompson-Hernández assuredly records that they do.”

Directed by Walter Thompson-Hernández · NR
