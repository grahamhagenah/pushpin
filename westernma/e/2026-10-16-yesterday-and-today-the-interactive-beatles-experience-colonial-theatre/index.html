Yesterday and Today: The Interactive Beatles Experience

Colonial Theatre · Fri, Oct 16 · 7:30pm

Step into the excitement of the British Invasion with Yesterday and Today: The Interactive Beatles Experience, a nostalgic journey through the music that changed everything. Created by brothers Billy, Matthew and Ryan McGuigan, this isn’t a typical tribute concert—it’s a fan-driven celebration where the audience chooses the entire setlist. Before the show, guests submit their favorite Beatles songs along with the memories behind them, and moments later those requests shape a one-of-a-kind performance.
