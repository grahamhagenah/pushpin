Tony Silva

Parlor Room · Sat, Oct 17 · 6:30pm · $30

Fiesta Rumbera is a Massachusetts-based quartet of world-class performers led by guitarist and vocalist Tony Silva, whose love and dedication to music of the Spanish diaspora has earned him well-deserved praise for nearly 20 years. Performing Rumba Flamenca, the party version of Flamenco (made famous by the Gypsy Kings), the group’s rhythms blend Spanish, Cuban and South American influences with fiery solos and onstage charisma that invite you to dance, clap, and celebrate.

With: Chris Devine, violin and flute (MInstrels in the Gallery, Fusion Nomads), Rudi Weeks, bass (Viva Quetzal),and Michel Moushabeck, cajon and percussion (Laayali, Masala Jazz).
