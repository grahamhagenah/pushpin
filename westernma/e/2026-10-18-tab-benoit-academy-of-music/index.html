Ghalia Volt is a modern blues artist whose time has come. With her relentless drive, captivating stage presence, and ability to command audiences in both solo and full-band settings, Volt has emerged as a rising force on the international blues scene. Originally from Belgium and now rooted in the American South, Volt bridges the gap between tradition and innovation, blending the grit of Hill Country blues with the raw energy of rock and roll. Whether performing with her powerful trio or as a One Woman Band, her shows are an unforgettable journey through the blues, infused with passion, authenticity, and an undeniable mystique.

Over the past two years, Volt’s artistry has grown into a commanding presence on stages around the world. In 2023, she was featured on 60 Minutes in an exploration of the modern blues, a milestone that affirmed her rising profile. Her critically acclaimed album Shout Sister Shout!, recorded at the legendary Rancho De La Luna studio in Joshua Tree, California, reached #2 on the Billboard Blues chart. Yet, it is her live performances that define her. In 2024, she toured extensively across the U.S. and Europe, sharing stages with artists like Tab Benoit, Samantha Fish, Jesse Dayton, Dragon Smoke, and Reverend Peyton’s Big Damn Band. She also took the spotlight as a featured headliner on the acclaimed Ruf Records’ 30th Anniversary Tour alongside Samantha Fish and Canned Heat, cementing her reputation as a standout act.
