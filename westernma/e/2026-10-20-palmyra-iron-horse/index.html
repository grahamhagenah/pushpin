Palmyra

Iron Horse · Tue, Oct 20 · 8pm · $18

Established in Virginia’s Shenandoah Valley, Palmyra captures the collective spirit of three Virginia natives: Teddy Chipouras, Mānoa Bell, and Sasha Landon.

Palmyra straddles at least two musical worlds. They are, on one hand, a band from the South that plays traditional instruments and indeed once lived in the old-time locus of Floyd, Virginia. Comparisons to and a kinship with The Avett Brothers and even Old Crow Medicine Show are inevitable. On the other hand, Palmyra writes about grief, gender dysphoria and identity, and coming of age in songs that flirt with soul, post-rock, and even emo; the South, too, is the place of My Morning Jacket, Band of Horses, Cat Power, and now, Palmyra.

The band’s first full length LP, Restless, is out now on Oh Boy Records. Framed by moments of struggle, solidarity, and hard-won growth, Restless is an unqualified ringer for anyone who loves the space where the roar of indie rock collides with raw folk.

West Virginia-born, Brooklyn-based singer-songwriter Olivia Ellen Lloyd had a banner 2025 with her sophomore album, Do It Myself, released to critical acclaim, and has returned to the studio in 2026 to build off that momentum with new music and a new alt-country direction. Her record release tour included a sold-out record release show at Public Records in NYC, a Mountain Stage debut alongside Jeff Tweedy, two Midnight Ramble appearances at Levon Helm Studios (including a sold-out opening slot with her full band), opening the sold-out Saturday night lineup at Brooklyn Folk Festival, and her debut as an official showcasing artist at Americana Fest. She also made live video debuts on Western AF and Paste Live Sessions. That album cycle followed her debut record Loose Cannon (streamed over 1 million times) and included support runs with Tommy Prine and Kelsey Waldon, as well as opening slots for Sarah Shook & The Disarmers, Sammy Raye & The Friends, Jobi Riccio and more.

She was a mainstay on Sirius XM’s Outlaw Country all year, recording a live in-studio session in May and performing in their annual live broadcast from Robert’s Western World during AmericanaFest. Come year’s end, Steve Earle included her song “Knotty Wood” on his personal Top 25 of ‘25 playlist, on his Hardcore Troubadour Radio program, the most widely listened to specialty show on Outlaw.
