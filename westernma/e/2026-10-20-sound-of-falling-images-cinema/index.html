Sound of Falling

Images Cinema · Tue, Oct 20 · 7pm

Presented as part of the German-Austrian Film Festival, organized and sponsored by the Williams College German Department.

This year, the German-Austrian Film Festival examines the depiction of the countryside in contemporary German-language cinema.

Set against rural landscapes, Fatih Akin's Amrum (Germany 2025), Mascha Schilinski's Sound of Falling (Germany 2025), and Andreas Prochaska's Welcome Home, Baby (Austria 2025) explore islands, villages, and isolated homes as sites of belonging, trauma, and transformation.

Together, these films reveal the countryside not as an idyllic escape from modern life, but as a landscape where the past lingers, generations collide, and the familiar can become both deeply comforting and unsettling.

Four adolescent girls each spend their youth in the same farmhouse over the last century. Though separated by decades, resonances between their lives emerge: their desires and distress, secrets and truths, encounters with another’s gaze and defiant gaze in return.

Directed by Mascha Schilinski · 2h 28m
