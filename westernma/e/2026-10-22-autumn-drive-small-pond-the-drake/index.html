$15 advanced, $18 day of show General Admission (Standing) - Doors open at 7PM Born as a recording project in Summer 2023, Autumn Drive has blossomed into an up-and-coming indie group to watch, touring across greater New England and beyond.

With roots in New Bedford, Massachusetts– Autumn Drive emerged as a local favorite among the iconic bar scene on Cape Cod and the Islands. Since, the group has taken to Boston and college communities across the state, selling out prominent venues and playing MojoFest; the largest college music festival in Massachusetts with 5,000 attendees.

Most recently, Autumn Drive comes off their first East Coast tour in late 2025, supporting their newest EP “Going Undone” with nearly 100,000 streams. The group was named a finalist for the 2025 Levitate Music Festival’s Songwriter of the Year national contest, making their large-scale festival debut. With three EPs under their belt, Autumn Drive looks to their debut LP in 2027 with a new double single, “Times Before / Walk Away” out now.

Small Pond didn't set out to make a concept record - they set out to survive the space between two recording sessions, four hundred miles from home, in a car named Vicki Jean Volvo. What came out the other side is Soft Burn, the New England indie rock band's sophomore album and the sound of four people who've stopped trying to agree on what kind of band they are.

Formed on the New Hampshire seacoast and now rooted in Portland, Maine, Small Pond built their name the way most great regional bands do: three EPs, a live record, relentless local shows, and a songwriting voice courtesy of singer Molly McDevitt - sharp enough to turn discomfort into something you want to sing along to. Their 2022 debut, Let It Fall Like It Should, established the blueprint: folk-honest lyrics braced against modern indie rock's heavier edges. Soft Burn, out October 21, is where that blueprint gets torn up.
