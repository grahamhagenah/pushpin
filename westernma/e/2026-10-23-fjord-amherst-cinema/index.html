Fjord

Amherst Cinema · Fri, Oct 23

Opens Friday 10/23.

A Norwegian woman (Renate Reinsve) and her devout Romanian husband (Sebastian Stan) relocate with their five children to a remote, close-knit, coastal town.

Their teenage children form a fast bond with a neighboring family as unease begins to simmer around their rigid beliefs.

When allegations of abuse emerge, they are forced to confront a system of judgment that blurs the line between concern and prejudice.

This is the second Palme d'Or-winning film from director Christian Mungiu, who also earned the award in 2007 for 4 MONTHS, 3 WEEKS AND 2 DAYS.
