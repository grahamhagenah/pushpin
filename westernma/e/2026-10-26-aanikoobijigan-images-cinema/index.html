Aanikoobijigan

Images Cinema · Mon, Oct 26 · 7pm

Presented as part of our second edition of Indigenous Peoples Film Festival, focusing on films by indigenous filmmakers from the US and Canada. This edition is co-curated by Images ED Dan Hudson and Images volunteer Liz Redwing.

AANIKOOBIJIGAN is our closing night selection, and Bonney Hartley, Tribal Historic Preservation Manager for Stockbridge-Munsee, is scheduled to attend.

Please join us for a social hour in the lounge before the screening from 6pm-7pm.

Trapped in museum archives, Ancestors bend time and space to find their way home. History, spirituality, and the law collide as tribal repatriation specialists fight to return and rebury Indigenous human remains, offering a revealing look at the still-pervasive worldviews that justified collecting them in the first place.

Directed by Adam Khalil, Zack Khalil · 1h 25m
