Nosferatu live-scored by the Andrew Alden Ensemble

Amherst Cinema · Thu, Oct 29 · 7pm

Directed by F.W. Murnau, this seminal vampire film follows Thomas Hutter as he journeys to the remote castle of the mysterious Count Orlok, unknowingly unleashing an ancient evil that follows him home.

An original score by the Andrew Alden Ensemble complements the film’s haunting imagery and creeping terror with a visceral blend of strings, piano, synthesizers, percussion, and electric guitar.

Performed live alongside the film, the ensemble transforms Murnau’s silent masterpiece into an immersive experience of mounting dread, obsession, and horror, bringing new life to one of cinema’s most enduring nightmares.

Directed by F.W. Murnau · NR
