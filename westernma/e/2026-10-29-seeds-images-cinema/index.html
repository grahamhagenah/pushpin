Seeds

Images Cinema · Thu, Oct 29 · 7pm

Presented as part of our second edition of Indigenous Peoples Film Festival, focusing on films by indigenous filmmakers from the US and Canada. This edition is co-curated by Images ED Dan Hudson and Images volunteer Liz Redwing.

SEEDS is our bonus encore horror film for the festival, and justin "Björn" adkins from Wild Soul River will present on three sisters farming and seed preservation.

Please join us for a social hour in the lounge before the screening from 6pm-7pm.

A Mohawk woman is hired as a social media influencer but gets caught in a deadly cat-and-mouse game, as she's forced to go to war to preserve her people's heritage from corporate interests.

Directed by Kaniehtiio Horn · 1h 22m
