Splendid Torch plays Fleetwood Mac

Parlor Room · Fri, Oct 30 · 7:30pm · $26.40

Splendid Torch is not following a trend. With their sensitivity, suppleness, power & pure skill, they’re redefining Americana. Splendid Torch, inspired by classic country and their past experience in traditional music, brings indie sensibility to their heartfelt –and at times unusual– songs. Fronted by vocalist Lily Sexton, they are proudly claiming their place in the lineage of prominent, genre-defying women, calling to mind Emmylou Harris, The Roches, & Sheryl Crow.

Splendid Torch reaches out with their music, beckoning one closer, inviting us all to be part of something bigger together, if only for one night.
