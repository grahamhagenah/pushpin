The Mist (Black & White)

Amherst Cinema · Fri, Oct 30 · 9:45pm

After a mysterious mist envelopes a small New England town, a group of locals trapped in a supermarket must battle a siege of otherworldly creatures... and the fears that threaten to tear them apart.

Starring Thomas Jane and Oscar® winner Marcia Gay Harden, THE MIST has emerged as one of the most unnerving horror films of the twenty-first century, featuring an ending that continues to shock audiences the world over.

Adapted from the novella by Stephen King by director Frank Darabont, THE MIST is presented here in Darabont's preferred black-and-white version.

Directed by Frank Darabont · R
