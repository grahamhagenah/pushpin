Vampyr (1932) w/The Andrew Alden Ensemble

Images Cinema · Fri, Oct 30 · 7pm

Directed by Carl Dreyer, a ghost hunter stumbles upon a small town haunted by a powerful vampire. The score features voice, violin, keyboard, cello, and percussion.

The Andrew Alden Ensemble is a New York-based chamber music group, touring for over a decade, that performs original scores live alongside classic films. Our instrumentation (violin, cello, keys, percussion, and voice) sits somewhere between contemporary classical and rock, and the effect in the room is unlike a traditional film score: the music is immediate, physical, and written specifically for and according to each film.

Directed by Carl Theodor Dreyer · 1h 14m · NR
