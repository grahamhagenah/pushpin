Fasten your garter belt and come up to the lab and see what's on the slab! THE ROCKY HORROR PICTURE SHOW is a screamingly funny, sinfully twisted salute to sci-fi, horror, B-movies and rock music, all rolled into one deliciously decadent morsel.

The madcap, musical mayhem begins when rain-soaked Brad (Barry Bostwick) and Janet (Susan Sarandon) take refuge in the castle of Dr. Frank-N-Furter (Tim Curry), a mad scientist from outer space who is about to unveil his greatest creation—and have a bit of fun with his reluctant guests!

Directed by Jim Sharman · R
