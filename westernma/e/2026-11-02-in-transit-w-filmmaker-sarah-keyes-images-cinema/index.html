In Transit w/filmmaker Sarah Keyes

Images Cinema · Mon, Nov 2 · 7pm

This special screening will feature a post screening Q&A with local filmmaker and producer Sarah Keyes!

Sarah Keyes is a theatre artist, independent film producer, and arts educator based in Troy, NY. She is on staff at Williams College and is a proud Images Cinema member. As an actor, she was most recently seen as LISA in iBoss at Great Barrington Public Theatre. You can currently see her work in Lend Me a Tenor at The Majestic. Other recent credits include: Survival of the Unfit (GBPT; Berkie Nominated for Outstanding Supporting Actress), Alien Girls (WAM Fresh Takes); Amchitka (GBPT Benefit Reading); 10x10 (u/s Barrington Stage Company); The Skin of Our Teeth (TFANA); Sense & Sensibility, Ragtime, Native Son, Life of Galileo, Constellations (PlayMakers Rep); The Mysteries (The Flea); Usual Girls (EST Bloodworks); Horse Girls (Ars Nova Ant Fest); Film: In Transit, Bears, Tell That to the Winter Sea, Good Grief, Invisible Girl. BA Fordham University Lincoln Center; MFA University of North Carolina Chapel Hill.

When a complacent young bartender living in small-town Maine agrees to model for a existentially floundering painter on an artist's retreat, both women must confront their preconceptions of what makes a good life.
