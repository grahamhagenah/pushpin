A twelve-member rock and funk juggernaut based out of Western MA, The Friendship Band began as a class offering of Whole Children ( and has expanded into a premiere live local act, rocking stages such as Flywheel, Northampton Center for The Arts, UMASS Ballroom, The Pines Theater, The Forbes Library, and The Iron Horse. The Friendship Band has shared the bill with many wonderful groups including Eavesdrop, Soul Magnets, The Expandable Brass Band, and Yo La Tengo. We believe in the power of music to heal, and communicate, and to express our true selves.

Mind Left Body is a western Massachusetts’s based ensemble specializing in thoughtful interpretations of classic Grateful Dead music. Hailing from the Pioneer Valley, performances showcase vocal harmonies and expansive musical explorations while also staying deliberate and focused on the beautiful songwriting talent that propelled the Grateful Dead to the ranks of one of America’s most interesting rock and roll bands.
