Bernstein’s Wall

"Bernstein’s Wall lets the maestro talk about subjects like McCarthyism, civil rights, political corruption, unjust wars, and the problems he faced being an American composer who was also Jewish." - Odie Henderson, The Boston Globe Douglas Tirola's thrilling documentary portrait of famed composer and conductor Leonard Bernstein uses the maestro's own voice to create an archival portrait that tells an intimate, complicated, heartbreaking and passionate life story that is ultimately trying to inspire us to make a change in our society today.
