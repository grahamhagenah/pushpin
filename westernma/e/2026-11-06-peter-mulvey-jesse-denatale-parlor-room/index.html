Peter Mulvey + Jesse DeNatale

Parlor Room · Fri, Nov 6 · 7:30pm · $30

Peter Mulvey is among the most celebrated American singer-songwriters of his era. Raised in Milwaukee, he sharpened his craft busking on the streets of Dublin and in the subways of Boston before touring nationally and internationally. Known for his intense percussive guitar style, wry lyricism, and eclectic influences (jazz, poetry, Tin Pan Alley), Mulvey has thousands of live performances under his belt, released over twenty albums, led bicycle tours, written a popular children’s book, given a TEDx Talk, launched/hosted Lamplighter Sessions, and collaborated widely across the indie folk scene. Mulvey. Never. Stops!

Jesse DeNatale is a singer songwriter from Northern California. He has released four critically acclaimed records, the last two,The Wilderness and The Hands of Time, are on the Blue Arrow Record label, (Jonathan Richman).

DeNatale, (sounds like, Dean - uh - Tally) will be on tour this November 2026 and coming to a handful of Eastern cities. He is excited to be making his first appearance by sharing the night with his friend Peter Mulvey in at The Parlor Room in Northampton MA.

Jesse’s music has been described as evocative and hopeful, a combination of poetry and storytelling. Over the years his love of various musical genres has inspired him to expand his ideas of songwriting as he continues to look for ways to reflect back the realities of being alive. Rich with imagery, intelligence and passion, his music remains timeless. It's soulful accessibility is what has garnered him praise from other artists and has kept his devoted fans listening.

A troubadour in spirit, Jesse has longtime roots in New England and spent many of his younger years in Deerfield Massachusetts traveling with his guitar from California where he found work at the Farm School called Woollman Hill. Over the years he’s kept a place in his heart for the culture, atmosphere and communities of the small towns of the Pioneer Valley. Some of his formative musical adventures took place while playing at bars and cafes like the Rendezvous in Turners Falls or the old Iron Horse in Northampton.

Jesse has toured with artists such as Jonathan Richman and supported many others such as Chris Smither, Steve Forbert and Dave Alvin. He has played in music festivals from San Francisco to Winnipeg to Killkeny IRL.
