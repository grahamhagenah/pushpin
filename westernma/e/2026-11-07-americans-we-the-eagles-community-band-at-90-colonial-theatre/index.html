Americans We: The Eagles Community Band at 90

Colonial Theatre · Sat, Nov 7 · 7pm

The Eagles Band comes to the Colonial to present Americans We, a celebratory concert of two anniversaries. The first half of the program is the finale to the Semiquincentennial year they have all been observing. It includes music rooted in our country’s history, with works by John Williams, Lin-Manuel Miranda, a salute to American jazz, and "Tribute to America" by director David Diggs.
