Spotlight

Triplex Cinema · Sat, Nov 7 · 3pm

Talkback with Boston Globe Editor Marty Baron after the film!

To honor the 25th anniversary of the landmark Boston Globe investigation that inspired the Best Picture winning film, 1761 Arts, the Berkshire International Film Festival, and The Triplex Cinema are hosting a screening of Todd McCarthy's Spotlight, followed by a talkback with former Boston Globe editor Marty Baron in conversation with Berkshire Magazine's Joshua Sherman, on November 7th at 3 pm.

Directed by Tom McCarthy · 2h 9m · R
