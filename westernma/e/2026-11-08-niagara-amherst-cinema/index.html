Niagara

Set against the roaring backdrop of Niagara Falls, the film revolves around Rose Loomis (Marilyn Monroe), a femme fatale with secret, murderous plans for her husband (Joseph Cotten), and a honeymooning young couple who become entangled in her ill-fated scheme.

NIAGARA is one of the rare classic noirs shot in Technicolor, featuring incredible, eye-bursting color.

Directed by Henry Hathaway · NR
