SAP

The Drake · Tue, Nov 10 · 8pm

General Admission (Standing) - Doors open at 7PM $15 Advanced, $20 Day of Show SAP is an independent, alternative rock trio from Austin, TX. Infusing the energetic and melodic sonics of 90’s grunge with emotionally driven dynamics of contemporary music, Sap creates a perfect soundscape for unpredictable live shows. The band is composed of guitarist/vocalist Evan Fundora, bassist Hank Barna, and drummer Jared Cox. Together they have accrued over 6 million streams worldwide, touring with longstanding bands like The Jins and most recently with up and coming acts such as Clarion, Rehash and Cheerleader Roadkill. Sap is set to go on their own US tour in support of their new 2026 album ‘Erasure’.

Channelling the high-energy, heavy breakdowns of 2000s post-hardcore, paired with melodic vocals that soar, SIR ECHO are a force to be reckoned with hailing from Upstate New York’s alternative scene.

Known for their razor-sharp riffs, cathartic choruses, and haunting melodies, their songs are a crashing ocean wave of emotion, and simultaneously, are the lighthouse guiding the ship home.

Gathered from the dynamic tango of harsh and clean vocals of Alexisonfire, stylistically blended with introspective intensity adjacent to Hail The Sun, the amalgamation of influences give the band a distinctive texture in the emo/alternative space.

Their latest release ‘To Fly With Clipped Wings’ (Produced by Alan Day of Four Year Strong), is the band’s debut EP released with Equal Vision Records.

Sludge Mother is a sonic force reigniting the raw energy of 1990's rock anthems. Channeling the heavy, sludgy riffs of Soundgarden and the emotive depth of Alice In Chains, Sludge Mother is resurrecting grunge’s soul, reimagined for a new era. Sludge Mother, known for her intense vocals and unfiltered lyricism, fuses the grit of Seattle’s golden age with a modern, visceral intensity. The result is a sound that’s both familiar and fresh—drenched in fuzzed-out guitars, brooding melodies, and the kind of anthemic choruses that demand to be shouted in dimly lit venues.
