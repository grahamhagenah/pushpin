Free Family Night

Mass MoCA · Thu, Nov 12 · 5:30pm · Free

Families with children of all ages are invited to Family Night at MASS MoCA on Thursday, November 12, from 5:30 to 7:30pm. This free event will feature after-hours access to select exhibitions in the museum, live performances, art-making, face painting, a museum of mysteries quest, food, giveaways and fun!

Free; RSVPs encouraged but not required.

Participatory Poetry Activation by The Mastheads Pop-up Dance Performances by the faculty of Creative Soul Face Painting – Offered for free by Hannah True & Western Mass Face Painting by Emily Gwynn.

Art-making – Individual and collaborative art-making projects in Kidspace.

Live Jazz Music Presented by BAAMS – the Berkshires’ Academy for Advanced Musical Studies presents a special night of world-class jazz featuring five extraordinary musicians, each bringing decades of experience, artistry, and heart to the stage. Special young musician guests from among BAAMS’ students will also be invited to sit in during the evening.

Museum of Mysteries Quest – Explore, discover clues, and uncover the mysteries in MASS MoCA’s galleries. Work together as a family to solve the mysteries and receive your own artist kit!

This program is supported by the WLS Spencer Foundation, MountainOne Bank, and Berkshire Taconic Community Foundation.
