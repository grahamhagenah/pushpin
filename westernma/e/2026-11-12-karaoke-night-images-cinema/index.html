Karaoke Night

Images Cinema · Thu, Nov 12 · 8pm · 21+

Sing your heart out each and every Thursday starting at 8pm at the Images Cinema Lounge. No need to buy a ticket, just come by, sign up on the clipboard, and we take care of the rest. If you want to be the host for an upcoming karaoke, send an email to andrew@ Hosts get to introduce singers, encourage people to sign up for a song, and (if they want to) suggest a theme for the night.

The bartender will be available and ready to serve cocktails, beer, wine, and cider. The event is limited to people age 21 and over.
