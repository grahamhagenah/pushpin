Fatherland

Amherst Cinema · Fri, Nov 13

Opens Friday 11/13.

At the height of the Cold War, the writer Thomas Mann and his daughter—actress, writer, and rally driver—Erika embark on a road trip in a black Buick cruiser across a divided Germany in ruins, traveling from the Allied-occupied city of Frankfurt to Soviet-controlled Weimar.

The new film from director Paweł Pawlikowski (IDA, COLD WAR) stars Sandra Hüller (ANATOMY OF A FALL) and Hanns Zischler (MUNICH).

“A film of deep, luminous feeling... Huller’s ability to let you into her characters’ thinking is a marvel.” Manohla Dargis. A New York Times Critic's Pick!
