Uncomputable Thread

Mass MoCA · Sat, Nov 14 · 1pm · $15–$20

Uncomputable Thread is a presentation, conversation and participatory movement workshop by artist Taeyoon Choi, created in collaboration with scholar Alexander R. Galloway, choreographer Cori Kresge and artist Roopa Vasudevan. The work examines the history of textile in relation to computing, alongside the artistic and political significance of what cannot be computed and draws on themes of the current exhibition, Technologies of Relation, which includes works by both Choi and Vasudevan.

Choi and Galloway’s presentation weaves together their long collaboration and ongoing conversation about the aesthetics and politics of code and textile, followed by a discussion moderated by Vasudevan. Kresge directs a participatory movement workshop that extends the conversation toward visceral and communal experience. Together, the project explores uncomputability as a creative framework for thinking about contemporary technologies and social systems, connecting the threads of cotton and fiber-optic cables running through data centers.

최태윤 Taeyoon Choi is an artist, writer, and educator who explores the poetics of technology and human relations, working with images, text, and code in collaboration with fellow artists and community members. In 2013, he co-founded the School for Poetic Computation, a hybrid school, residency, and research group exploring the intersections of code, design, hardware, and theory. His work has been presented at institutions including the Whitney Museum of American Art and MASS MoCA.

Cori Kresge is a New York-based dance artist whose practice moves across choreography, somatic inquiry, participatory structures, and poetry.
