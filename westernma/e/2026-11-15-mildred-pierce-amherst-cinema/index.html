Mildred Pierce

Amherst Cinema · Sun, Nov 15 · 1:30pm

In this gripping blend of melodrama and murder mystery, Joan Crawford plays a hard-working single mother who inches towards disaster as she starts a successful restaurant business to support her venomous daughter.

This adaptation of the novel by James M. Cain, a pioneer of hardboiled American crime fiction, won Crawford the Academy Award for Best Actress.

Directed by Michael Curtiz · NR
