Wicker

2 theaters (Amherst Cinema, Triplex Cinema) · Sun, Nov 15 · from 10:45am

An audacious comedic fable about sex, marriage, and the absurdities of social convention, WICKER tells the wholly original love story of an ostracized fisherwoman (Olivia Colman) who commissions a husband to be made by the local basketmaker (Peter Dinklage).

When her wicker husband (Alexander Skarsgård) proves more spectacular than she ever imagined, his soulful spirit and unique prowess awaken something inside her, setting off a wave of jealousy, fascination, and upheaval that threatens the very fabric of village life.

“Bouncing all the way to a truly perfect ending, WICKER is funny and freaky and sweetly romantic. It is also relentless in its conviction that the most destabilizing force in a society is a woman who dares to ask for what she wants.”Jeannette Catsoulis. A New York Times Critic's Pick!

Directed by Alex Huston Fischer, Eleanor Wilson · R
