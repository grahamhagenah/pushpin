Club Kid

Amherst Cinema · Sat, Nov 21 · 1:35pm, 4:25pm, 7:10pm, 9:55pm

A hard-partying New York City club promoter (writer/director Jordan Firstman) sees his whirlwind lifestyle upended by the arrival of the 10-year-old son he never knew he had.

“CLUB KID turns out to be a deeply sincere movie about the feeling of having stayed too long at the fair and what it takes to move forward, and how growing up can be both great and gutting. Everybody loves a good party. But life when the lights come up can be beautiful, too.” Alissa Wilkinson. A New York Times Critic's Pick!

Directed by Jordan Firstman · R
