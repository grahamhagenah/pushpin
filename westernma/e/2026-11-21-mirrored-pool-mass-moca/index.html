Mirrored Pool

Mass MoCA · Sat, Nov 21 · 7pm · $20–$56

Where do we fit in this endless universe? Glimpse the precious fragility of our life on Planet Earth as we peer into the Mirrored Pool.

Mirrored Pool is a puppetry and music collaboration between acclaimed puppeteer and La MaMa resident artist Tom Lee and musicians Lisa Kaplan and Matthew Duvall of the Grammy-award winning sextet Eighth Blackbird. The piece explores humanity’s place in our vast universe, showcasing the virtuosity of Eighth Blackbird’s music against the vital, human power of puppetry. Mirrored Pool takes special inspiration from astronomer Carl Sagan, whose work on the Voyager spacecrafts and the Golden Record are now the most distant human-made objects and sounds from our planet — currently 15.7 billion miles away from Earth.

Created and performed by Tom Lee, Lisa Kaplan, and Matthew Duvall Puppets & masks by Jaerin Son Additional puppet design by Averly Sheltraw Lighting by Seojung Jang Puppetry direction by Justin Perkins An Eighth Blackbird production in association with Yara Arts Group with support from the MAP Fund.
