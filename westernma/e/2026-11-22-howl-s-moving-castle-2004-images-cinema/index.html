Howl's Moving Castle (2004)

Images Cinema · Sun, Nov 22 · 11am · All ages

Young@Heart presents "Curl Up With a Good Film", a series highlighting classic films based on beloved children's books. This series is co-presented by the Milne Public Library. Sunday screenings will be presented in a sensory friendly environment with adjusted light and sound levels.

To ensure this presentation is as accessible to all-ages as possible, these screenings are presented with the English voice cast.

When Sophie, a shy young woman, is cursed with an old body by a spiteful witch, her only chance of breaking the spell lies with a self-indulgent yet insecure young wizard and his companions in his legged, walking castle.

Directed by Hayao Miyazaki · 1h 59m · PG
