Join us for a free screening of PAN'S LABYINRTH as part of our 20th Anniversary Kickoff, presenting three films from Amherst Cinema's first year. Free tickets are available the day of the celebration on a first come, first served basis. Arrive early for treats and games!

PAN'S LABYRINTH originally played at Amherst Cinema January through March of 2007.

An Academy Award–winning dark fable set five years after the end of the Spanish Civil War, PAN'S LABYRINTH encapsulates the rich visual style and genre-defying craft of Guillermo del Toro.

Eleven-year-old Ofelia (Ivana Baquero, in a mature and tender performance) comes face to face with the horrors of fascism when she and her pregnant mother are uprooted to the countryside, where her new stepfather (Sergi López), a sadistic captain in General Francisco Franco’s army, hunts down Republican guerrillas refusing to give up the fight.

The violent reality in which Ofelia lives merges seamlessly with her fantastical interior world when she meets a faun in a decaying labyrinth and is set on a strange, mythic journey that is at once terrifying and beautiful.

In his revisiting of this bloody period in Spanish history, del Toro creates a vivid depiction of the monstrosities of war infiltrating a child’s imagination and threatening the innocence of youth.

PAN'S LABYRINTH is presented in a new 4K restoration.
