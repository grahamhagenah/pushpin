Showgirls

Triplex Cinema · Mon, Nov 23 · 8pm

Queer Cinema Club Berkshires and No Comply Foods are teaming up for a screening of the ultimate so-bad-its-amazing cult movie: Paul Verhoeven's Showgirls! Arriving in Las Vegas with nothing more than a suitcase and a dream, Nomi (Elizabeth Berkley) works her way up from a seedy stripclub to dancing at the high-profile Stardust Hotel, with an eye on taking the lead role from marquee dancer Cristal (Gina Gershon) in this erotically excessive spin on All About Eve.

Directed by Paul Verhoeven · 2h 11m · NC-17
