CCM: A Night of Worship

HOPE Center for the Arts · Sat, Nov 28 · 7pm · $30–$50

True Vine Radio invites you to C.C.M — Christ‑Centered Music: A Night of Worship, a powerful worship concert featuring Isaac Cruz, Kathy Stuart, and worship leaders united to uplift the name of Jesus.

Headlined by Christian Contemporary Artist Charles Weems, known for “Slow Me Down” and his chart‑topping new release “I Will Follow,” this worship experience is positioned to draw youth and families from Springfield and surrounding communities. Believers from across Western Massachusetts will gather for an atmosphere of worship, unity, thankfulness, and spiritual renewal.
