Mind Left Body

CitySpace · Sat, Nov 28 · 8pm · 21+

Mind Left Body & CitySpace Present: Back on the Bus at CitySpace November 28| Doors 7pm, Show 8pm The performance will feature immersive lighting visualizations by ThreeThinx by the MORPH Collective; a special screening of community submitted video produced by Easthampton Film Festival; and a mini pop-up market featuring local artists.

Stay tuned for ways to get involved!

21+ w/valid ID

This show is standing room only— please reach out if you have accessibility needs.

Mind Left Body: 1976— Back on the Bus at CitySpace, the show explores the Dead’s return to the road after their 20 month hiatus.

CitySpace Partners in Performance is made possible by the Community Foundation of Western Massachusetts, Massachusetts Cultural Council, Finck and Perras Insurance, Delap Real Estate, and Easthampton Animal Hospital. Thank you for your support!
