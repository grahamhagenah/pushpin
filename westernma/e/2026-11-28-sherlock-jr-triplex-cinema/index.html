Sherlock Jr.

Triplex Cinema · Sat, Nov 28 · 7pm · All ages

Witness some of the most mind-boggling stunts — and biggest laughs — in movie history when we screen Buster Keaton’s Sherlock, Jr. with a live score performed by Joanna Seaton and Donald Sosin on November 28th! A benefit screening presented in partnership with Christ Trinity Church of Sheffield to raise money for Berkshire Bounty, join us for a program that includes a singalong of songs from 1924, a slideshow of famous Sherlocks, an additional Buster Keaton short film, and Keaton’s iconic feature about a projectionist who dreams he’s a world famous detective.

Directed by Buster Keaton · 1h 20m · All Ages
