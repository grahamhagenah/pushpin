TLI Jazz: Ted Rosenthal Quartet featuring Terell Stafford—The Good Old Days

Tanglewood · Sat, Nov 28 · 8pm

Rosenthal and his trio will be joined by trumpeter Terell Stafford in a program featuring music from Rosenthal's most recent CD release, The Good Old Days. The music will include standards and originals that cover earlier styles of jazz: swing, early bop, and ragtime.

"Virtuosic and light-stepping, Rosenthal and his bandmates make classic, classy music with deep roots in the jazz tradition." —AllAboutJazz "These fresh and interesting interpretations of classic formative jazz styles make very enjoyable listening." —Jazz Journal During November and the first half of December, TLI concerts will serve as a drop-off location for this year’s U. S. Marine Corps Reserve Toys for Tots Program in coordination with the Massachusetts State Police. Guests are asked to please bring a new, unwrapped, non-violent toy to drop in the collection boxes located in the Linde Center lobby.
