Second Nature: Documentary Screening with Dame Judi Dentures & Friends

CitySpace · Sun, Nov 29 · 4pm

Second Nature: Documenting Queer Gender and Sexuality in the Animal World A Documentary Produced and Narrated by Elliot Page Sunday, November 29 4-6:30pm The Rooted Co-op and Dame Judi Dentures Productions present a free community screening of Second Nature, Elliot Page’s funny, heartwarming documentary about queer and trans diversity in the animal world. The afternoon will begin with drag performances by Dame Judi Dentures and other local artists, followed by the film screening, and then a reception with resource tables from local LGBTQ+-affirming organizations including TransHealth and Translate Gender. Sliding scale tickets (with a free option!) support event costs and free screenings of the film for LGBTQIA+ youth in states where inclusive sex ed is banned. All are welcome. We highly recommend reserving a spot in advance, as capacity is limited. Reserve your spot here.
