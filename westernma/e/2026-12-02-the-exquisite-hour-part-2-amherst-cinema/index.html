The Exquisite Hour, Part 2

Amherst Cinema · Wed, Dec 2 · 7pm · Free

This screening is free to the general public. Tickets are available only at the Amherst Cinema box office on a first-come, first-served basis. They can be picked up in advance during the Cinema’s regular box office hours, or at the door the evening of the screening.

Following an inaugural program in February 2026, this screening features a set of 16mm experimental films that harness the beauties and intensities of photochemical filmmaking.

“The Exquisite Hour” takes its name from a film originally completed by Phil Solomon in 1989, which was intended, in part, as a rumination on the impending death of celluloid film. Nearly forty years later, the filmic medium is still alive due in no small measure to an experimental film tradition that has been expanding the aesthetic capacities of photochemical filmmaking for decades.

The second edition of “The Exquisite Hour” showcases six contributions to this tradition that were created by artists from different generations and locales. While these works employ varied formats, techniques, and methodologies, they all share an investment in the future of analog film culture, which this program seeks to affirm and extend.

Films will be projected from a mix of 16mm and digital.

Curated by Abraham Ravett, Professor of Film and Photography, Hampshire College, and Josh Guilford, Assistant Professor of English in Film and Media Studies, Amherst College.

Presented in partnership with the English Department and Film and Media Studies Program at Amherst College.
