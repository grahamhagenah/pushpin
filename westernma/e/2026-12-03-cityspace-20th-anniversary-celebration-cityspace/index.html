CitySpace 20th Anniversary Celebration

CitySpace · Thu, Dec 3 · 6pm

Thursday, December 3, 2026 | 6–9 pm CitySpace at Old Town Hall, 43 Main Street, Easthampton Celebrate 20 years of CitySpace at Future Forward, a one-night-only, pull-out-all-the-stops celebration of Old Town Hall’s past, present, and very big future.

Expect delectable food, music by DJ Bex Taylor, the incomparable Dame Judi Denchers as emcee, live entertainment, fabulous auction items, raffles, surprises, and plenty of reasons to raise a glass. We’re celebrating two decades of artists, performances, wild ideas, community, and the people who helped transform a historic building into a place where magical and creative things happen.

Twenty years deserves a party. This is that party.

We are grateful to the businesses and organizations who are making Future Forward possible including Chemetal, KW Home and Galaxy.

Sliding scale tickets are available starting at $35 ($37.13 with fees). All ticket levels include admission and food. Choose the level that works for you, or give a little more to help support. CitySpace and what comes next.

Cash bar available. Festive attire inspired by the past, present, or future is encouraged (not required).
