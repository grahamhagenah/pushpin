StageFright: A Tribute to The Last Watlz

Stone Church · Thu, Dec 3 · 8pm

STAGEFRIGHT brings a very special performance of The Last Waltz, which features an incredible collective of musicians reimagining the music, energy, and collaborative magic of The Last Waltz and The Band. These tribute performances have become beloved events at Union Pool in Brooklyn, NY and venues across the country. Expect timeless songs, rotating performers, a full horn section and an ode to the greatest concert film ever made.
