$22 advanced, $25 day of show General Admission (Standing) - Doors open at 7PM Eliza McLamb's warm, intimate songs weave through confessional folk and clamorous alt-rock in a way that recalls contemporary indie heroes like Julia Jacklin and Lucy Dacus. After building a grassroots fan base through social media, the North Carolina native released her debut EP, Salt Circle, in 2022. She soon joined the roster of Royal Mountain Records, which released her full-length debut, Going Through It, in 2024. Her heightened success led to McLamb leaving Binchtopia, the popular podcast she had co-hosted since 2021, to focus exclusively on her music career. Her sophomore album, Good Story, appeared in 2025, following her first headlining tour.

For Lily Konigsberg, it seemed that the path to success was laid out in front of her. Years of hustling in the underground music scene, both with her long running band Palberta and under her own name, garnered her a sizable cult following. Through rigorous touring and word of mouth, she earned "your favorite songwriter's favorite songwriter" status and was on track to being recognized as a force of nature in the wider music world with the release of her critically lauded debut album (Lily We Need to Talk Now which came out on Wharf Cat in 2021), and her 2022 album as a duo called My Idea with Nate Amos of This Is Lorelei/Water From Your Eyes.

Since 2022 Konigsberg has been silent, but today she is returning to share a new song called “Free Byrd,” her first single in nearly 5 years. The product of time spent working with her longtime friend and collaborator Hugo Stanley (Palm, Frankie Cosmos), who she enlisted as both a producer and instrumentalist, the song is a fitting introduction to a new creative era for Konigsberg. It's an ode to the bittersweet nature of starting again and of the sacrifices required to do so, serving as both empowering breakup anthem and a swan song for a tumultuous past life. An infusion of Lily's signature earworm-generating pop sensibility and Hugo's punchy arrangements, "Free Byrd" makes a convincing last-second bid for song of the summer, and serves as an immensely promising preview of things to come.
