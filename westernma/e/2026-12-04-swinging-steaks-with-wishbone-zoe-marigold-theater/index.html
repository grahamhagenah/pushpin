Swinging Steaks with Wishbone Zoe

Marigold Theater · Fri, Dec 4 · 8pm

Join us Friday, December 4th for the return of Boston’s own, Swinging Steaks, plus a killer opening set by local legend, Wishbone Zoe!

🎤 Boston’s own Swinging Steaks were doing “Americana” before it even had a name. Signed to the legendary Capricorn Records in the early ’90s, they broke out with Southside of the Sky — earning national praise, two AAA Top 10 singles, and stages alongside heavyweights like Los Lobos, The Band, John Fogerty, and Lynyrd Skynyrd.

With seven albums under their belt and decades of road-tested chemistry, the Steaks are still one of New England’s most beloved live acts. Expect killer harmonies, sharp songwriting, and that unmistakable mix of rockers, ballads, and mandolin-driven Americana that keeps fans coming back for more. A perfect night out for anyone who loves roots rock done right.

Wishbone Zoe is the multidisciplinary project of Western Massachusetts songwriter, multi-instrumentalist, animator, and visual artist Zosia Kochanski. Once described by Pitchfork as a “junkyard-rocker,” Kochanski’s music has moved freely between experimental folk, power pop, noisy rock, digital collage, and stripped-down acoustic songwriting, often incorporating homemade sounds, animation, and performance art. Recent releases like 2025’s Blue Distances and 2026’s Psyche’s Romp continue a catalog defined less by genre than by restless experimentation and a distinctly DIY sensibility.
