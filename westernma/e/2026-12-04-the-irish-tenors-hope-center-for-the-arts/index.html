The Irish Tenors

HOPE Center for the Arts · Fri, Dec 4 · 7pm · $65–$90

For more than a quarter century, The Irish Tenors have thrilled audiences around the world with their unmistakable blend of soaring classical voices, Irish charm, and heartfelt storytelling. Since first joining voices in 1998, they have sold out iconic venues including Radio City Music Hall, Carnegie Hall, the Sydney Opera House, and The Hollywood Bowl, while becoming beloved PBS favorites through acclaimed specials such as Ellis Island. With millions of albums sold and a repertoire that effortlessly spans treasured Irish ballads, popular standards, and stirring contemporary favorites, The Irish Tenors have become one of the most successful Irish touring acts in the world.

This December, they return with The Irish Tenors: A Family Christmas, a holiday celebration that has become a cherished tradition for audiences across the country. Featuring beloved Christmas classics, inspirational seasonal favorites, and the rich harmonies that made their We Three Kings holiday album a perennial favorite, this festive program captures both the joy and reverence of the season. Expect an evening filled with warmth, laughter, nostalgia, and the unmistakable magic of holiday music shared with the people you love.

Led by the extraordinary voices of Anthony Kearns, Ronan Tynan, and Patrick Hyland, The Irish Tenors bring dazzling vocal power, emotional depth, and a touch of Irish sparkle to every performance. Whether delivering the spine-tingling beauty of “Danny Boy,” the festive exuberance of Christmas favorites, or uplifting songs that bring audiences to their feet, their concerts are unforgettable celebrations of music, memory, and togetherness. Gather the whole family and make this spectacular holiday event part of your Christmas tradition.
