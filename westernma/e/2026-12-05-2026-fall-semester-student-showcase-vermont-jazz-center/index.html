2026 Fall Semester Student Showcase

Vermont Jazz Center · Sat, Dec 5 · 12pm

The Vermont Jazz Center (VJC) invites the community to celebrate the culmination of its fall semester at the Student Showcase on Saturday, December 5, from 12:00 PM (noon) to 5:00 PM. Over the course of the afternoon, ten ensembles will share the results of three months of study, rehearsal, and collaboration. Listeners can expect a mix of jazz standards, Latin jazz, samba percussion, vocal harmonies, improvisational forays and spirited tunes from the Blue Note era.

This performance highlights the depth of talent within the VJC’s education program, bringing together musicians of all ages and backgrounds. The event is free and open to the public; a suggested $5 donation supports the VJC Scholarship Fund.

This year’s Student Showcase will take place in conjunction with the Cotton Mill Open Studio & Holiday Sale.
