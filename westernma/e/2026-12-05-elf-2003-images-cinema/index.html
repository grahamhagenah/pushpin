Elf (2003)

Images Cinema · Sat, Dec 5 · 12pm, 2:30pm

Shown as part of the Williamstown Holiday Walk. Presented for free to the community by the Williamstown Cultural District and Images Cinema.

When young Buddy falls into Santa's gift sack on Christmas Eve, he's transported back to the North Pole and raised as a toy-making elf by Santa's helpers. But as he grows into adulthood, he can't shake the nagging feeling that he doesn't belong. Buddy vows to visit Manhattan and find his real dad, a workaholic publisher.

Directed by Jon Favreau · 1h 37m · PG
