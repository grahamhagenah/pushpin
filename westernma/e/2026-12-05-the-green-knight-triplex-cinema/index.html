The Green Knight

Triplex Cinema · Sat, Dec 5 · 7pm

Q&A with costume designer Malgosia Turzanska!

We kick off the holiday season with The Green Knight, David Lowry’s stunning adaptation of the classic Arthurian legend, followed by a conversation with costume designer Malgosia Turzanska!

When the mythical Green Knight (Ralph Ineson) barges into King Arthur’s court on Christmas day, only Arthur’s headstrong nephew Sir Gawain (Dev Patel) accepts his challenge. Forced to face the knight the following Christmas, Gawain sets off on a journey filled with ghosts, giants, thieves, and schemers that tests his character and discover his true strength.

After the screening, we’ll be joined by Academy Award nominated costume designer Malgosia Turzanska to discuss the process of combining historical accuracy and fantasy elements to create the movie’s signature look, and what it took to bring her designs to life.

Directed by David Lowery · 2h 10m · R
