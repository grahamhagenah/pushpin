Albany Berkshire Ballet Presents: The Nutcracker

Colonial Theatre · Sun, Dec 6 · 1pm

Celebrate the magic of the holiday season as Albany Berkshire Ballet (ABB) returns with its annual tour of The Nutcracker. Choreographed by ABB’s Artistic Director Madeline Cantarella Culpo, this beloved production features lavish sets and scenery by Stockbridge native and renowned theatre and film set designer Carl Sprague, with costumes by Sam Meredith. Young local dance students join professional ABB dancers in special children’s roles, sharing the stage with a company of performers from across the United States and around the world.
