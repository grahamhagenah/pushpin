Holiday Vespers

Mount Holyoke · Sun, Dec 6 · 4pm, 7:30pm · Free

Join the Mount Holyoke Choral Forces, Jazz Ensembles, Klezmer Ensemble, Flute Choir, and the English Handbell Ensemble for their annual performance of Holiday Vespers.

Featuring music by Betty Bertaux, Claude Debussy, Katerina Gimon, Stephen Paulus, Erica Phare-Bergh, Josef Spivak, John Tavener, and annual holiday carols and singalongs.

This event is free and open to the public.

There are two performances: one at 4 pm and at 7:30 pm.

Early arrival is recommended to guarantee seating.
