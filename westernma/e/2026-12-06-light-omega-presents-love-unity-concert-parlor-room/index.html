Light Omega Presents: Love/Unity Concert

Parlor Room · Sun, Dec 6 · 2pm

The cost of admission is a donation of any amount you choose. Donations will go to One Spirit, a nonprofit organization supporting the Lakota of Pine Ridge since 2005, and they will be grateful for your generosity.

If you sign up for a recurring donation, your future donations will go to the Village of Light Ashram's Feed the Hungry Program - you can read more about Feed the Hungry at You will receive a donation receipt via email - this will serve as your ticket. Please print or show a copy at the door.

The Poster Art was created in acrylic paint on canvas by 12-year-old Isabella Belt, this original artwork depicts the love and strength carried between generations of women. Isabella is from Wagmíza Wakpála, “Corn Creek,” the community known today as Allen on the Pine Ridge Reservation.
