Five College West African Music Ensemble

Mount Holyoke · Tue, Dec 8 · 7:30pm · Free

Students from MUS161 and MUS261 will perform music from the Gbe speaking people of southern Ghana, Togo and Benin on traditional instruments.

The concert will include musical selections from Gahu and Adjogbo, and the performance will feature guest artists from Ghana and Benin.

All are welcome, the concert is free and open to the public.
