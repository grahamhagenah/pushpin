Member Discovery Series: Marianne Beaulieu

Iron Horse · Tue, Dec 8 · 7pm · $18

Currently based out of her hometown of Montreal, Marianne Beaulieu is an indie folk singer-songwriter whose goal has always been to bring comfort through her music. From the age of 5, she started learning the piano, before taking singing classes in her teenage years and teaching herself the guitar. She soon learns to use music as an outlet and as a means to silence the world and sing her troubles away.

Arden Lloyd blends emotionally poignant lyrics with precise production to create music that pulls you in and doesn’t let you go. Her voice and storytelling are at the heart of her performances, leaving audiences with a lingering sense of connection. She’s played extensively in Boston and NYC, as well as in her hometown in Western MA - everywhere from cozy house shows to sold-out headline events. In 2020, she won the National YoungArts Competition for Voice: Singer-Songwriter, and was named a 2021 U.S. Presidential Scholar in the Arts, one of 20 in the nation. Arden’s recent releases from her upcoming mixtape showcase her evolving sound and worldbuilding, exploring intimacy, sense of self, and contradiction.
