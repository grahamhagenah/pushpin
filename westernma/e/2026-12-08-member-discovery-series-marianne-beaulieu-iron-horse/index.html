Member Discovery Series: Marianne Beaulieu

Iron Horse · Tue, Dec 8 · 7pm · $18

Currently based out of her hometown of Montreal, Marianne Beaulieu is an indie folk singer-songwriter whose goal has always been to bring comfort through her music. From the age of 5, she started learning the piano, before taking singing classes in her teenage years and teaching herself the guitar. She soon learns to use music as an outlet and as a means to silence the world and sing her troubles away.
