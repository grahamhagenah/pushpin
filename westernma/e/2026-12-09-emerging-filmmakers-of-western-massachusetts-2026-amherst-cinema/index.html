Emerging Filmmakers of Western Massachusetts 2026

Amherst Cinema · Wed, Dec 9 · 7pm · Free

This screening is free to the general public. Tickets are available only at the Amherst Cinema box office on a first-come, first-served basis. They can be picked up in advance during the Cinema’s regular box office hours, or at the door the evening of the screening.

The Easthampton Film Festival co-presents a lively selection of narrative and documentary shorts made by Western Massachusetts-based filmmakers, offering audiences an opportunity to experience stories rooted in places, people, and issues they know firsthand.

A panel of featured filmmakers joins us for a post-screening conversation led by Kt Baldassaro, Head of the Easthampton Film Festival Horror Film Subcommittee, Festival Moderator, and Public Ambassador.

Presented in partnership with the Easthampton Film Festival.
