Join us for the next installment of the NXT Rockumentary Film Series.

Filmed in December 1968, this British concert film brings together leading rock and blues musicians for a unique musical spectacle staged in a makeshift circus tent. Blending live performances with a playful, carnival-like atmosphere, the production features a diverse lineup of artists, ranging from energetic rock bands to blues performers and an experimental one-time supergroup. The event combines music, theatrical flair, and the spirit of the late 1960s counterculture.

Originally intended for television broadcast, the film was withheld from release because of concerns about the quality of the performances and the exhaustion of the musicians after an exceptionally long filming session. It also captures the final public appearance of one founding member of the featured band. Despite remaining unseen for decades, the film eventually received an official release in 1996, becoming a valuable record of an extraordinary moment in rock music history.
