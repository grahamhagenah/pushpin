SEASON 4 FINALE: RETURN OF THE CHILDLESS CAT LADIES Riding the blue wave, the Return of the Childless Cat Ladies will descend upon Marigold Theater for our Season 4 finale. We’ll revisit the inquiry into the intersection of feminism, memes, and cats as explored in Skye Trivia Vol. 22 (October 2024), with new angles, new questions, and new possibilities for engagement from our community in the form of music, skits, stories, and more. Costumes to match the theme welcome, or simply wear blue, as we celebrate 4 years of Skye community. See you December 9!

Wednesday, December 9th · 84 Cottage St Easthampton Ma · Doors 7:00 PM · Show 7:30 PM · FREE!

We used to be called Skye’s Trivia Variety Show but we changed the name to reflect that it’s not pub quiz trivia night, even if a game of questions is involved. Each month we explore a different theme through music, skits, storytelling, games, or other activities as conducted or performed by those in attendance, our Skye community. But we also play a trivia game. Our goal isn’t to test you or incite cutthroat competition, but rather to playfully engage the theme. You’ll laugh, you might cry, and you’ll definitely learn something.

Teams of any size, play alone, play casually, or get into it. We’ll introduce you to some new people to play with if you’d like.

If you like to compete, Marigold gift cards of $50 and $25 for the top two scoring teams, and in Season 4, we have introduced the legacy Popcorn Noir Traveling Trivia Trophy from an alternate timeline. Winning team takes it home for the month, with license to adorn it as they see fit.

SKYE’S QUEER CURIOSITIES is a collaborative queer-run production and community event, written by Harper Doherty and hosted by Ivy Davida and Elana Rosičková. The three of us are a “system,” three distinct entities sharing one very non-binary, shapeshifted body (they/them). The show is named after our octopus Skye, in a nod to versatility and the focus on community outreach.
