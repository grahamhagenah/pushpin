Joybubbles

Amherst Cinema · Thu, Dec 10 · 7pm

Director Rachael J. Morrison, a Hampshire College alum, joins us for a post-film discussion.

This screening is free to Amherst Cinema Members.

JOYBUBBLES is a documentary about a boy who discovers he can control the global telephone system by whistling a magic tone.

Born blind and yearning for connection, his early obsession with the telephone sparks a subculture that shapes the future of hacking and technology.

An official selection of the 2026 Sundance Film Festival.

Please note that this film is presented with open audio description and open captions.
