Nicolás Emden

Parlor Room · Thu, Dec 10 · 7:30pm · $24

Nicolás Emden is a Chilean singer-songwriter based in Boston, making music where South American folk roots meet Americana, indie folk, and rock. His songs are built for collective experience: concerts become participatory spaces where audiences sing, connect, and break language and cultural barriers together. A four-time New England Music Award nominee, he won World Act of the Year in 2023.
