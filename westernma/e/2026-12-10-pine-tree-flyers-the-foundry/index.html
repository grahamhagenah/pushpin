Pine Tree Flyers

The Foundry · Thu, Dec 10 · 7:30pm

Pine Tree Flyers is a quartet bringing renewed energy and respect to the traditional music of New England. Featuring fiddle, accordion, guitar, and piano, the group blends deep roots in contra dance music with influences from Irish, Scottish, and Québécois traditions to create a vibrant and distinctly Northeastern folk sound.
