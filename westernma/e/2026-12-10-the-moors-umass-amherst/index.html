The Moors

UMass Amherst · Thu, Dec 10 · 10am · $7–$19

A hapless governess, a mysterious hero, scheming siblings, and the bleak setting of the windswept countryside: Jen Silverman’s The Moors has all the trapping of the best gothic yarns written by folks like the Brontë sisters. At once satire and homage to this source material, this dark and funny play also introduces us to two surprisingly literate animals and their star-crossed love. This richly weird set-up offers a unique lens to explore the things that motivate us: love, power, the search for connection.

Recommended for ages 16 and up due to adult situations, violence and gore, and references to sexual assault THE MOORS is presented by arrangement with Concord Theatricals on behalf of Samuel French, Inc.
