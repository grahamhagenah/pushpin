Cinderella by The United European Ballet Company

Symphony Hall Springfield · Fri, Dec 11 · 7pm

Step into the magical world of Cinderella, one of the most beloved fairytales of all time, brought to life in a dazzling Broadway-style ballet production filled with beauty, humor, romance, and unforgettable theatrical magic.

This enchanting production by the United European Ballet tells the timeless story of a kind and gentle young woman whose courage, grace, and belief in love lead her from a life of hardship to a royal ball and a dream come true. With expressive choreography, colorful characters, elegant storytelling, and the unforgettable romance of Cinderella and the Prince, this ballet is a perfect experience for audiences of all ages.

Set to the magnificent score of Sergei Prokofiev, one of the greatest composers of the 20th century, Cinderella combines classical ballet tradition with the charm and excitement of a grand theatrical fairytale. Audiences will enjoy elaborate hand-painted sets, exquisite costumes created by European designers, and a brilliant cast of international dancers who bring this beloved story to life with artistry, emotion, and grace.

Promoted by Classical Arts Entertainment under the leadership of Michael Art, this family-friendly production invites you to experience the magic of live theater at its finest. Bring your family, friends, loved ones, and children, and enjoy a beautiful evening filled with music, dance, laughter, romance, and wonder. Cinderella is more than a ballet. It’s a celebration of dreams, kindness, love, and the belief that magic can happen when the heart stays true.

Ballet in two acts with one intermission.

Suitable for ages 3 and up.
