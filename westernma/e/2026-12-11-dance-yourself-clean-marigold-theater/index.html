Marigold Presents: Dance Yourself Clean, an open mix vinyl dance party with DJMentalDrift and Keat Sweat Remember dancing at clubs in the 80s and 90s? The internet hadn’t been invented. People didn’t have cell phones. Fog machines were weird, but fun. You’d dance for hours and hours and feel better. That’s the vibe. We’ll do our best to recreate it. Vinyl records, of course.

No one turned away for lack of funds, but suggested donation of $10-$30.
