Wunnerful Show

Marigold Theater · Fri, Dec 11 · 6pm

Friday, December 11

Marigold Presents: Wunnerfull Show, a variety show organized in the spirit of Lawrence Welk 🪗 And a one and a two!

An evening MC’ed by Vanessa Brewster, featuring songs by Lousie Coombe and Lisa Bastoni, live tap dancing with Dorothy VanDeCarr, original puppets created by name secrets and Two Ghosts, sing-along selections from The Point, and a formal Lawrence Welk dance hour (not even close to all polka) with 91.1 WMUA’s DJMentalDrift.

Also, what a night to pick up holiday gifts at our pop-up holiday market with LOOKY HERE, Two Ghosts, name secrets, Public Interest Postal Service, and more!

Frilly dresses and flouncy tops encouraged. All creatures welcome. No one turned away for lack of funds, but contributions directly support the independent artists making the evening possible!
