Amherst Ballet presents The Snow Maiden

HOPE Center for the Arts · Sat, Dec 12 · 2pm

Amherst Ballet’s beloved winter show, ‘The Snow Maiden’, returns. This magical production invites you to the land of Father Frost, full of swirling snowflakes and glittering fairies, where we meet the Snow Maiden on her search for love beyond her wintry realm. Join her quest in this heartwarming tale of longing and friendship, amidst the turning of winter into spring. Set to selections from three works by Peter Tchaikovsky: The Serenade for Strings, Symphony No. 1 in G-minor (Winter Reverie), and Incidental Music for the play, “The Snow Maiden,” and presented by students from the Amherst Ballet School and our volunteer company, this production is an excellent opportunity for young audience members to experience the magic of ballet for the first time. Several characters from the show will be available for photographs during intermission, and our director will give backstage tours to show how we bring a production together following each show.
