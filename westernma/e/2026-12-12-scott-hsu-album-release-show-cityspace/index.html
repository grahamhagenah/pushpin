Scott Hsu Album Release Show

CitySpace · Sat, Dec 12 · 7:30pm

Scott Hsu, December 12 · Pay it Forward 2026

American Scott Hsu 許多藝 is releasing his new rock/pop album, 新生 Hsin Sheng, and celebrating with a release show! 新生 means “new life” or “rebirth.” Sung in Mandarin and English, the album explores love, hope, identity, and growing into something new.

Join Scott on Saturday, Dec 12 at CitySpace in Easthampton for a night of bilingual rock and pop. All ages are welcome, so bring the whole family!

Scott Hsu, is a Taiwanese-American rock/pop/electronica artist from Amherst Massachusetts. His songs are passionate and heartfelt, rooted in authenticity, gravitating toward themes of love, nostalgia, transformation, and redemption. With lyrics in English and Mandarin, he blends his cross-cultural heritage into his music to reflect his unique identity—global, hybrid, and personal. Scott’s latest singles include the dreamy Chinglish pop/punk song "陪你 / I Just Wanna Be With You" (2025) and the plucky pop Americana tune "You Got My Love" (2024). His discography includes his acoustic album Confessional EP (2025), and Cliffs EP (2024), which features the catchy synth rock single "Play"
