The Muppet Christmas Carol

Amherst Cinema · Sat, Dec 12 · 12:45pm · $5

Please note that passes cannot be redeemed for this screening.

The Muppets put their spin on the tale of Ebenezer Scrooge (Michael Caine), a Christmas-hating miser visited by three spirits.

They foretell his future and share secrets from his past and present, helping to change his view on life.

Brian Henson, the son of Muppet founder Jim Henson, took over directing duties after the untimely death of his father for THE MUPPET CHRISTMAS CAROL, a unique re-telling of the Charles Dickens tale.

The $5 Family Films series is supported in part by Greenfield Savings Bank and a grant from the Amherst Cultural Council, a local agency which is supported by the Mass Cultural Council, a state agency.
