TLI Jazz: Marissa Mulder—Merry and Bright

Tanglewood · Sat, Dec 12 · 7pm

Three-time MAC Award and Noel Coward/Julie Wilson Award-Winner, Marissa Mulder performs her acclaimed Christmas show Merry and Bright at TLI. You’ll be enchanted by Marissa’s jazzy and endearing renditions of Christmas favorites like “Santa Baby,” “Winter Wonderland,” and “Have Yourself A Merry Little Christmas,” along with beloved jazz standards.

During November and the first half of December, TLI concerts will serve as a drop-off location for this year’s U. S. Marine Corps Reserve Toys for Tots Program in coordination with the Massachusetts State Police. Guests are asked to please bring a new, unwrapped, non-violent toy to drop in the collection boxes located in the Linde Center lobby.
