Formed in 2010 in Atlanta, Georgia, WHORES. has established themselves as one of the most successful and motivated bands in the noise-rock genre today. Elements of Helmet, Melvins, and the Amphetamine Reptile roster can be heard throughout their catalog, but Whores. are no regurgitated throwback act. Through intense, cathartic live performances and the perfected aggressive tenacity present on their recordings, the band has gained a fervent following sure to do nothing but grow in the years to come TREE was formed in 1986 as a punk hardcore band in Westwood Massachusetts. The band played the club scene through the 80's and 90's in and around Boston and New England gaining popularity. Tree, always known to advocate for political and social justice, came to partner with MasCann ( and help spearhead the legalization movement in Massachusetts. Tree and Sam Black Church would be known to alternate headlining spots at the annual "Freedom Rally" on the Boston Commons, playing to extensive audiences of 100k+, and advocating for legalization. Tree's song "God Grows Grass" became the anthem for the movement.

Tree's recording "Downsizing the American Dream", recorded and engineered by Roy Z (Iron Maiden, Judas Priest), received extensive airplay and elevated the band to national status, facilitating the band to tour the country and be added to the Vans Warped Tour and the annual River Rave shows. The band took a hiatus in the late 2000's but reformed in 2021 and has been active since in performing, writing and recording new material. The band won the Boston Music Awards in 2025 for "Best Boston Hardcore Punk Band", and is releasing a new album "Missing in America" through Creep Records in November 2026.
