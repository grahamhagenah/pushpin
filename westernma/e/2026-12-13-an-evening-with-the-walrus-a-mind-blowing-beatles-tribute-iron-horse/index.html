An Evening with The Walrus - A mind blowing Beatles Tribute

Iron Horse · Sun, Dec 13 · 7pm · $30

"The Beatles get a mind-blowing tribute."

The Walrus has been faithfully and energetically recreating the music of The Beatles (proper and solo works) since 2002.

All members are active recording and touring artists and have been featured in multiple glowing Rolling Stone articles and reviews.

Core members Spencer Albee, Zach Jones, Sean Morin, Jon Roods, Anna Lombard, and Andrew Hodgkins dig in to the music of The Beatles with as much reverence as possible, and lean in to the rock energy of the live performance.

In short, this is a rock show.

No wigs, no accents, and just a little too loud.
