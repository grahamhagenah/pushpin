Day Drooler

Stone Church · Sun, Dec 13 · 7pm

Day Drooler is a five-piece indie folk rock band formed at Dartmouth.

Ever had a daydream that knocks you out? Now your mouth is open, you're drooling in public, you wake up, and everyone’s watching you. You are now one of us, welcome. We write jammy songs that start soft and get loud. They are a fly on the wall— telling stories about place and family, belonging, and not belonging.

This December (12-22), they are on tour through Brattleboro (Stone Church 12/13), Hanover (Sawtooth 12/15), Boston (O'Brien's 12/16 & The Lilypad 12/22), and NYC (Mercury Lounge 12/20).
