Spirit of Springfield - Free Holiday Concert

Symphony Hall Springfield · Sun, Dec 13 · 2pm

Springfield Symphony Hall is filled with the sounds of the holiday and patriotism for the annual concert by the 215th Army Band of the Massachusetts Army National Guard. They bring holiday cheer, lift spirits and get the audience singing-a-long during the free concert in Springfield Symphony Hall.

It is a wonderful opportunity for families to experience the grandeur of Springfield Symphony Hall and the musical talents for the 40-members of the military band. Their performance features a broad spectrum of material.

Sunday, December 7, 2025 · 2pm · 34 Court Street · Admission - Free with Ticket · Tickets Available November 27, 2024
