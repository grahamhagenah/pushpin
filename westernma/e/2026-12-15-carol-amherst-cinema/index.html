Carol

Amherst Cinema · Tue, Dec 15 · 7pm

Therese Belivet (Rooney Mara), a young woman in her 20s, is a clerk working in a Manhattan department store and dreaming of a more fulfilling life when she meets Carol (Cate Blanchett), an alluring woman trapped in a loveless, convenient marriage.

After an immediate connection sparks between them, the innocence of their first encounter dims and their connection deepens. As conventional norms of the time challenge their undeniable attraction, an honest story emerges to reveal the resilience of the heart in the face of change.

In an adaptation of Patricia Highsmith’s seminal novel The Price of Salt, CAROL follows two women from very different backgrounds who find themselves in an unexpected love affair in 1950s New York. With much of the story set during the holiday season, this drama has become a Christmas-adjacent classic for grown-ups.

CAROL is directed by Todd Haynes (FAR FROM HEAVEN, I'M NOT THERE) and written by Phyllis Nagy, with moving performances from Blanchett, Mara, Sarah Paulson, Kyle Chandler, Jake Lacy, and Cory Michael Smith.

"At once ardent and analytical, cerebral and swooning, CAROL is a study in human magnetism, in the physics and optics of eros. With sparse dialogue and restrained drama, the film is a symphony of angles and glances, of colors and shadows." A.O. Scott. A New York Times Critics' Pick!
