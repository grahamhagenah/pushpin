$25 advanced, $25 day of show, $60 VIP General Admission (Standing) - Doors open at 7PM Horror punk legends BLITZKID return to the road this fall in support of their long-awaited new studio album, MODERN HEL, their first full length release in sixteen years.

Formed in 1997, Blitzkid quickly became one of the most influential bands in modern horror punk and has remained one of the genre’s leading exponents. Known for their driving punk energy, dark atmosphere, and unforgettable melodic hooks, the band has built an international following through decades of touring across the globe.

From Wave Gotik Treffen to Wacken Open Air, Blitzkid’s versatility has allowed them to perform at some of the world’s most notable genre festivals, demonstrating the band’s unique ability to connect with audiences across multiple underground scenes.

With MODERN HEL, Blitzkid enters a powerful new chapter. While the band’s signature haunting melodies and anthemic choruses remain firmly intact, the new material pushes their sound into more aggressive territory, incorporating a blistering fusion of thrash and hardcore to a degree the band has never explored before.

The result is a blistering blend of driving riffs and haunting hooks that have defined Blitzkid for nearly three decades.

MODERN HEL TOUR brings this new era to stages across the United States from October through December, performing classics spanning their catalog alongside brand new material from MODERN HEL.

A limited VIP Meet and Greet experience will be available at all shows, including early entry, exclusive merchandise, and a special pre show acoustic performance.
