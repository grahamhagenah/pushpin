Dandling

Parlor Room · Wed, Dec 16 · 7:30pm · $24

Fiddler and singer Lissa Schneckenburger teams up with percussive dance innovator Nic Gareiss and multi-instrumentalist Garrett Cameron for a dynamic new collaboration rooted in tradition and injected with playful creativity. The trio calls themselves Dandling, and blends driving fiddle tunes, inventive rhythms, and spontaneous musical interplay as they explore traditional music and original compositions.
