A Celebration & Fundraiser for Eric Gaffney

Iron Horse · Thu, Dec 17 · 7pm · $39.60

Stored Up Wonder: A celebration and fundraiser for Eric Gaffney Gimme Indie Rock! Gimme Eric Gaffney!

Northampton’s own Eric Gaffney (co-founder of Sebadoh, Grey Matter, Fields of Gaffney, and Gracefully Aging Hippy Soloists) has been playing Drums and Guitar since 1980, and recording & performing locally and internationally since 1983. His prolific recorded output over the last 40+ years can be found in local record stores as well as his Bandcamp pages, which were called “a treasure trove” by Pitchfork magazine.

Born in Boston and now living locally, Black Francis (co-founder of the Pixies, Frank Black & the Catholics) began playing music and writing songs as a teenager in Westport, MA before meeting future Pixies guitarist Joey Santiago while both were attending UMass-Amherst in the mid-80s. Constantly touring and recording, this is a rare chance to catch him in the intimate environs of the Iron Horse The usual misfit band of Western Mass ruffians. You know them, or at least you’ve seen them around.

Exclusive limited merchandise for sale at the show!

Last May/Spring Eric was diagnosed with Stage 3 colon cancer which had spread locally. In June he had extensive surgery at Mass General to remove all of the cancerous tumor wherever it had spread. Since then he has persevered and endured 4 rounds of chemotherapy, endless tests, brutal side effects, bureaucracy, transportation difficulties, and enough medication to sink a ship.

Anyone who has faced serious illness can tell you that the fear and weight of it all can be overwhelming right when you most need hope, optimism, and support. Eric has heroically faced all of it when all he really wants to do is play gigs, write, draw, and hang with friends. He wants to play a fun show! We want you there!
