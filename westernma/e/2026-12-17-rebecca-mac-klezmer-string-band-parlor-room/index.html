Rebecca Mac Klezmer String Band

Parlor Room · Thu, Dec 17 · 7:30pm · $24

The Rebecca Mac String Band plays intricate and off-beat arrangements of original fiddle tunes, drawing inspiration from the traditional soundscapes of Klezmer and Eastern-European folk music. Having released their debut record, Night Bird, in the spring of 2026, the Rebecca Mac String is one of the most exciting new klezmer groups on the scene today.

Weaver is a songwriter living in Northampton, Massachusetts. With a background as a storyteller, they write lyrically-focused songs. Drawing from Russian and Jewish socialist melodies, and blending them with American folk sensibilities, they are writing new music to express the despair and steadfastness of the labor movement today.
