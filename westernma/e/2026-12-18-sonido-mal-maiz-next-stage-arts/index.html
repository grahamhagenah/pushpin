Sonido Mal Maiz

Next Stage Arts · Fri, Dec 18 · 7:30pm

$20 Advance / $24 At the Door / $10 Livestream Sonido Mal Maïz creates explosive psychedelic Latin music. The expansive creations of Sonido Mal Maiz emanate a passion for fire that ignites the heart and soul of music lovers. The band weaves together the rhythms and sounds of the Americas through spiritual improvisation and poetic storytelling of the myths and legends of Central America. Together we journey through the cosmovision of the past toward a creative collective future.

Described by the Seven Days as “Simmering with steamy dance vibes” Sonido Mal Maiz is the collaboration of Maiz Vargas Sandoval (vocal, bass)

Graham Lambert (guitar, vocals) Colin Henkel (drumset) Mike Hartigan (keys, vocals) who together weave a tapestry of creative expression to uplift the community. “Musica es medicina!” Sonido Mal Maiz invites the listener to shed oppression, worries and the struggles of daily life and to share in a ritual of rejuvenation and ecstasy within the music.
