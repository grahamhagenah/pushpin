Acclaimed jazz guitarist and Grammy Award-winning producer John Pizzarelli, together with celebrated vocalist and Broadway star Jessica Molaskey, present The Glory of Love: Married to Jazz, a brand-new concert celebrating the enduring partnerships of legendary jazz couples whose music—and love—were deeply intertwined.

The husband-and-wife duo, renowned for their irresistible charm, exceptional musicianship, and effortless onstage chemistry, brings fresh interpretations of beloved songs made famous by iconic pairs including Bobby Troup & Julie London, Astrud & João Gilberto, Jackie & Roy, Peggy Lee & Dave Barbour, and more. Through witty banter, heartfelt storytelling, and stunning performances, Pizzarelli and Molaskey celebrate the timeless artistry, romance, and enduring legacy of these extraordinary musical partnerships.

With more than 30 albums to his credit, John Pizzarelli is one of today’s leading jazz guitarists, celebrated for his sophisticated playing, signature swing, and unmistakable vocal style. Jessica Molaskey, whose Broadway credits include Sunday in the Park with George, Parade, and the 2025 Lincoln Center production of Floyd Collins, brings theatrical elegance and a velvety voice that perfectly complements Pizzarelli’s masterful musicianship. mahaiwejazz
