Brian Dickens + Maki Matsui

Parlor Room · Sun, Dec 20 · 1:30pm · $21.60

Brian Dickens is a singer, songwriter and guitarist from Franklin County, Massachusetts, who specializes in high bandwidth, delirium coded, all original folk, rock, and blues. With the help of wandering fingerstyle acoustic guitar and long winded vocal, he crafts songs with lyrics which Sheryl Hunter, for the Greenfield Recorder, recently wrote, "capture the feeling of experiences lived, not just sung about." Whimsical in composition but earnest in delivery, Brian celebrates 20 years of songwriting craft in 2026.

With a name that evokes meadows and pine woods, it seems as if the Tokyo-born singer songwriter Maki Matsui (松居牧) could easily have grown up in the hills of Western Massachusetts. Her songs are deeply rooted in her found home of Conway and the surrounding woods and hill towns. Her sophisticated, ethereal folk tunes surprise melodically and emotionally as they muse and delight in the life of an earthling, making even hard things beautiful with their yearning for sincerity and gentleness.
