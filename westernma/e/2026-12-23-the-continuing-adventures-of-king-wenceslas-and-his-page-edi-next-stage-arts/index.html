The Continuing Adventures of King Wenceslas and His Page Edith

Follow King Wenceslas and his Page, Edith, across the deep and crisp and even snow of Medieval Europe in their comedic search for meaning and goodness. This play delves deep between the stanzas of everyone’s favorite Christmas carol, delighting audiences with quick-paced physical comedy, stage magic, and puppets, all performed by four actors. You’ve seen A Christmas Carol and The Nutcracker, this season why not try a new holiday treat, (Good) King Wenceslas, a winter play for winter people!
