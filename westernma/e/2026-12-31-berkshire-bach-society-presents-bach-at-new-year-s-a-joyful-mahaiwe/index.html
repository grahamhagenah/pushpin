The Berkshire Bach Society presents Bach at New Year’s: A Joyful Noise, an event that has been a holiday tradition since 1993. Nine-time Grammy Award winner and Artistic Director Eugene Drucker, concertmaster, and James Bagwell, BBS Director of Choral Music and Principal Conductor, lead the Berkshire Bach Ensemble and vocal soloists in Orchestral Suites and Arias by Bach, Handel, and others. Join Berkshire Bach for an evening of great music and dazzling performances and start your New Year on a high note. Come for the joy!
