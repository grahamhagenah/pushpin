Valley Jazz Voices Winter Concert

Bombyx · Sat, Jan 23 · 3pm · $10 - $20

Take Route 66 to the moon and back! Travel with Valley Jazz Voices through the Great American Songbook. They got Cole Porter! They got Gershwin! They got Irving Berlin and Johnny Mercer! In 4-part harmony!

In the 50's and 60's, the US State Department "Jazz Ambassadors" program sponsored American jazz musicians touring all over the world. Why? Because jazz is America, with its invention and improvisation, its cooperation and competition, its swagger and its heartache. Jazz is a kind of multi-vocal democracy, and embodies what we claim are American values. That's soft power.
