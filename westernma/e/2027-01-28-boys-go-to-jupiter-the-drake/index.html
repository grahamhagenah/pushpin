$20 advance, $23 day of show General Admission (Standing) - Doors at 7PM Boys Go To Jupiter is most definitely a band. But it feels like something else. A friend you can just talk to for hours. A story that hooks you from the very first word. Or three Brooklyn twenty-somethings who really, really love a great song. A whirlwind of influences and genres, their captivating sound is grounded in strong storytelling and powerful live performance. Blending joyful indie-pop, dreamy soul, lush folk and sugary punk, the Brooklyn-based band delivers. Whether supported by their six-piece band or as a trio, they share a love for songs with heart, humor, and hooks that have audiences dancing, singing, crying, and back again. Their debut album Meet Me After Practice was released in February 2025 and are currently recording their next project.
