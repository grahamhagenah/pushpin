Gibney Company: Another Look

Mass MoCA · Sat, Jan 30 · 7pm · $49–$79

“The troupe’s superpower is clear: its strong and versatile dancers, artists who appear to be physically capable of anything.” —The New York Times, June 2022 Following two successful collaborations, Gibney Company Resident Choreographer Lucinda Childs is creating a full-length work set to Another Look at Harmony, Part 4, a major score by Philip Glass that has never been choreographed at this scale. Premiering at New York City Center in January 2027, the work is conceived in celebration of Philip Glass’ 90th birthday and reunites two defining figures of American minimalism in a rare and timely artistic convergence.

Led by Artistic Director Gina Gibney and Director Gilbert T. Small II, Gibney Company commissions and performs works by renowned and emerging choreographers from New York and around the world who are committed to exploring connections between the rigorous, often superhuman physicality of contemporary dance alongside humanistic, responsive storytelling.

Another Look is presented with the support of Dance Reflections by Van Cleef & Arpels.

This program is supported in part by the Irene Hunter Fund for Dance at MASS MoCA in association with Jacob’s Pillow.
