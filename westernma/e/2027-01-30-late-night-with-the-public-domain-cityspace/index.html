Late Night with the Public Domain

CitySpace · Sat, Jan 30 · 7:30pm

Late Night with the Public Domain, January 29 & 30 Pay it Forward Late Night with the Public Domain; a late night talk show where expired IPs take the stage. Part talent show, part comedy sketch with puppets, singing, dancing, hand constructed wild sets and more.

Ang Buxton mentored Bella Levavi, performing stand up comedy and producing an array of shows, they are back on stage with a big weird show. Ang recently performed at the We Make Noise Festival and is the founder of Far Out Fest. Bella is a puppeteer, she organizes Greenfield Crankie Fest.

Pay It Forward events are made possible by the Community Foundation of Western Mass, Massachusetts Cultural Council, Greenfield Savings Bank, Never Give Up, Greenfield Northampton Cooperative Bank, Tom Schiff Consulting, Tandem Bagel Company and Waugh Agency Insurance.
