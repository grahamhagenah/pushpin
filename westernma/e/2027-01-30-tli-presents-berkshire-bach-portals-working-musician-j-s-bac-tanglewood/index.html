TLI Presents: Berkshire Bach Portals—Working Musician—J.S. Bach Plays the Berkshires

Tanglewood · Sat, Jan 30 · 3pm

Presented in collaboration with Berkshire Bach Society, Working Musician—J.S. Bach Plays the Berkshires, is a short film about the evolving significance of Bach’s music in today's culture, told through the lens of Berkshire Bach and the way its performances come together. The film is by documentarian Anthony Rossello and features interviews with pianist Simone Dinnerstein, BBS Artistic Director Eugene Drucker, composer and music personality Bruce Adolphe, and critic Tim Page on what makes Bach’s music important for modern listeners. It includes telling anecdotes from Bach’s life and follows the preparations for the BBS signature series Bach at New Year’s featuring the six ‘Brandenburg’ Concertos. After the presentation, Eugene Drucker hosts a conversation with the filmmaker, the performers, and the audience.

BBS Portals is a quarterly series that explores creativity and the impact the music of J.S. Bach has had across artistic disciplines.
