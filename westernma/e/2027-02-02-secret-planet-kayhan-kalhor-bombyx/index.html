Secret Planet: Kayhan Kalhor

Bombyx · Tue, Feb 2 · 7pm · $22.50 - $55

Kayhan Kalhor is an internationally acclaimed virtuoso and the world’s leading player of the kamancheh, the Persian spike fiddle. He can hold a room spellbound with the instrument for an hour of unbroken improvisation. Through his many collaborations, he has been instrumental in popularizing Persian music in the West and remains a creative force in today’s global music scene. His performances of traditional Persian music and innovative cross-cultural projects have captivated audiences worldwide.

Born in Tehran with Kurdish ancestry, he began his musical studies at the age of seven under Master Ahmad Mohajer. A child prodigy, he was invited at the age of thirteen to work in the Iranian National Radio and Television Orchestra, where he performed for five years. With the Iranian Revolution under way and a new turmoil engulfing Iran, the seventeen-year-old walked out of the country carrying just a backpack and his kamancheh. He traveled more than 2,500 miles across Turkey, Romania, Yugoslavia and Italy, picking up farmwork until settling in Rome and pursuing a degree in classical music. Across several decades, he has constructed a musical resume that spans collaborations with the New York Philharmonic, Francis Ford Coppola, Toumani Diabaté, Yo-Yo Ma, Arooj Aftab, and more. After multiple nominations, he became the recipient of a Grammy in 2017.

He will be accompanied by the tombak player Behrouz Jamali for this performance at Bombyx. This concert will be fully seated; advance tickets are highly recommended.
