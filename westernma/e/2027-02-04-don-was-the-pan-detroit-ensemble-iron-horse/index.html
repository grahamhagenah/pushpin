Don Was & The Pan Detroit Ensemble

Iron Horse · Thu, Feb 4 · 7pm · $66

Terrapin Station and Groove In The Face Of Adversity Drawing on the wide-ranging influences of his five-decade career, Don Was brings his Pan-Detroit Ensemble to the stage to celebrate the 50th anniversary of the Grateful Dead’s landmark album Terrapin Station, presenting the album in its entirety. Building on Don’s long musical association with Bobby Weir, the Ensemble brings its own musical personality to the Dead’s music, offering a fresh interpretation through its distinctive blend of steamy jazz, juke-joint blues, and loose-limbed funk. The concert will also feature music from the band’s debut album, Groove in the Face of Adversity, which channels the soul of the Motor City through a sound both timeless and deeply personal.

Music has always loomed large in the life of Don Was. Born in Detroit in 1952, he has enjoyed a multifaceted career as a musician, record producer, music director, film composer, documentary filmmaker, and radio host. Since 2011, he has also served as President of America’s venerated jazz label, Blue Note Records.

As a record producer, he has won six Grammy Awards, including Album of the Year in 1989 for Bonnie Raitt’s Nick of Time and Producer of the Year in 1994. Albums he has produced have sold close to 100 million copies for a wide array of artists such as The Rolling Stones, Bob Dylan, Willie Nelson, John Mayer, Ringo Starr, Wayne Shorter, The B-52s, and Charles Lloyd. In 1995, he produced and directed the Brian Wilson documentary I Just Wasn’t Made For These Times, which won the San Francisco Film Festival’s Golden Gate Award. As a film composer, he received the 1994 British Academy Award (BAFTA) for Best Original Score for the film Backbeat. He also won the 2014 Emmy Award for Outstanding Music Direction for CBS’s The Beatles: The Night That Changed America.

In 2018, Don joined Grateful Dead guitarist Bob Weir to form the Wolf Brothers, a band that continued to tour in the U.S. and overseas until Weir’s passing in 2026. In April 2023, he served as music director for Willie Nelson’s 90th birthday celebration at the Hollywood Bowl, and he is featured in the Willie Nelson & Family documentary on Paramount+. In September 2024, he was honored with a Lifetime Achievement Award by the Americana Music Foundation at Nashville’s Ryman Auditorium. He also hosts Dinner With Don Was, a weekly radio show on John Mayer’s SiriusXM Channel 14.
