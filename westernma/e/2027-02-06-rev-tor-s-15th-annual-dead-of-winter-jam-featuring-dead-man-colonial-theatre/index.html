Rev Tor’s 15th Annual Dead Of Winter Jam Featuring Dead Man’s Waltz & Friends

Colonial Theatre · Sat, Feb 6 · 7:30pm

Drawing from the Northeast festival scene, Dead Man’s Waltz brings seasoned musicians together for an adventurous trip through the Grateful Dead’s expansive songbook. Familiar melodies leave room for unexpected turns as the band follows the music wherever it leads. It’s a spirited celebration of songs built to be explored, shared and heard anew.
