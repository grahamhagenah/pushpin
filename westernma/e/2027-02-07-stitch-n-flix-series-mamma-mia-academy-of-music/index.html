Stitch 'n Flix Series: Mamma Mia!

Academy of Music · Sun, Feb 7 · 2pm

Settle in for a cozy afternoon of creativity and cinema at Stitch ‘n Flix!

Bring your friends and your knitting, crocheting, embroidery, or any portable craft project to the theater and enjoy classic films in a relaxed, welcoming environment. Not a crafter? That’s okay! Come enjoy the movie anyway!

With gently dimmed lighting, you can work on your projects while watching beloved movies on the big screen. Each event also features special guests from the local crafting community, showcasing their work and offering pop-up shops full of handmade treasures.

Today’s feature is Mamma Mia! (2008), featuring an all-star cast that includes Meryl Streep, Pierce Brosnan, Julie Walters, Christine Baranski, Colin Firth, Stellan Skarsgard, and Amanda Seyfried, as well as the feel-good music of ABBA. This romantic jukebox musical tells the story of hotelier Donna, who is planning her daughter Sophie’s wedding. Unbeknownst to her, Sophie has invited three men from her mother’s past in order to discover which one is her father. Bring your tissues and your dancing shoes, along with your knitting needles!
