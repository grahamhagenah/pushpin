$15 advanced, $18 day of show General Admission (standing) - Doors open at 7PM Amelia Day is a musical jack-of-all-trades. Standing at the intersection of folk, rock, and pop, Day blends heartfelt storytelling, raw delivery, and unforgettable melodies into music that feels both fresh and familiar — like rediscovering an old favorite. A self-described writer before anything else, her perceptive lyricism extends far beyond her 23 years, with lines reminiscent of songwriting greats like Joni Mitchell and Paul Simon. As the Spokesman-Review notes, Day has a signature style that features “wordplay, double-meanings and rhythmic variations,” with themes ranging from journeying into adulthood to climate change. Many of Day’s songs are also shaped by her intimate relationships, where her queerness and candor take center stage.

"I knew you then, before all of this…but you left me for your ego trip," she sings to her ex-girlfriend on "Ego Trip," the title track from her newest EP. Stacked high with alt-rock guitars, pop hooks, and swooning keyboards, the song is more than a genre-bending kiss-off to a no-good lover; it's also a reminder that true artists remember where they came from, honoring their roots instead of chasing after trends.

Nashville-based singer-songwriter Sarah Gross brings a fresh, alternative perspective to Americana, combining the genre’s rich storytelling traditions with the edge and honesty of the East Coast. Whether writing about the quiet comforts of love or the complexities of identity, acceptance, and belonging, Sarah finds the extraordinary in the contradictions of everyday life.

Her latest record, The Old Side of Being Young, produced by Collin Pastore and Jake Finch (credits include Lucy Dacus, boygenius, and Ashe, among others), explores the strange, often messy transition into adulthood with a clarity and emotional depth that have become hallmarks of her songwriting. The release has earned Spotify editorial support and fueled a 15-city headline tour, building on the momentum of continued audience growth as a result of social media virality and appearances onstage at UBS Arena with Zach Bryan and on The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon.
