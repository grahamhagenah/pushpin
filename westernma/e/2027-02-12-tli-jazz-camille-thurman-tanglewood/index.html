TLI Jazz: Camille Thurman

Tanglewood · Fri, Feb 12 · 7pm

Celebrate Valentine's Day with an unforgettable evening of jazz featuring acclaimed saxophonist, vocalist, composer, and multi-instrumentalist Camille Thurman. A former member of the Jazz at Lincoln Center Orchestra and a two-time ASCAP Herb Alpert Young Jazz Composers Award winner, Thurman has earned acclaim for her virtuosic saxophone playing, expressive vocals, and captivating stage presence. Hailed as one of today's most dynamic voices in jazz, Thurman brings extraordinary artistry, infectious energy, and heartfelt expression to a program that honors the rich tradition of jazz while embracing its vibrant future.

This event will feature cabaret-style table seating and delectable food and beverage options available for pre-order and purchase.
