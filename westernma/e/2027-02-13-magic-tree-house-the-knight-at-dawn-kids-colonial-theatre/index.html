Magic Tree House: The Knight at Dawn KIDS

Colonial Theatre · Sat, Feb 13 · 2pm

Fresh from their adventure with the dinosaurs, Jack and Annie return to the magic tree house and open a book about the Middle Ages. When Annie wishes she could visit the castle on its pages, the siblings are whisked back to the age of knights. Curious about the mysterious Black Knight and his quest, they set off through a castle full of surprises, from a grand medieval feast to secret tunnels and dancing suits of armor. As they search for a way home, Jack and Annie discover the power of hope and what it really means to be brave.
