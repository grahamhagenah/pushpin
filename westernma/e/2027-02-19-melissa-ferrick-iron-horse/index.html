Melissa Ferrick

Iron Horse · Fri, Feb 19 · 7pm · $42

For 30 years, Melissa Ferrick has defied easy categorization, weaving indie, Americana, folk, and rock into a sound that's entirely her own. From opening for Morrissey on an hour's notice in 1991 to an Atlantic Records deal, eighteen albums, her own independent label, and stages shared with Bob Dylan, Ani DiFranco, k.d. lang, and Suzanne Vega, Ferrick has built a career on raw honesty, disarming wit, and songwriting that turns a room of strangers into a shared experience — a live show audiences keep coming back for.
