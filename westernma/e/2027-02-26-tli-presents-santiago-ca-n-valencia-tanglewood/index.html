TLI Presents: Santiago Cañón-Valencia

Tanglewood · Fri, Feb 26 · 7pm

Cellist Santiago Cañón-Valencia has emerged as one of the most exciting artists of his generation. Named a BBC New Generation Artist and Silver Medalist and Audience Favorite at the International Tchaikovsky Competition, he has also earned top prizes at the Queen Elisabeth, Carlos Prieto, and Beijing International competitions. Praised for his extraordinary virtuosity and expressive depth, Cañón-Valencia brings a fresh, compelling voice to beloved masterworks and contemporary repertoire.
